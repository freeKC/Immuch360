## Ol foto mo video 360° olsem wan sfia

Yu sevem kopi blong ol foto blong yu long wan [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich) seva, mo sam long olgeta oli kam long wan 360° kamera o long foto sfia mod blong wan fon. Long ofisol mobael app, ol foto ia oli soem olsem wan flat longfala pikja, mo ol 360° video oli plei flat tu. Immich web app i save soem wan 360° foto olsem wan sfia, be mobael app i no save: ol man oli stap askem samting ia stat long Janueri 2024 long [diskasen #6572](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/6572).

Immuch360 i openem olgeta olsem wan sfia we yu lukluk raon insaed long hem, long ol Android mo iOS fon mo tablet. Wan foto i tanem taem yu pulum, i kam bigwan taem yu pinsim o yu tajem tu taem, i gohed tanem smol taem yu pulum kwik, i stat long fasfala vyu we kamera i rekodem (GPano metadata), mo i kam moa klia taem yu zum; app i save handelem ol panorama we oli no fulwan (GPano krop). Wan video i plei long wan sfia pleia insaed long app, wetem saon, pul mo gyroscope. Ol 360° fael we oli joinem finis oli wok long evri ples: ol fael we Insta360 app o Studio i eksportem, GoPro Player, Ricoh Theta, mo ol foto sfia blong fon. Ol raw fael we oli kam stret long kamera, app i joinem olgeta, luk [Ol raw fael blong 360° kamera](#raw-360-camera-files-without-the-cameras-app).

### Openem wan 360° foto olsem wan sfia

1. Openem foto long taemlaen, long wan album, long [lis blong 360°](#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list) o long wan sea folda. Ol 360° foto oli gat wan 360° saen long smol pikja blong olgeta.
2. Tajem 360° long ba antap long vyua. Sfia vyua i open, wetem wan saen blong lodem taem ful pikja i stap lod; zum we yu bin gat long flat vyu i stap semak.
3. Pulum blong lukluk raon, pinsim blong zum, tajem tu taem blong zum i go insaed. Afta wan kwik pul, vyu i gohed tanem smol mo i go slo.
4. Blong lukluk raon taem yu tanem fon, tajem gyroscope baten antap long raet (i of fastaem).
5. Tu narafala baten antap long raet oli 360°/180° (ful sfia o VR180 haf sfia, i no soem long ol panorama we oli no fulwan) mo 3D (leaot blong wan stereo foto), luk [3D mo VR180](#3d-and-vr180-photos-and-videos). Wan raw Insta360 foto i gat gyroscope nomo.
6. Klosem wetem kros antap long lef.

### Plei wan 360° video

1. Openem video mo tajem 360° long ba antap. Long wan 360° video, 360° baten i kam fastaem; long ol fon mo tablet, Spatial baten i kam afta taem seting ia i on (luk [Spatial 2.5D](#depth-on-a-flat-screen-spatial-25d-experimental)).
2. 360° pleia insaed long app i open long ful skrin mo i plei wetem saon: Media3 long Android, SceneKit long iOS.
3. Pulum blong lukluk raon, o muvum fon. Android pleia i folem fon oltaem; iOS pleia i gat wan swij blong gyroscope, we i on fastaem.
4. Tajem pikja blong luk ol kontrol: klosem, 360°/180° mo 3D antap, afta plei, ol baten blong jam mo ba blong taem long Android. iOS pleia i gat plei mo stop smol nomo naoia.
5. Taem video i gat tu odio trak o moa (ol lanwis, toktok), wan "Odio trak" baten i letem yu jusum wan. Taem video i stap lod o i stop, pleia i soem "I stap lodem" wetem hamas bafa blong hem i fulap.

Pleia i plei fael we i stap long fon, o fael blong wan netwok sea, sapos i gat. Sapos i no gat, hem i stream long seva blong yu: transcoded stream fastaem, o orijinal sapos yu askem long Settings (Ol seting), Asset Viewer (Lukluk long media), "Sos blong video" (stat long build 15; bifo i gat swij "Force original video" (Fosem orijinal video)), luk [Ol tekniko saed blong video mo ol decoder](#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

### Wan 360° fael we i soem flat: Lukum olsem 360°

Sam 360° fael oli no gat wan projeksen tag, ale seva i no makem olgeta olsem 360° mo oli soem flat.

1. Openem foto o video mo tajem ⋮ antap long raet.
2. Tajem "Lukum olsem 360°", we i stap bitwin Slideshow (Soem ol foto wanwan) mo Download (Daonlodem). 360° baten i kamaot long ba antap.
3. Blong tekemaot, sem samting ia i ridim "Stop blong tritim olsem 360°" naoia.

Fon i tingbaot samting we yu jusum, mo i no jenisim wan samting long seva. Long wan fael blong netwok sea, hem i wok blong taem ia nomo we yu lukluk.

### Ol samting we i no wok yet

- **iOS 360° video pleia**: plei mo stop smol, i no gat ba blong taem yet.
- **Bifo mo nekis**: ol 360° pleia long fon oli no save go long media bifo o nekis yet; immersive vyu blong Quest i save.
- **Ol bigfala panorama**: wan foto i soem long 8192x4096 nomo, hem i bigfala tekstia we plante GPU blong fon oli save tekem, ale wan panorama we i bigwan moa i kam smol long saes ia.
- **Dual fisheye .dng**: i stil soem flat.

## Wetaot seva o akaon

Yu no gat wan Immich seva, o yu no wantem wan akaon: yu wantem nomo se ol 360° foto long fon blong yu oli open olsem wan sfia we yu save tanem wetem gyroscope. Immich app i askem yu blong login fastaem.

Long pej blong login, "Yusum wetaot wan server" i openem Immuch360 long ol foto mo video blong divaes hem wan, wetem ol vyua blong 360°, 3D, VR180 mo Spatial, lis blong 360° mo ol netwok sea, i no nidim wan Immich akaon. Ol samting blong seva oli stap haed o oli no wok kasem taem yu konek long wan seva; i no gat wan samting we i lego divaes. Long wan Meta Quest 3, hem i openem ol foto mo video blong hedset hem wan.

<img src=".github/readme/b19-library-no-server.png" width="260" alt="Laebri tab wetaot seva: 360° antap, afta On this device wetem tu 360° foto, mo Ol netwok sea wetem wan sea we nem blong hem NAS">

### Stat wetaot seva

1. Openem app. Long pej blong login, aninit long adres blong seva mo Settings, tajem "Yusum wetaot wan server".
2. Letem akses long ol foto mo video blong yu. Sapos yu talem no, app i talem "App ia i no save luk ol foto blong divaes ia", wetem "Letem akses" mo "Openem ol seting".
3. Photos (Ol foto) tab i soem taemlaen blong divaes. Search (Lukaotem) mo Albums (Ol album) oli stap gri, from we oli kam long seva. Laebri tab i gat 360° antap, afta On this device (Long divaes ia: ol album blong divaes) mo Ol netwok sea.
4. Menu antap long raet i talem "Divaes ia nomo": app i soem ol foto mo video blong divaes ia, i no sendem wan samting i go long eni ples.

### Konek long wan seva afta

1. Openem Settings (menu antap long raet, afta Settings). Fas samting i "Konek long wan server", "Sync wetem wan Immich server blong sevem kopi, lukaotem mo serem". Menu antap long raet i gat sem samting tu.
2. Login olsem yu stap mekem oltaem. Taemlaen, ol album, sevem kopi mo ol narafala samting blong seva oli kamaot.

### Ol samting we i no wok yet

- **Galeri blong divaes hem wan**: app i soem ol foto mo video blong galeri blong divaes. Long wan iPhone, taem app i lukaotem ol 360° fael, hem i no lukluk ol fael we oli stap long iCloud nomo kasem taem oli daonlod long divaes.
- **Wetaot seva**, lis blong 360° i gat ol samting we skan blong divaes i faenem, mo ol fael we yu jusum blong lukum olsem 360°, luk [Lis blong 360°](#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list).

## Ol netwok sea: wan NAS, wan kompiuta o wan seva blong media

Ol 360° video blong yu oli stap long wan NAS o wan kompiuta, mo yu wantem lukluk olgeta long fon o long hedset be yu no wantem kopiem olgeta fastaem. Wetem hedset, ol man oli kopiem evri fael wanwan long wan kebol; ol seva blong media olsem Plex mo Jellyfin oli plei ol 360° video flat, olsem ol man oli raetem long ol forum blong olgeta; Immich app i ridim Immich seva blong yu nomo.

Immuch360 i save lukluk mo plei ol foto mo video blong eni seva we i toktok SMB (Samba, Windows), WebDAV o, stat long build 19, DLNA/UPnP (wan seva blong media: Plex, Jellyfin, minidlna, Gerbera, Emby, wan NAS o wan TV bokis), stret long sea. Hem i faenem ol seva long netwok blong yu hem wan, mo i plei ol fael stret long sem ol vyua olsem long ful app (360°, 3D, VR180, Spatial 2.5D, immersive vyu blong Quest), wetem wan Immich seva o wetaot, long ol fon mo long Meta Quest 3. I no daonlodem wan samting. Taem wan seva i konek, yu save sendem ol fael we yu jusum i go long Immich akaon blong yu (stat long build 15).

### Ademap wan sea

1. Openem Laebri tab, afta Ol netwok sea. Fas taem, pej i talem "I no gat sea yet" wetem wan baten "Ademap wan sea"; + antap long raet i mekem sem samting long eni taem.
2. Pej "Ademap wan sea" i lukaotem ol seva long netwok blong yu fastaem mo i putum olgeta aninit long "I faenem long netwok", wetem kaen blong olgeta (SMB, WebDAV, DLNA, Fon). Lukaot ia i tekem kolosap sikis sekon; "Skanem bakegen" i statem bakegen. Hem i yusum Bonjour/mDNS mo wan skan blong lokol netwok we wan tru SMB o WebDAV toktok i jekem, mo stat long build 19, wan SSDP lukaot blong ol DLNA seva blong media.
3. Tajem wan seva: kaen, seva, pot mo rod oli fulap.
4. I no stap long lis? Fulumap fom wetem han. Kaen: "SMB (Samba, sea blong Windows)", "WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology mo ol narawan)" o "Seva blong media DLNA (Plex, Jellyfin, NAS, TV bokis)". Afta Nem, "Nem o adres blong seva" (wan nem o wan adres; wan ful adres olsem `smb://nas/photos`, `\\nas\photos` o `https://nas:5006/photos` i fulumap ol narafala ples), "Pot (sapos yu wantem)" taem i no stret pot we oli yusum oltaem, "Sea" blong SMB o "Rod blong adres blong WebDAV" blong WebDAV, "Fasfala folda (sapos yu wantem)", "Nem blong yusa" mo "Pasword", mo "Sef konekson (HTTPS)" blong WebDAV.
5. SMB: taem yu raetem seva mo nem blong yusa finis, "Jusum wan sea" i soem ol sea blong seva.
6. DLNA: wan seva blong media i no gat nem blong yusa o pasword. Givim seva, pot mo "Rod blong diskripsen" blong diskripsen blong divaes (`/rootDesc.xml` blong minidlna), o pestem ful adres, olsem `http://192.168.1.10:8200/rootDesc.xml`, long ples blong seva.
7. Tajem "Testem konekson". Hem i ansa "I konek finis, N samting long fasfala folda", o i talem from wanem i no wok. Afta tajem Seftem, long botom blong fom.

Wan nem blong yusa wetem wan pasword we i emti, app i sendem olsem: wan Freebox Server i wantem `freebox` mo no pasword blong ol disk blong hem. Blong jenisim o tekemaot wan sea afta, yusum pensel klosap long hem long pej Ol netwok sea.

### Lukluk mo plei

1. Tajem wan sea. Ol folda oli kam fastaem, afta ol foto mo video olsem plante smol pikja (wan pikja blong evri video tu, we divaes i kipim). Olgeta we app i luksave olsem 360° oli gat 360° saen, mo stat long build 19, ol Apple spatial foto mo video oli gat wan 3D saen. Pulum i go daon blong rifresem.
2. Tajem wan foto: hem i open long ful skrin (pinsim, tajem tu taem), mo 360° baten blong hem i openem sfia vyua.

## Ol tekniko saed blong video, ol decoder mo from wanem wan video i stap stop stop

Ol man oli askem wanem codec, saes mo bit rate Quest 3 i save plei, mo from wanem wan 5.7K eksport i stap stop stop long hedset be i plei gud long fon. Ansa hem i hardware decoder: H.264 decoder blong Quest 3 (XR2 Gen 2) i stop kolosap long 4096x2304, ale wan 5760x2880 H.264 video (levol 6.0, kolosap 200 Mbit/s, nomol Insta360 eksport) i ridim kolosap 17 fps long hedset, wetem ol skwea long pikja, be sem fael i plei gud long wan fon. Sem video long HEVC (H.265) i plei gud long hedset: wan Insta360 X4 8K HEVC video (7680x3840, 29.97 fps, 210 Mbit/s, Main profael levol 6.1, 8 bit) i plei gud long immersive vyu, long stret saes blong hem mo wetaot transcode (wan yusa long wan Quest 3 i talem).

Immich app i gat wan "Force original video" swij nomo mo i soem codec nomo. Immuch360 i soem wanem video ia i olsem mo wanem divaes i save ridim, mo i jusum fael we bambae i plei.

<img src=".github/readme/b19-video-details.png" width="260" alt="Ol tekniko saed blong wan 360° video: Codec H.264 High (avc1.640033), 3840 x 1920, 30 fps; Bit rate 16 Mbit/s; Pikja 8 bit, SDR, BT.709; Divaes ia i save ridim: Yes, Hardware, kasem 4096 x 2304">

### Ridim ol tekniko saed blong wan video

1. Openem video mo swaep i go antap: ol tekniko saed oli kamaot aninit long pikja.
2. Codec: codec wetem profael mo codec string blong hem, saes blong pikja mo hamas pikja long wan sekon, olsem "H.264 High (avc1.640033) • 3840 x 1920 • 30 fps", olsem long pikja antap.
3. Bit rate (stat long build 18): bit rate blong video trak, o hemia we fael i talem; sapos no, app i makem "ful fael, wetem saon" o "oli tingting long saes blong fael".
4. Pikja (stat long build 18): bit dip, SDR o HDR transfer (HLG, PQ, Dolby Vision) mo kala spes.
5. Divaes ia i save ridim: yes o no, hardware o software, bigfala saes we decoder i save tekem, mo "I no gat decoder blong (profael)" taem divaes i gat codec be i no gat profael ia.

Wan laen i soem nomo taem app i ridim fael finis, mo blong wanem fael i talem nomo. Wan Apple spatial foto o video i kasem wan laen blong hem, luk [Ol Apple spatial foto mo video](#apple-spatial-photos-and-videos).

### Luk wanem divaes i save ridim

Settings, Advanced, "Ol video decoder blong divaes ia" (stat long build 15) i soem wanem fon o hedset i save ridim: codec, hardware o software, bigfala saes, hamas pikja long wan sekon, mo stat long build 18 ol profael (Main, Main 10, HDR10). Stat long build 19, hem i gat wan MV-HEVC samting, codec blong ol Apple spatial video. Kopi baten i putum lis long clipboard blong wan ripot blong problem.

### Jusum orijinal o transcoded stream

1. Openem Settings, Asset Viewer, afta Videos.
2. Aninit long "Sos blong video" ("Wanem fael i plei taem server i gat wan transcoded kopi"), jusum "Orijinal taem divaes ia i save ridim", "Oltaem orijinal" o "Oltaem transcoded stream".

Stat long build 15, samting we yu jusum i wok blong evri video blong seva: flat pleia, 360° mo Spatial pleia, mo immersive vyu blong Quest. Kasem taem yu jusum, wan fon i kipim wanem olfala swij "Force original video" i talem (i of fastaem: transcoded stream, we i orijinal hem wan taem seva i no transcodem), mo Quest i plei orijinal taem hedset i save ridim. Jek i ridim codec, saes mo hamas pikja long wan sekon long fael mo i lukluk olgeta wetem ol hardware decoder (H.264 long Quest 3 i stop long 4096x2304 we oli mesarem). Wan pleia we i no save ridim orijinal i jenis i go long transcoded stream wetem wan mesej: "I plei transcoded stream: orijinal (codec mo saes) i bitim wanem divaes ia i save ridim".

Long hedset, immersive vyu i statem orijinal mo, long ol fas pikja, i jenis i go long transcoded stream blong seva taem orijinal i bitim ol decoder, mo i talem long info panel; taem i no gat transcoded stream, taem hem tu i bigwan tumas, o taem fael i kam long hedset o long wan netwok sea, info panel i talem blong 10 sekon, wetem wanem blong jenisim. Build 14, hemia we oli sendem long Horizon Store, i jekem H.264 we i bitim 4096x2304 nomo, afta i traem plei stream blong seva long sem fasin.

Blong ol raw video wetem tu lens, jek i askem tu decoder long sem taem, mo stat long build 19, i no letem tu stream we evriwan i bitim 2048x2048 i go long wan software decoder, luk [Ol raw fael blong 360° kamera](#raw-360-camera-files-without-the-cameras-app).

### Givim long hedset wan video we hem i save ridim

Long Immich, go long Administration (Manejmen), Settings, Video Transcoding Settings (Ol seting blong jenisim video), mo jusum wan long tufala ia:

- **Evri H.264 video i kam HEVC**: putum Video codec long HEVC mo Target resolution (Saes we yu wantem) long Original (Orijinal) (720p we i stap fastaem bambae i mekem wan 360° video i kam smol long 1440x720), mo kipim HEVC nomo long Accepted video codecs (Ol video codec we oli oraet). Evri H.264 video blong laebri i transcode, i no ol 360° video nomo, mo hedset i kasem HEVC long ful saes.
- **Olgeta we oli bigwan moa long 1440p nomo**: kipim H.264 long Accepted video codecs, putum Transcode policy (Rul blong transcode) long "Videos higher than target resolution or not in an accepted format" (ol video we oli bigwan moa long saes we yu wantem, o we fomat blong olgeta i no oraet) mo Target resolution long 1440p, i gud moa wetem Video codec long HEVC. Evri samting we i bigwan moa i transcode mo hedset i kasem 2880x1440. Ol nomol 4K video oli transcode i go long 1440p tu.

Ol seting ia oli wok long ful seva, blong evri yusa mo evri app, mo blong transcodem wan bigfala laebri i tekem plante aoa blong CPU mo moa spes long disk; ol orijinal oli no jenis. Ol braosa we oli no save HEVC (Firefox long plante sistem, Chrome wetaot hardware HEVC) bambae oli no plei wan HEVC transcode long Immich web app. Moa isi fasin blong ol niu video hem i blong eksportem long H.265 long Insta360 app o Studio. Afta yu jenisim ol seting ia, ol video we oli stap finis oli mas transcode bakegen: Administration, Job Queues (Ol laen blong wok) (Jobs (Ol wok) long ol olfala vesen blong Immich), Transcode videos (Transcodem ol video), All.

Narafala rod, mo hemia nomo blong ol fael blong wan netwok sea o blong hedset (oli no gat transcoded stream), hem i blong transcodem eksport long HEVC bifo yu aplodem o yu kopiem i go long sea:

```bash
ffmpeg -i VID_360.mp4 -c:v libx265 -crf 20 -preset medium -tag:v hvc1 -c:a copy -movflags +faststart VID_360_hevc.mp4
exiftool -overwrite_original -tagsFromFile VID_360.mp4 -XMP-GSpherical:all VID_360_hevc.mp4
exiftool -ProjectionType VID_360_hevc.mp4        # must print: equirectangular
```

`-tag:v hvc1` i makem HEVC trak long fasin we ol Apple divaes mo ol braosa oli wantem, `-c:a copy` i kipim saon olsem we i stap. ffmpeg i tekemaot 360° tag blong eksport, exiftool laen i kopiem i kambak; wetaot laen ia, Immich i soem video olsem wan flat video.

### Ol orijinal we oli no save stream

Taem seva i no lukluk ol HTTP Range rikwes long orijinal mo MP4 indeks (moov) i stap long en blong fael, ful fael i mas daonlod fastaem bifo fas pikja, ale app i go bak long plei stream blong seva. Wan reverse proxy long fored blong Immich we i holem ol ansa o i tekemaot ol Range heda i save mekem samting ia.

## Buildim yu wan

Rod blong build i sem olsem Immich mobael (Flutter 3.47, we [mise](https://mise.jdx.dev) i lukaotem):

```bash
git clone https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360.git
cd Immuch360/mobile
mise install
mise run install
mise run codegen
flutter build apk --release --flavor phone                                   # phones: the universal APK of the GitHub releases (add --split-per-abi for the per ABI files)
flutter build appbundle --release --flavor phone                             # the App Bundle sent to Google Play (also mise run build:android)
flutter build apk --release --flavor quest --target-platform android-arm64 --android-project-arg arm64only=true   # Meta Quest 3: the -quest-release.apk of the releases (also mise run build:quest)
flutter build ios --release                                                  # iPhone and iPad, on a Mac with Xcode and your own signing team
```

Oli tekem ol pikja blong stoa long ol debug simulator build we oli mekem wetem `--dart-define=IMMUCH_SCREENSHOTS=true`, we i haedem debug banner nomo.
