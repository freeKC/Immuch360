## Where to get it

The app is on Google Play for phones and tablets; the App Store version is waiting for Apple's review, the app is on the Meta Horizon Store and its first update is in Meta's review, and the Google Play version for TVs is waiting for Google's review of the TV release. Immuch360 Desktop, for Windows, is a preview on GitHub. The GitHub release is always the newest build:

The App Store link will be added here as soon as the listing is published; the app is already on the [Meta Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/). Log in with your usual Immich server URL and account, or tap "Use without a server" on the login page to start on the device's own photos and videos. The APK from GitHub does not update itself: watch the Releases page, and once you have installed the app from a store, take the updates from that store.

The store build and the GitHub APK are signed with different keys: to switch from one to the other, uninstall the app first (its settings and saved shares go with it). How to install each one is in [Platform support](platform-support.md).

## The builds

- **Phones, tablets, headsets and TVs**: build 23, version 3.3.0-rc.0, build number 3030021, is the GitHub release [v3.3.0-rc.0-23](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-23) of 10 October 2026: the universal APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-23-release.apk`), the APKs for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64, the Quest APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-23-quest-release.apk`) and the App Bundle (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-23.aab`) that goes to Google Play.
- **Its base**: Immich `main` of 10 October 2026 (version 3.3.0-rc.0, not a stable release yet). It was tested against an Immich 3.2.4 server and a server built from that same Immich `main`, see [Everything else is Immich](user-guide.md#everything-else-is-immich).
- **Windows**: desktop build 4 is the GitHub pre-release [desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-4](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-4) of 10 October 2026: `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.4-windows-x64.zip`, about 58 MB, with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. It was built from commit 99113354a, with the sources of the phone build 22 and the project's own libmpv: the release [libmpv-windows-20261009-3](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/libmpv-windows-20261009-3) of 9 October 2026, built by `.github/workflows/immuch360-libmpv.yml` from pinned sources, which are published with it. The earlier desktop builds carried media-kit's 2024 libmpv; their tags are listed in [Branches](build.md#branches).
- **In the stores**: Google Play has build 20 (phones and tablets), the Meta Horizon Store build 14, and the App Store nothing yet; build 23, build 21 and version 3.3.0 are in their reviews.

## Roadmap

What is not done yet, the most likely first. Nothing here is a promise, and feedback on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) helps decide what comes first.

- **Immuch360 Desktop, Windows first**: desktop build 4 is out, with the 360° video player of desktop build 3 (360°, 3D, VR180 and raw 360° videos, the two lenses of raw files, the size of the view measured on each computer) and the Tapo live view, and, from desktop build 4, the project's own libmpv in place of media-kit's 2024 one; the desktop sources are in the main branch of the fork, `immuch360`, since 9 October 2026 (see [On a Windows computer](user-guide.md#on-a-windows-computer-immuch360-desktop-preview)). In the main branch for desktop build 5: the 360° photos and videos opened with the program ("Open with", the command line), which desktop build 4 shows flat, open in their 360° view, and files and folders can be dropped on the window. Next, the test of each function by hand on a Windows PC with real files, both screens and a real camera, with its fixes; then Spatial 2.5D with the webcam; then Linux and macOS, which the project's CI already builds, started and shipped with their video player; then an installer, a signed build and automatic updates.
- **Google Play**: build 20 has been live since 7 October 2026, in place of build 18, and build 23 (3030021), sent on 10 October 2026 with the Immich changes of October and the TV fixes of builds 22 and 23, is in Google's review; the listing for TVs has been under Google's review since 9 October 2026. Build 21 changes nothing on phones and tablets.
- **App Store**: version 3.3.0 is waiting for Apple's review; it carries the features of build 11, so the upload to Immich and the video decoder check (build 15) and the raw Insta360 files (build 16) come with the next App Store update. The link will be added here when it is live.
- **Meta Horizon Store**: Meta approved the listing on 7 October 2026 with build 14, and the app has been public on the [Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/) since that day. Build 21 is submitted as its first update: it brings everything since build 14 (uploads from a share to Immich, the video source chosen from what the headset decodes, raw Insta360, GoPro and DJI files, DLNA, the phone share, Apple spatial photos, Plex Media Server libraries, Tapo cameras), and the store lists it for the Quest 2, Quest Pro, Quest 3 and 3S. To install the store version, a sideloaded copy has to be uninstalled first (see [Install](platform-support.md#install-on-the-headset)).
- **Store listings**: the Google Play listing was rewritten in October 2026 with new screenshots, and its TV screenshots and TV banner were added on 8 October 2026; the listing for TVs has been under Google's review since 9 October 2026. The App Store text still describes the first builds (360° photos and videos, raw files shown flat); it will present the 3D, VR180 and Spatial viewers, the mode without a server, network shares, the media player and the raw Insta360 files. The Meta Horizon Store text already presents the media player.
- **Raw 360° camera files, next**: a progress indicator while a raw photo is prepared for the headset; levelling of GoPro and DJI videos from their own motion data; dual fisheye .dng; device reports on the two lens playback of build 18 (X4, X5, X6, GoPro MAX 2, Osmo 360) to confirm seams and decoder budgets.
- **DLNA, phone share and Apple spatial, next**: the device reports of build 19 (Plex, Jellyfin, a NAS and the Freebox Server over DLNA; a phone serving a Quest, on its hotspot too; real iPhone spatial photos and videos in the headset); the multicast entitlement asked from Apple, so that iPhones find every DLNA server; previous and next between spatial photos in the headset; a spatial badge on server photos in the timeline; spatial videos in 3D on the Quest, if its decoders allow it.
- **360° players on phones, next**: a time bar in the iOS 360° video player (the Android one has it), previous/next in the 360° players of phones as in the Quest immersive view, and photos in the native 360° video player.
- **Network shares, next steps**: swiping from one file of a folder to the next in the photo and video pages (the Quest immersive view already goes through the 360° files of a folder), Digest authentication for WebDAV, the user name from the Bonjour record.
- **Flat videos**: the audio track choice in the flat player of phones, for server, device and share videos alike (the 360° and Spatial players have it, and so does the flat player of Immuch360 Desktop).
- **Android TV, next**: the device test on a real TV (builds 21 and 22 were tried on the Google TV emulator, without the Plex and Tapo pages), and the TV release on Google Play once Google has reviewed the listing for TVs; later, channels on the TV home screen.
- **Tapo cameras, next**: the device test of build 20 with real cameras; the live view on iPhone and iPad; H.265 recordings; playing a clip while it is fetched; a whole day of recordings on one time line.
- **Plex, next**: the device test of build 20 (phones, the Quest, an iPhone, a TV, away from home); bringing the token from the computer with a QR code; hiding the DLNA side of a Plex server in the list of found servers; IPv6.
- **Upstream**: small pull requests to Immich for the parts the maintainers want, starting with the 360° photo viewer.
