# Release status

The builds of Immuch360, what each store has on 20 October 2026, and what comes next.

Back to the [README](../../README.md)

## Where to get it

The app is on Google Play for phones and tablets and on the Meta Horizon Store; the App Store version is waiting for Apple's review, and the Google Play version for TVs is waiting for Google's review of the TV release. Immuch360 Desktop is a preview on GitHub: for Windows, and as early previews for Linux and macOS. The GitHub release is always the newest build:

Log in with your usual Immich server URL and account, or choose "Use without a server" to start on the device's own photos and videos: on the welcome page from build 24, on the login page up to build 23 (the Horizon Store version until Meta approves version 3). The APK from GitHub does not update itself: watch the Releases page, and once you have installed the app from a store, take the updates from that store.

The store build and the GitHub APK are signed with different keys: to switch from one to the other, uninstall the app first (its settings and saved shares go with it). How to install each one is in [Platform support](platform-support.md).

## The builds

- **Phones, tablets, headsets and TVs**: build 26, version 3.3.0-rc.0, build number 3030024, is the GitHub release [v3.3.0-rc.0-26](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-26) of 20 October 2026: the universal APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-26-release.apk`), the APKs for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64, the Quest APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-26-quest-release.apk`) and the App Bundle (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-26.aab`) that goes to Google Play.
- **New in build 26**:
  - **The search filters with a TV remote.** In the People filter, Right from a person reaches an action rail where Apply waits, and Up from there reaches Clear; the radio buttons of the other filter sheets reach Clear and Apply too. The Media Type and Display options sheets fit the screen of a TV. The album sort and the search type open in a dialog that keeps the focus, marks the current choice, and applies a choice with OK (Back changes nothing); phones keep their menus. This work started from mlc70's pull request [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#1](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/1), whose two commits are kept with their authorship. Checked on the Google TV emulator and by widget tests, not on a real TV. Two TV problems remain, see [Known issues](known-issues.md#android-tv-and-google-tv): the Location filter sheet keeps the focus in its Country field (Back closes it), and the quick date sheet shows two choices at a time and scrolls.
  - **16 KB memory pages.** The Rust native core is now linked for 16 KB pages, the release workflow builds with NDK 28, and a check of each APK and of the App Bundle (`mobile/scripts/check_16kb.sh`) stops a release whose 64 bit libraries are not ready for them. Google Play requires this from apps that target Android 15 or later. Four 32 bit libraries that come prebuilt with dependencies stay aligned on 4 KB, which the rule does not cover.
  - **Meta Quest**: closing the app no longer logs "Unable to start service" in the headset's log. The Quest build does not carry the phone share service, and the app no longer tries to stop it there.
- **Builds 24 and 25**: build 24 (13 October 2026) opened the app on a welcome page, "Use without a server" first, and listed the DJI Osmo 360 files of a share with the 360° badge; it kept the Android version code of build 23, which the stores refuse. Build 25 (14 October 2026) is the same code with its own version code, 3030023: from build 25 the release workflow sets it from the tag, 3029998 plus the build number. Build 25 is the one on Google Play and in Meta's review; build 24 is the one in Apple's review.
- **Its base**: Immich `main` of 10 October 2026 (version 3.3.0-rc.0, not a stable release). It was tested against an Immich 3.2.4 server and a server built from that same Immich `main`, see [Everything else is Immich](user-guide.md#everything-else-is-immich).
- **Immuch360 Desktop**: desktop build 6 is the GitHub pre-release [desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-6](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-6) of 20 October 2026, made by the project's CI (`.github/workflows/immuch360-desktop-release.yml`) from the sources of build 26. Its files are named `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.6-<system>`: the Windows installer (`-windows-x64-setup.exe`, made with Inno Setup) and ZIP (`-windows-x64.zip`), the Linux packages (`-linux-amd64.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu, `-linux-x86_64.rpm` for Fedora) and the macOS app (`-macos.dmg` and `-macos.zip`), with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. What each one does is in [Platform support](platform-support.md#macos-and-linux).
- **New in desktop build 6**:
  - **Linux plays flat videos** through the libmpv of the system (libmpv2, libmpv1 on Ubuntu 22.04, mpv-libs on Fedora), which the .deb and the .rpm now depend on, with xdg-user-dirs, with video thumbnails; checked with the videos of a folder. The server, the shares, Plex and the Tapo recordings and live view use the same player; they are in the sources and built by the CI, not checked on Linux yet. 360°, 3D, VR180 and raw dual lens videos play flat with the message "This computer cannot show the 360° view of this video: it plays flat.", since the 360° video renderer is Windows only. The SMB library now needs glibc 2.25 at most, so SMB shares can open on Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 too (its Samba tests pass; not checked with a real share on Linux yet). A bundle copied by hand onto a system without libmpv says which package to install. On Fedora, camera videos (H.264 Main and High) need the FFmpeg of RPM Fusion.
  - **macOS plays flat videos** through the project's own libmpv, built under the LGPL and published with its sources (release [libmpv-macos-20261020-9](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/libmpv-macos-20261020-9)); 360°, 3D and raw videos play flat with the same message. Secrets go to the login keychain. The app declares its use of the local network, the Bonjour services it looks for, and why it may open the protected folders. Files opened from the Finder reach the app that runs.
  - **The three systems**: a file opened while the viewer shows another one now opens the new file, and the 73 texts of the computer version are translated in the 88 other languages of the app.
  - **Windows** is unchanged otherwise: still unsigned, with no update check, and the test of each function by hand is still to do.
- **Checked for desktop build 6**: the CI installs each Linux package in clean containers of Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, Debian 12, Fedora 40 and Fedora 44, and plays a video in each; a .deb made by the CI ran under WSLg on the test PC (a folder with the thumbnails of its videos, a 20 second video played, a 360° video and a raw .insv file played flat with their message). On the Mac of the CI, each run starts the app with a self check, which passed every step on the last runs before the release: a folder and a video given on the command line, a photo opened through Launch Services as the Finder does, a video handed to the app that runs, the keychain written and read back, and 5 seconds of video. Neither system has run the app on a real computer yet.
- **Desktop build 5** (13 October 2026, [desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-5](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-5)): the Windows installer next to the ZIP, files opened with the program and dropped on the window, the welcome page, DJI Osmo 360 files in two players, and the first Linux and macOS packages. Checked on 13 October 2026 with builds of the CI: the installer on the test PC (installed for the current user, the same files as the ZIP, "Open with" for 19 file types, no default program changed, uninstalled cleanly); the .deb and the .rpm in clean containers of the five distributions above, and the .deb started under WSLg on the test PC; the macOS app on the Mac of the CI only ("Open with" in Launch Services, a start).
- **The video library of each system**: on Windows, the project's own libmpv since desktop build 4, release [libmpv-windows-20261009-3](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/libmpv-windows-20261009-3) of 9 October 2026, built by `.github/workflows/immuch360-libmpv.yml` from pinned sources, which are published with it; on macOS, the release libmpv-macos-20261020-9 of the same workflow (mpv 0.41.0, FFmpeg 8.0), since desktop build 6; on Linux, the libmpv of the distribution, since desktop build 6. Desktop builds 1 to 3 carried media-kit's 2024 libmpv on Windows; their tags are listed in [Branches](build.md#branches).
- **In the stores**: Google Play has build 25 (phones and tablets), the Meta Horizon Store build 23, and the App Store nothing yet; build 26 goes to Google Play next, build 25 is in Meta's review, version 3.3.1 (build 24) in Apple's, and the TV listing in Google's.

## Roadmap

What is not done yet, the most likely first. Nothing here is a promise, and feedback on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) helps decide what comes first.

- **Immuch360 Desktop**: desktop build 6 is out for the three systems. On Windows, next: the test of each function by hand on a Windows PC with real files, both screens and a real camera, with its fixes; then Spatial 2.5D with the webcam; then a signed build and an update check. On Linux: a run on a real Linux PC (a live USB session), videos drawn by the graphics chip on X11 sessions, a start that does not wait when no secret service answers, then the 360° video renderer over OpenGL, the computer share and the watching of folders. On macOS: a run on a real Mac (Gatekeeper, the OpenGL video path, the keychain after an update, the local network, Finder drops, signing in to a server), then a Developer ID signature and a notarization, then the 360° video renderer.
- **Google Play**: build 25 is public since 20 October 2026, after build 23 on 14 October 2026 with the new listing; build 26 is sent after its GitHub release, with the 16 KB memory pages that Google Play asks for. The listing for TVs has been under Google's review since 9 October 2026.
- **App Store**: version 3.3.1, with build 24, has been waiting for Apple's review since 14 October 2026, with a new text, iPhone and iPad screenshots of the mode without a server, and the header and search visuals of the store; version 3.3.0 was rejected on 13 October 2026 under guideline 4.3(a), and the answer went with 3.3.1. The link will be added here when it is live.
- **Meta Horizon Store**: the app has been public on the [Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/) since 7 October 2026, with build 14 first. Version 2, with build 23, is public since 18 October 2026: it brought everything since build 14 (uploads from a share to Immich, the video source chosen from what the headset decodes, raw Insta360, GoPro and DJI files, DLNA, the phone share, Apple spatial photos, Plex Media Server libraries, Tapo cameras). Version 3, with build 25 and its welcome page, has been in Meta's review since 18 October 2026. The store lists the app for the Quest 2, Quest Pro, Quest 3 and 3S; only the Quest 3 is tested. To install the store version, a sideloaded copy has to be uninstalled first (see [Install](platform-support.md#install-on-the-headset)).
- **Store listings**: the Google Play listing was rewritten in October 2026, with new phone and TV screenshots and a TV banner, and is public since 14 October 2026; its tablet slots still show the phone screenshots. The App Store text was rewritten with version 3.3.1. The Meta Horizon Store listing has English screenshots of the app's window since version 2.
- **Raw 360° camera files, next**: a progress indicator while a raw photo is prepared for the headset; levelling of GoPro and DJI videos from their own motion data; dual fisheye .dng; device reports on the two lens playback of build 18 (X4, X5, X6, GoPro MAX 2, Osmo 360) to confirm seams and decoder budgets.
- **DLNA, phone share and Apple spatial, next**: the device reports of build 19 (Plex, Jellyfin, a NAS and the Freebox Server over DLNA; a phone serving a Quest, on its hotspot too; real iPhone spatial photos and videos in the headset); the multicast entitlement asked from Apple, so that iPhones find every DLNA server; previous and next between spatial photos in the headset; a spatial badge on server photos in the timeline; spatial videos in 3D on the Quest, if its decoders allow it.
- **360° players on phones, next**: a time bar in the iOS 360° video player (the Android one has it), previous/next in the 360° players of phones as in the Quest immersive view, and photos in the native 360° video player.
- **Network shares, next steps**: swiping from one file of a folder to the next in the photo and video pages (the Quest immersive view already goes through the 360° files of a folder), Digest authentication for WebDAV, the user name from the Bonjour record.
- **Flat videos**: the audio track choice in the flat player of phones, for server, device and share videos alike (the 360° and Spatial players have it, and so does the flat player of Immuch360 Desktop).
- **Android TV, next**: the device test on a real TV (builds 21, 22 and 26 were tried on the Google TV emulator, without the Plex and Tapo pages); the Location filter sheet and the quick date sheet of the search with the remote; the TV release on Google Play once Google has reviewed the listing for TVs; later, channels on the TV home screen.
- **Tapo cameras, next**: the device test of build 20 with real cameras; the live view on iPhone and iPad; H.265 recordings; playing a clip while it is fetched; a whole day of recordings on one time line.
- **Plex, next**: the device test of build 20 (phones, the Quest, an iPhone, a TV, away from home); bringing the token from the computer with a QR code; hiding the DLNA side of a Plex server in the list of found servers; IPv6.
- **Upstream**: small pull requests to Immich for the parts the maintainers want, starting with the 360° photo viewer.
