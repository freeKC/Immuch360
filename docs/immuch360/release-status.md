The app is on Google Play for phones and tablets and on the Meta Horizon Store; the App Store version is waiting for Apple's review, and the Google Play version for TVs is waiting for Google's review of the TV release. ## The builds

- **Phones, tablets, headsets and TVs**: build 28, version 3.3.0-rc.0, build number 3030026, is the GitHub release [v3.3.0-rc.0-28](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-28): the universal APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-28-release.apk`), the APKs for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64, the Quest APK (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-28-quest-release.apk`), the App Bundle (`Immuch360-v3.3.0-rc.0-28.aab`) that goes to Google Play, and `Immuch360-libsmb2-sources.tar.gz`, the corresponding source of the SMB library.
- **New in build 28**:
  - **Sign in with your phone.** The sign-in page of Android TV and Google TV, of the Meta Quest window and of the computers shows a QR code and a code of 10 characters beside the server form. Immuch360 on an Android phone signed in to the server (Settings, "Connect a TV or computer") scans it, or takes the code typed by hand, shows a number of six digits to compare with the screen, and signs the screen in with a session of its own, which it asks the server for and sends encrypted on the home network: the password never leaves the phone, and nothing is typed on the remote. The session is named after the screen, listed on the phone under "Connected by this phone" and in Immich (Account settings, Authorized devices), and signed out from either; signing the phone out signs out the screens it connected. Without the app, the camera of the phone opens a page served by the screen, where the email and the password go encrypted and are used once. The scanner also recognises the code of the LG webOS app and opens its page in the browser. On iPhone and iPad, the phone side comes with version 3.3.2. See [Sign in with your phone](user-guide.md#sign-in-a-tv-a-headset-or-a-computer-with-your-phone).
  - **DJI link diagnostics** (experimental, on by default; Android phones and tablets and the Meta Quest): a page, in Network shares, that reads a DJI remote controller plugged in with a cable. It says whether the device can read a USB accessory at all, follows the state of the link, counts the frames by channel, and exports a capture of what arrived, for the next steps towards a drone's video in the headset. It only listens and sends no command: the one byte a second that tells whether the remote takes data carries none. See [DJI link](user-guide.md#dji-link-diagnostics-experimental).
  - **The Tapo live view on iPhone and iPad**, for version 3.3.2: the app turns the stream of the camera into a live HLS playlist on the device, played by the system player a few seconds behind the camera, with its sound.
  - **Quality**: before the paid iOS build, a free GitHub macOS runner compiles the iOS app of the release (`.github/workflows/immuch360-ios-check.yml`).
  - **Checked**: sign in with your phone between a Google TV emulator and a phone emulator against an Immich 3.3.0-rc.0 server (a QR code read from a screenshot of the TV, the code typed, the number on both sides, Cancel, Sign out from the phone and from the TV, the browser page with wrong and right passwords), and the sign-in page of the TV with its keyboard after a fix of the release; the DJI link decoder by about a hundred unit tests, its page on a phone emulator; the iOS live view against an RTSP server shaped like a camera, its playlist decoded without an error, its Swift compiled on the macOS runner. Not tried yet: a real camera scan, a headset, a computer signed in that way, a real DJI remote, an iPhone.
- **New in build 27** (22 October 2026, [v3.3.0-rc.0-27](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-27)):
  - **Android TV and Google TV, after a first run on a real TV.** The app ran on a real Android TV for the first time on 21 October 2026 (with build 25 or 26). Photos and 360° photos went well; five problems came back: a video played in a loop and Left and Right could not leave it, the controls stayed over the picture, 360° videos never played, the white pages were too bright, and the app froze once while photos scrolled. Build 27 answers them:
    - **Videos with the remote**: Left and Right go to the previous or next item, even while a video plays (a video alone in its list still jumps 10 seconds); OK shows the controls with play and pause selected, a second OK pauses; the controls have 10 second back and forward buttons; the controls and the date hide 5 seconds after the last key and come back on any other key; videos no longer play in a loop by default on a TV (Settings, Asset Viewer, Videos, Looping).
    - **Dark by default**: on a TV the app is dark, its launch screen included, even when the TV says light; Settings, Preferences, Theme has one "Dark mode" switch there.
    - **The details**: the Menu key, or OK held for a moment, opens the details of a photo or video, as the Info key does (most Google TV remotes have no Info key). On a TV they are read only: no description field, no pencils, no keyboard opening on the way down, and the focus starts on their first row; a person opened from them has no edit menu either.
    - **The search filters**: OK on Country, State, City, Make or Model opens the choices in a list; the date filter shows its five choices at once, and OK on the year opens a list of years. The Tags, Location, Date, Media Type, Star Rating and Display options sheets put Clear and Apply beside the choices, one Right away, and take most of the height of the screen; Star Rating scrolls to all its choices. This round builds on mlc70's pull request [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#3](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/3), five of whose commits are kept with their authorship, after [#1](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/1) in build 26.
    - **A video that cannot play says why**, instead of loading forever: "This video cannot be played", with the cause (beyond the video decoders of the device, with their limit) and what the server can do, or "This video is not loading" after 20 seconds without progress. Play tries again, and Left and Right move on. A 360° video beyond the decoders asks before the 360° player opens (Play or Close), and the 360° player names the cause when it cannot play it.
    - **A smoother grid**: less image memory on a TV, a TV with 3 GB of memory or less treated as one with little memory, and a held arrow moving at most about 10 rows a second, which stops when it is released.
    - **The Logs page** records why a video does not play, the failures of the 360° player, a stalled app (no answer for 3 seconds) and why the app last ended (Android 11 and later), so that a report from a real TV says what happened.
    - Checked on the Google TV emulator with the keys of a remote and by widget tests; build 27 has not run on a real TV yet.
  - **Turn a photo with one key**, on every platform: the Rotate button of the bottom row on phones, tablets and in the Quest window (a long press turns it the other way), R and Shift+R on a keyboard, the yellow and blue keys of a TV remote, the rotate button of the top bar on a computer. Your own photos on an Immich server 2.6 or later are turned through Immich's photo editor, so they show turned on the web and every device; the others are turned on this device only. 360°, 180° and 3D photos, Apple spatial photos, raw files, 2:1 photos of Insta360 cameras, videos and the locked folder never turn, and the 360° views always read the photo as the camera made it. Asked for by gthrepwood in [discussion #3](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/discussions/3). Checked on the Google TV emulator against an Immich 3.3.0-rc.0 server (a turn saved on the server and undone, the colour keys, a 360° photo left alone) and by widget tests on every platform.
  - **DJI drones over USB** (Android phones and tablets, experimental and on by default: Settings, Advanced, "DJI drones (experimental)" turns it off): a "DJI drone" page in Network shares reads a drone, its controller or a card plugged in with a USB-C cable, lists its photos and videos by day without the proxy and flight data files, and sends the originals to Immich, or keeps them in the gallery of the phone. A video whose flight data (.SRT) holds a position is placed on the map when the server found none in it, which is the case of every DJI Neo 2 video today. The spheres of DJI drones, which most often carry no 360° tags, show as 360° photos on every platform, and can be sent with the tags Immich needs (an option of the upload, off by default). Checked on an Android phone emulator with synthetic files; not with a real drone yet, see [DJI drones](user-guide.md#dji-drones-photos-and-videos-over-usb-experimental).
  - **Every device**: the name and the controls of a video of a share hide 5 seconds after the last key or touch while it plays; the date filter of the search scrolls on a phone held sideways.

## Roadmap

What is not done yet, the most likely first. Nothing here is a promise, and feedback on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) helps decide what comes first.

- **Immuch360 Desktop**: desktop build 8 is out for the three systems, signed on Windows. On Windows, next: the test of each function by hand on a Windows PC with real files, both screens, a real camera and sign in with your phone, with its fixes; Spatial 2.5D with the webcam, in development for November 2026; then an update check. On Linux: a run on a real Linux PC (a live USB session) with its graphics chip, on X11 and Wayland, then the 360° video renderer over OpenGL, the computer share and the watching of folders. On macOS: a run on a real Mac (Gatekeeper, the OpenGL video path, the keychain after an update, the local network, Finder drops, signing in to a server), then a Developer ID signature and a notarization, then the 360° video renderer.
- **Google Play**: build 25 is public since 20 October 2026, after build 23 on 14 October 2026 with the new listing; build 26, with the 16 KB memory pages that Google Play asks for, went to Google's review on 20 October 2026, build 27 replaced it there on 22 October 2026, and build 28 replaces build 27 after its GitHub release. The listing for TVs has been under Google's review since 9 October 2026. In build 27, Left and Right go to the next item while a video plays and OK shows the controls first, where Google's quality guidelines for TV apps ask that they seek and pause: the review of the TV listing may ask to go back, which is one switch in the code.
- **App Store**: version 3.3.1 has been waiting for Apple's review since 14 October 2026; on 22 October 2026 it was sent again with build 27, the name "Immuch360 Photo & Video Player", a new text and new iPhone and iPad captures. Version 3.3.0 was rejected on 13 October 2026 under guideline 4.3(a), and the answer went with 3.3.1. Version 3.3.2, with build 28, follows it. The link will be added here when it is live.
