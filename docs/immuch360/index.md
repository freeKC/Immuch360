# Immuch360 documentation

The documents of this folder, for the people who use Immuch360 and for those who build it.

Back to the [README](../../README.md)

- [Features](features.md): the complete inventory of the functions, and the feature by platform matrix.
- [User guide](user-guide.md): every function, step by step, with its options, its messages and its limits.
- [Compatibility](compatibility.md): the formats and sources by platform, the comparison with the Immich app and other apps, the servers and devices checked, and the limits by feature.
- [Platform support](platform-support.md): how far each system is available, what it has, how it is installed, its input, and the license and trademarks.
- [Known issues](known-issues.md): the known problems, and what has not been checked on a device yet, by platform and by feature.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): the logs to attach to a report, a video that stutters, the first start on Windows, videos on Linux, a report from a Mac, and the messages that need an answer.
- [Technical details](technical-details.md): the architecture, the Flutter app and its native modules, the media bridge, the sources, the stitching of raw files, the decoders, the TV mode and the desktop layer.
- [The desktop 360° video renderer](desktop-360-video-renderer.md): libmpv, the projection pass of the Windows 360° player, and the measurements behind it.
- [Release status](release-status.md): the builds, what each store has, and the roadmap.
- [Build it yourself](build.md): the build for phones, tablets, headsets, TVs and computers, the branches and the CI.
- [Store communication brief](STORE_COMMUNICATION_BRIEF.md): the positioning, the promise and the benefits of Immuch360, for whoever writes the store listings.
