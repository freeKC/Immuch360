# Immuch360

**Midia yako. Skrini yoyote. Mtazamo wowote.**

Immuch360 ni programu huria na ya chanzo wazi ya kutafuta, kuvinjari, kucheza na kuchunguza picha na video zako: za kawaida, na zile za panoramiki, 360°, 3D na za kuzamisha ambazo programu nyingine huonyesha bapa, mara mbili au zilizonyooshwa. Inazisoma zilipo, kwenye kifaa chenyewe, kwenye NAS au kompyuta, kwenye seva ya Plex au kwenye seva ya Immich, na kuzicheza kwenye simu, kompyuta kibao, televisheni, vifaa vya kichwa vya Meta Quest na kompyuta. Hakuna akaunti, hakuna wingu, hakuna seva inayohitajika.

- **Inafanya nini?** Inaonyesha picha na video zako kama albamu na kuzicheza kwenye kitazamaji kinachofaa kila faili: bapa, kama tufe unaloweza kuzunguka ndani yake, katika 3D, kwenye kifaa cha kichwa.
- **Midia gani?** Picha na video bapa, panorama, picha na video za 360°, VR180, 3D ya stereo, picha za spatial za Apple, tufe za 360° za droni za DJI, na faili ghafi za kamera za 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), zinazoshonwa na programu.
- **On which devices?** Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Android TV and Google TV, LG TVs with webOS and Samsung TVs with Tizen (a separate TV app, in test builds), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S, Windows (preview), and Linux and macOS as early previews.
- **Je, ninahitaji seva au akaunti?** Hapana. Immuch360 inafanya kazi yenyewe na midia ya kifaa chako. Seva ya Immich, NAS, seva ya midia au seva ya Plex ni chaguo unazoziongeza unapozihitaji.
- **Ninawezaje kuingia kwenye televisheni, kifaa cha kichwa au kompyuta?** Kwa simu yako: changanua msimbo wa QR unaoonyeshwa na ukurasa wake wa kuingia, au andika msimbo wake, thibitisha, na inafunguka kwenye maktaba yako ya Immich ndani ya sekunde chache, bila kuandika chochote kwa kidhibiti cha mbali.

<a id="what-you-can-do"></a>
## Unaweza kufanya nini

Matumizi kila moja hapa chini linaeleza unachopata, kisha jinsi ya kuyafanya kwa hatua chache. Mwongozo kamili, wenye kila chaguo, ujumbe na kikomo, upo kwenye [mwongozo wa mtumiaji](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Tazama picha na video zako

Immuch360 is first a gallery and a player for ordinary photos and videos. The timeline, the albums, the favorites, the memories and the search work with an Immich server; the device's own photos and videos, and the folders of a computer, show without any server. Videos play in a native player with sound, seeking, a buffering indicator and a choice of audio tracks, in the 360°, Spatial and computer players and, from build 29, in the flat player of the Android app, which shows subtitles too. On a TV the remote drives everything; on a computer the mouse and the keyboard do.

1. Fungua kichupo cha Picha: rekodi ya matukio ya seva yako, au ya kifaa bila seva.
2. Gusa picha au video ili kuifungua; sogeza kidole kushoto na kulia ili kuendelea na inayofuata, bana au gusa mara mbili ili kukuza.
3. Sogeza kidole juu kwenye video ili kuona maelezo yake: kodeki, ukubwa, kasi ya fremu, kasi ya biti, na iwapo kifaa hiki kinaidekodi.
4. On Android phones and TVs, from build 29, a film with several audio languages or with subtitles has an audio button and a subtitles button in its controls: the audio language you pick is kept for the next films, and subtitles stay off until you pick them, except forced ones. The Immich app's Android player has neither.

Zaidi: [vitazamaji na kicheza video](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [maelezo ya video na videkoda](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Zungusha picha kwa kitufe kimoja

Zungusha picha kwa 90° kwa kubonyeza mara moja, bila kufungua kihariri: kitufe kwenye skrini, kitufe cha kibodi au kitufe cha rangi cha kidhibiti cha mbali cha televisheni, kwenye kila jukwaa. Programu ya Immich huzungusha picha ndani ya kihariri chake; Immuch360 hufanya hivyo kwa kitufe kimoja, na kwa picha zako mwenyewe kwenye seva ya Immich 2.6 au toleo jipya zaidi hupitia kihariri hichohicho cha seva, hivyo programu ya wavuti na vifaa vyako vingine vinazionyesha zikiwa zimezungushwa. Picha nyingine yoyote huzungushwa kwenye kifaa hiki tu. Picha za 360°, 180° na 3D hazizungushwi kamwe, ili mitazamo ya kuzamisha ibaki imenyooka.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360 Rotate 90°">
</p>

1. **Simu, kompyuta kibao na dirisha la Meta Quest**: fungua picha na gusa Rotate kwenye safu ya chini, kabla ya Edit, ili kuizungusha kisaa; kubonyeza kwa muda mrefu huizungusha upande mwingine. Kwa kibodi, R na Shift+R.
2. **Android TV na Google TV**: kitufe cha njano cha kidhibiti cha mbali huzungusha picha kisaa na kitufe cha bluu kinyume cha saa, popote ulipo uteuzi ndani ya kitazamaji. Kwenye kidhibiti cha mbali kisicho na vitufe vya rangi, bonyeza Chini: upau ulio chini ya picha unaonekana ukiwa umechagua Rotate (hatua moja ya Kulia zaidi picha ikiwa na kitufe cha maandishi), na kila OK huzungusha picha.
3. **Televisheni za LG (webOS)**: njano na bluu kama kwenye Android TV, R na Shift+R kwenye kibodi, au kitufe cha kuzungusha kwenye vidhibiti vya picha.
4. **Windows, Linux na macOS**: R huzungusha kisaa, Shift+R kinyume cha saa, au kitufe cha kuzungusha kwenye upau wa juu, kabla ya skrini nzima.
5. **Picha za hifadhi ya mtandao, Plex au kamera**: kitufe cha kuzungusha kikiwa cha kwanza kwenye upau wa juu, R na Shift+R, au vitufe vya njano na bluu.

Mahali mzunguko unapohifadhiwa: picha ya akaunti yako mwenyewe kwenye Immich 2.6 au toleo jipya zaidi huzungushwa kupitia kihariri cha picha cha seva, ambacho huhifadhi faili asili na kutengeneza picha iliyozungushwa (upau mwembamba unaonekana kinapofanya kazi; mizunguko minne, au mzunguko mmoja wa kurudi, hurejesha picha asili). Picha za kifaa, za hifadhi, za mshirika au za albamu iliyoshirikiwa, na kila picha kwenye seva ya zamani zaidi huzungushwa kwenye kifaa hiki tu, kwa picha hiyo, na faili haibadilishwi kamwe; programu inasema hivyo mara moja kwa picha ya Immich. Kwenye programu za Android, iOS na kompyuta, mzunguko uliohifadhiwa kwenye kifaa unaonekana kwenye kitazamaji, lakini bado si kwenye vijipicha, onyesho la slaidi na kumbukumbu. Video, folda iliyofungwa na kila picha ya 360°, 180° au 3D hazina Rotate.

<a id="explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d"></a>
### Chunguza panorama, 360°, VR180 na 3D

Picha ya 360° inafunguka kama tufe unalolizungusha kwa kidole au kwa kuusogeza simu; video ya 360° inacheza kwenye kicheza cha tufe chenye sauti; faili za VR180 huchorwa kwenye nusu tufe; faili za 3D za stereo huonyesha jicho moja kwenye skrini bapa na macho mawili kwenye kifaa cha kichwa. Panorama zisizo kamili huhifadhi upunguzaji wake, na picha huanza kwenye mtazamo kamera ilivyorekodi. Faili ambayo seva haiiweki alama inaweza kutazamwa kama 360° kwa mgusano mmoja.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-sphere.jpg" width="260" alt="Picha ya 360° ya ufukwe wa ziwa kwenye kitazamaji cha tufe: kitufe cha kufunga juu kushoto, vitufe vya 360°, mpangilio wa 3D na jairoskopu juu kulia">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-video.png" width="420" alt="Video ya 360° ya barabara ya pwani ikicheza kwenye kicheza cha 360° cha simu iliyoshikwa kwa ulalo: kufunga na jina la faili juu kushoto, 360° na 3D juu kulia, iliyotangulia, ruka nyuma, cheza, ruka mbele na inayofuata katikati, upau wa muda chini">
</p>

1. Fungua picha au video. Faili za 360° zina beji ya 360° kwenye kijipicha chake, na kichupo cha Maktaba kina orodha ya 360° inayozikusanya, yenye vichujio vya kipindi, chanzo, aina na kamera.
2. Gusa 360° kwenye upau wa juu. Buruta ili kuzunguka, bana ili kukuza, gusa kitufe cha jairoskopu ili kuzunguka kwa kuigeuza simu.
3. Kitufe cha 3D hubadilika kati ya "Mono (si 3D)", "3D, juu na chini" na "3D, ubavu kwa ubavu"; kitufe cha 360°/180° hubadilisha kati ya tufe kamili na nusu tufe ya VR180.
4. Kwa faili inayoonekana bapa ingawa ni ya 360°: ⋮ juu kulia, kisha "Tazama kama 360°".

Kwenye simu na kompyuta kibao, video ya stereo pia inaweza kupata kina kwenye skrini bapa: kicheza cha Spatial 2.5D kinatumia kamera ya mbele kufuata kichwa chako na kuonyesha mtazamo kati ya macho mawili (ya majaribio). Picha za spatial za Apple zilizopigwa kwa iPhone zinatambuliwa na kuonyeshwa katika 3D kwenye Meta Quest.

Zaidi: [picha na video za 360°](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#360-photos-and-videos-as-a-sphere), [3D na VR180](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#3d-and-vr180-photos-and-videos), [Spatial 2.5D](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#depth-on-a-flat-screen-spatial-25d-experimental), [picha na video za spatial za Apple](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#apple-spatial-photos-and-videos), [orodha ya 360°](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list).

<a id="use-your-own-files-without-an-account"></a>
### Tumia faili zako mwenyewe, bila akaunti

Immuch360 haihitaji akaunti wala seva. Kuanzia build 24 programu inafunguka kwenye ukurasa wa kukaribisha, ambapo "Tumia bila seva" inakuja kwanza: inafungua programu kwenye picha na video za kifaa, pamoja na kila kitazamaji, orodha ya 360° na vyanzo vya mtandao. Hakuna chochote kinachotoka kwenye kifaa. Seva inaweza kuunganishwa baadaye, kutoka Mipangilio au kichupo cha Maktaba, na vipengele vya seva vinaonekana basi.

<img src="../.github/readme/b19-library-no-server.png" width="260" alt="Kichupo cha Maktaba bila seva: kipengee cha 360° juu, kisha On this device chenye picha mbili za 360°, na Hifadhi za mtandao zenye hifadhi inayoitwa NAS">

1. Fungua programu. Ukurasa wa kukaribisha unaonyesha "Midia yako. Skrini yoyote. Mtazamo wowote." na chaguo tatu: gusa la kwanza, "Tumia bila seva". Mpaka build 23, ukurasa wa kuingia ulikuja kwanza, pamoja na "Tumia bila seva" chini ya fomu.
2. Ruhusu ufikiaji wa picha na video zako. Ukikataa, programu inasema "Programu haiwezi kuona picha za kifaa hiki", pamoja na "Ruhusu ufikiaji" na "Fungua mipangilio".
3. Kichupo cha Photos (Picha) kinaonyesha rekodi ya matukio ya kifaa. Search (Tafuta) na Albums (Albamu) zinabaki za kijivu, kwa kuwa zinatoka kwenye seva. Kichupo cha Maktaba kina 360° juu, kisha On this device (Kwenye kifaa hiki) (albamu za kifaa) na Hifadhi za mtandao.
4. Menyu ya juu kulia inasema "Kifaa hiki pekee": programu inaonyesha picha na video za kifaa hiki, hakuna kinachotumwa popote.

Kwenye kompyuta, folda unazochagua huchukua jukumu la albamu; kwenye televisheni, isiyo na picha zake, vyanzo vya mtandao hufanya hivyo.

### Fikia midia ya NAS, kompyuta au seva ya midia

Video zako ziko kwenye NAS, kompyuta au seva ya midia. Simu pia inaweza kushiriki albamu yake yenyewe kwenye Wi-Fi kwa kifaa cha kichwa au mteja wowote wa WebDAV, na kompyuta ya Windows inaweza pia.

<a id="your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server"></a>
### Picha na video za droni yako ya DJI kwenye seva yako ya Immich

Import the photos and videos of a DJI drone over USB, straight to your Immich server: plug the drone or a card reader into an Android phone, pick the new files and send them, with the videos placed on the map from the drone's flight data and the drone's 360° spheres shown as 360°. The Immich app backs up only what is already in the phone's gallery, and the Immich server cannot read the place of a DJI Neo 2 video yet. This page is experimental, on Android phones and tablets only, and has not been tried with a real drone yet.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/dji-drone-import.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360, a DJI drone and Immich">
</p>

1. Kigae cha "DJI drone" kipo kwenye Maktaba, Hifadhi za mtandao: "DJI drones (experimental)" imewashwa kwa chaguomsingi kwenye Settings, Advanced. Izime hapo ili kuficha kigae.
2. Plug the drone (a Neo 2 can stay off) or a card reader into the phone with a USB-C data cable; a DJI remote without a screen (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3) holds no photos. On the DJI drone page, tap "Choose the drone or the card", choose it in the side menu of the picker, open its DCIM folder or keep the whole drive, then "Use this folder" and Allow.
3. Ukurasa unaorodhesha picha na video kwa siku, bila faili saidizi za .LRF, .SRT, .THM na .m4a. Gusa "Select all new", kisha "Pakia kwenye Immich"; faili zilizotumwa awali zina alama ya wingu na zinarukwa. "Keep on the device" badala yake hunakili faili kwenye albamu ya simu, katika albamu "Immuch360 import".
4. Ili video iwekwe kwenye ramani, washa Video Subtitles kwenye DJI Fly (hali ya video, Settings, Camera) kabla ya kurusha droni: droni kisha huandika faili ya .SRT kando ya kila video, programu husoma mahali kutoka humo na kupaweka kwenye seva yako baada ya kupakia, wakati Immich haikupata mahali.

Tufe za 360° za droni za DJI (Mini, Air, Mavic na nyingine, zinazotambuliwa kutokana na kamera yao) zinaonekana kama 360° kwenye Immuch360 hata bila lebo za 360°. Immich inahitaji lebo hizo: chaguo la upakiaji "Tag drone spheres as 360° for Immich", lililozimwa kwa chaguo-msingi, hutuma picha bila kuibadilisha pamoja na faili ndogo ya .xmp kando yake, ili Immich nayo iionyeshe kama tufe. Neo 2 haina hali ya tufe wala ya panorama: picha na video zake ni bapa. Simu iliporekodi sauti ya klipu ya Neo 2, sauti hiyo iko kwenye faili tofauti ya .m4a: ukurasa unaiwekea klipu alama, na Immich inaipokea bila sauti (ipakue kwa DJI Fly ili kubaki na sauti). Kadi yoyote ya kamera yenye folda ya DCIM inafanya kazi vivyo hivyo kwenye kisomaji cha kadi. Hakuna kinachotumwa kwa DJI.

<a id="your-dji-drones-live-video-on-your-phone-experimental"></a>
### Your DJI drone's live video on your phone (experimental)

See what your DJI drone sees on your Android phone, without DJI Fly and without any account: plug a DJI RC-N3 remote controller into the phone with its cable, and Immuch360 shows the drone's live video, H.265 in 1080p, with the battery of the remote and of the drone, the signal, the gimbal and the flight data, read from the cable. The remote keeps full control of the drone. It was tried on the ground with an RC-N3, a DJI Neo 2 and a Samsung Galaxy S24+; other remotes and drones have not been tried. The flight and battery numbers are not compared with DJI Fly yet: do not fly by them.

Inasikiliza tu. Hakuna amri inayotumwa kamwe kwa kidhibiti cha mbali wala kwa droni: baiti moja ambayo ukurasa huandika kila sekunde, kuanzia sekunde 20 baada ya kidhibiti cha mbali kufunguliwa, inaonyesha tu kama kidhibiti cha mbali kinapokea data, na haibebi amri yoyote.

1. Mipangilio, Mahiri, "Uchunguzi wa kiungo cha DJI (wa majaribio)" umewashwa kwa chaguo-msingi (kamwe kwenye televisheni); kigae cha "Kiungo cha DJI" kipo katika Maktaba, Hifadhi za mtandao.
2. Turn on the drone and the remote, close DJI Fly, then open the DJI link tile and read the safety notice.
3. Tap "Start the capture", then plug in the remote with its cable. If Android asks which app should open it, choose Immuch360 without "Always", which would take the remote away from DJI Fly; if it asks for a USB mode, choose charging only.
4. The state reads "Accessory open", then "Remote frames arrive", and the video follows within a few seconds. The button on the picture shows it full screen, and the "Flight and battery" section gives the numbers of the remote and the drone.
5. "Export the capture" writes three files to `Download/Immuch360/dji-link`; "Play a capture" plays an export again on the page, without the remote, to see how well a phone or a headset decodes the drone's video.

The drone's photos and videos cannot be reached through the remote, which does not open the drone's storage: copy them from the drone itself over USB or from its card, as in [the section above](#your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server). On a Meta Quest the page is there and plays a capture, but the drone's live video does not reach the headset yet. Keep the exported files to yourself, as the page says, since they hold the serial number of the remote; a report in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with the states and counters the page shows helps, above all with other DJI remotes.

## Vipengele vya kina

Kile programu inachoweza kusoma na kucheza, kwa msomaji anayetaka ukubwa wote. Mstari kila mmoja umeelezwa kwa kina kwenye [mwongozo wa mtumiaji](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md) na [ukurasa wa utangamanifu](../docs/immuch360/compatibility.md).

- **Miundo**: picha na video bapa (kile kifaa kinachodekodi: MP4, MOV, MKV na zaidi); picha na video za 360° za equirectangular; panorama zisizo kamili (upunguzaji wa GPano); VR180; 3D ya stereo, juu na chini au ubavu kwa ubavu, inayosomwa kutoka faili (kisanduku cha st3d) au kukisiwa kutoka fremu; picha za spatial za Apple (jozi za stereo za HEIC) na video za spatial (MV-HEVC, jicho moja); faili ghafi za Insta360 .insp na .insv, GoPro .360, DJI .osv; vyanzo vya HLG na PQ vya biti 10 vilivyorekebishwa toni kwenye tufe.
- **Uonyeshaji na metadata**: metadata za GPano na za tufe zinazosomwa kutoka faili, kwa maombi ya safu kwenye seva au hifadhi; mtazamo wa awali wa kamera; tufe za droni za DJI zinazotambuliwa kutokana na kamera yao, pamoja na faili saidizi ya .xmp ya hiari ili Immich nayo izitambulishe; "Tazama kama 360°" inakumbukwa kwenye kifaa; VR180 inatambuliwa kutoka faili au jina lake; mahali pa video ya droni panasomwa kutoka kumbukumbu yake ya safari ya .SRT; metadata inabaki bila kubadilika kwa sababu faili asili ndiyo inayochezwa, kamwe nakala iliyobadilishwa; mzunguko wa robo unaohifadhiwa kupitia kihariri cha Immich au kubaki kwenye kifaa, kamwe kwenye faili ya kuzamisha.
- **Native playback**: Media3 on Android and the TV, SceneKit and AVFoundation on iOS, the Meta Spatial SDK in the headset, libmpv on Windows; the original or the transcoded stream chosen from what the device decodes; a list of the device's video decoders in Settings, Advanced; the audio track choice, subtitles in the Android player and a buffering indicator.
- **Faili ghafi zinazoshonwa kwenye kifaa**: fragment shader ya Flutter kwa picha; athari ya GPU kwenye kicheza kwa video za njia moja; videkoda mbili za maunzi kwa wakati mmoja na GPU compositor kwa video zenye lenzi moja kwa njia au faili (OpenGL ES 3 kwenye Android na Quest, Metal kwenye iOS, na kwenye Windows, kuanzia toleo la desktop 5, vicheza viwili vya libmpv ambavyo fremu zake huunganishwa na programu kwenye chip ya michoro); urekebishaji wa kamera wenyewe (mifano ya Mei, V6 na equidistant) na rekodi ya akselerometa; kifaa hubadili kutumia lenzi moja, mkondo uliobadilishwa, nakala ya LRV ya kamera, au video isiyoshonwa, kinaposhindwa kuendesha videkoda mbili.
- **Sources**: the device's gallery; the folders of a computer; a drone or a camera card over USB, through the system folder picker (Android, experimental); a DJI RC-N3 remote controller over its cable for the drone's live video (Android, experimental); an Immich server; SMB 2 and 3, WebDAV, DLNA/UPnP; Plex over HTTPS checked against the server's own certificate; Tapo cameras over the local network (HTTPS and RTSP); a phone or a computer shared on the Wi-Fi as a WebDAV server; servers found by Bonjour/mDNS, a local network scan, SSDP, GDM and the TP-Link discovery, and followed when they change address.
- **Usomaji kwa mahitaji**: daraja la ndani linahudumia vicheza kwa safu za baiti na tokeni kwa kila kipindi; vizuizi vikubwa, usomaji wa mbele na muunganisho hadi sita wa SMB kwa wakati mmoja; vijipicha kutoka fremu ya video kila moja; hakuna kinachonakiliwa kwenye kifaa.
- **Maunzi**: videkoda vya maunzi hutumika popote vilipo, pamoja na mipaka yake iliyopimwa (kikomo cha H.264 cha Quest 3, lenzi mbili za 2048x2048 kwa upeo kwa videkoda vya programu); kwenye Windows mtazamo wa 360° unachorwa na chip ya michoro kutoka kila fremu iliyodekodiwa, ukubwa wa fremu unapimwa kwenye kompyuta kila moja, chip iliyoambatanishwa au ya pekee hugunduliwa.
- **Mambo mahususi kwa jukwaa**: jairoskopu na kicheza cha Spatial 2.5D kwenye simu; mtazamo wa kuzamisha, vidhibiti na mikono kwenye Quest; mpangilio wa kidhibiti cha mbali, kidirisha cha kibodi na bango la televisheni kwenye Android TV; kwenye kompyuta, kipanya na kibodi, skrini nzima, dirisha moja, faili na folda zinazofunguliwa na programu au kudondoshwa kwenye dirisha lake, zinazosomwa kama faili za folda zako, picha za HEIC, AVIF na TIFF, na picha ghafi za kamera za folda wakati Windows ina kisimbuzi kwa ajili yake, na ulinzi wa data wa Windows kwa manenosiri na tokeni.
- **Privacy by construction**: no account, no cloud, no analytics; passwords and tokens in the device's secure storage (on LG and Samsung TVs, which have none for the app, in the app's own storage, not encrypted); a screen signed in from a phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on the home network, never the password (an LG TV gets the password from a page in the phone's browser, unencrypted on the home network, and keeps only the session); the camera of the Spatial player and of the code scanner used on the device only; shares, Plex and cameras reached on your network only.

### Android TV na Google TV

1. Mpaka Google Play itoe programu kwenye televisheni, pata `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` ya ulimwengu wote kutoka ukurasa wa [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. Kwenye televisheni, washa chaguo za msanidi programu na utatuzi wa USB (kwenye Google TV: Mipangilio, Mfumo, Kuhusu, chagua "Android TV OS build" mara saba, kisha Mipangilio, Mfumo, Chaguo za msanidi programu; majina yanatofautiana kati ya televisheni).
3. Kutoka kompyuta iliyo kwenye mtandao uleule, ukitumia adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, kubali ombi kwenye televisheni, kisha `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Programu inaonekana kati ya programu za televisheni, pamoja na bango lake. Sakinisha toleo linalofuata kwa njia ileile: `-r` inabakiza kuingia na mipangilio.

<a id="lg-tvs-webos"></a>
### Televisheni za LG (webOS)

1. Unahitaji televisheni ya LG yenye webOS 4.0 au zaidi, kompyuta kwenye mtandao uleule na akaunti ya bure ya msanidi programu wa LG. Kwenye televisheni, sakinisha Developer Mode kutoka LG Content Store, ingia, washa Dev Mode Status na uache televisheni ijiwashe upya; andika anwani yake ya IP na kaulisiri ambayo programu inaonyesha.
2. Kwenye kompyuta, sakinisha webOS CLI (Node.js 18 au zaidi): `npm install -g @webos-tools/cli`, kisha sajili televisheni kwa `ares-setup-device` (anwani yake ya IP, mlango 9922, mtumiaji `prisoner`) na `ares-novacom --device <name> --getkey`, inayouliza kaulisiri.
3. Pakua `Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk` kutoka [toleo la awali la webOS](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/webos-v0.2.0), ilinganishe na `SHA256SUMS.txt`, kisha endesha `ares-install --device <name> Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk`. [webOS Dev Manager](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop) ya webosbrew hufanya vivyo hivyo bila mstari wa amri.
4. Kipindi cha Developer Mode hudumu saa 1000 na kitufe cha EXTEND cha programu ya Developer Mode hukifanya upya; kinapoisha, televisheni huondoa programu zilizosakinishwa kwa njia hii. Maelezo yapo kwenye [webos/README.md](../webos/README.md#install-on-a-tv-in-developer-mode).

<a id="samsung-tvs-tizen"></a>
### Samsung TVs (Tizen)

1. You need a Samsung TV with Tizen 5.5 or later (2020 models and later), a computer on the same network and a Samsung account. On the TV: Apps, App Settings, type 12345, turn Developer mode on, enter the IP address of the computer, then restart the TV.
2. Download `Immuch360-Tizen-0.1.0.wgt` from the [Tizen pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/tizen-v0.1.0) and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. A Samsung TV installs only an app signed for it, so the package must be signed again with a Samsung certificate that lists the DUID of your TV: [Apps2Samsung](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung), a community tool, reads the DUID, signs the package with your Samsung account and installs it; Tizen Studio with the Samsung Certificate Extension does the same by hand.
3. Open the app and sign in by typing your server address, then your email and password or an API key. About this TV shows what the TV supports, as text and as a QR code: that text, posted in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with what does not work, helps the most.

<a id="meta-quest"></a>
### Meta Quest

1. Kutoka [Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/): toleo la duka, linalosasishwa kadri Meta inavyokagua kila uwasilishaji.
2. Au sakinisha kwa mkono faili ya `-quest-release.apk` ya [release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases) katika hali ya msanidi programu kwa adb au SideQuest: `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-quest-release.apk`, kisha fungua Maktaba ya kifaa cha kichwa, "Vyanzo visivyojulikana".
3. Toleo la duka na APK ya GitHub zimesainiwa kwa funguo tofauti: ili kubadilisha kutoka moja kwenda nyingine, uninstall programu kwanza, hii inapoteza kuingia, mipangilio na orodha ya hifadhi za mtandao.

<a id="windows"></a>
### Windows

1. Download `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-windows-x64-setup.exe` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) (with `SHA256SUMS.txt` to check it) and open it. The installer and the ZIP are signed; Windows shows APROGSYS as the publisher. While the publisher is new to SmartScreen, it may still ask once: "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Chagua "Sakinisha kwa ajili yangu tu" (hakuna haki za msimamizi) au "Sakinisha kwa watumiaji wote", kisha lugha ya kisakinishi, ile ya Windows iliyochaguliwa tayari.
3. Anzisha Immuch360 Desktop kutoka menyu ya Kuanza. Picha na video zinaipata kwenye "Fungua na" (bonyeza kulia kwenye faili, Fungua na); haijifanyi kamwe kuwa programu yao chaguomsingi. Uanzishaji wa kwanza wa build mpya unaweza kuchukua hadi dakika moja wakati Windows inachunguza faili.
4. Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. To update, open the installer of the next build: your sign in, folders and settings stay. The ZIP of the same release is the same program as a folder, to unzip anywhere and start `immuch360.exe` from it. On a computer where desktop builds 4 to 8 stopped at the start on "duplicate column name: group_date", build 9 opens that database and keeps your data.

### Linux

On Ubuntu 22.04 or later and Debian 12 or later: `sudo apt install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-amd64.deb`; on Fedora 40 or later: `sudo dnf install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-x86_64.rpm`, both from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9). Then start Immuch360 Desktop from your applications. A preview: the folders of the computer, photos and 360° photos as a sphere, and flat videos through the system's libmpv, which the package installs (360°, 3D and raw videos play flat, with a message); SMB shares work from Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 on. On Fedora, camera videos need the FFmpeg of RPM Fusion. Without GNOME Keyring or KWallet, the passwords are kept for the session only, and the settings of the app say so.

### macOS

Open `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-macos.dmg` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) and drag the app to Applications (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later). It is not signed nor notarized yet: Control click it and choose Open on macOS 14 and earlier, or choose "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security on macOS 15 and later. Nobody working on the project has a Mac: the app was only run on the project's CI Mac, where it opens files and folders and plays flat videos with its own libmpv; its network access has not been tried. Reports are welcome.

<a id="privacy"></a>
## Faragha

Nothing goes to the developer: no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising. The app talks only to the servers you choose (your Immich server, your shares, your Plex server, your cameras) and, without a server, to nothing at all. Passwords and tokens stay in the secure storage of the device; LG and Samsung TVs have none for the app, so there they stay in the app's own storage, not encrypted. The camera is used on the device only, by the Spatial 2.5D player and to scan the code of a screen you sign in; its images are never stored and never sent anywhere. A screen signed in from your phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on your home network, never your password; an LG TV is signed in by a page in the phone's browser, which sends your email and password to the TV unencrypted on your home network, and the TV keeps only the session. The full policy is in [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), and the details per source in the [user guide](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

<a id="the-project"></a>
## Mradi

Immuch360 ni tawi lisilo rasmi la programu ya simu ya [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich), chini ya [GNU AGPL v3](../LICENSE), halihusiani na, wala haliidhinishwi na, timu ya Immich au FUTO. Tawi hili lipo kwa sababu kutazama 360° kwenye simu kumeombwa kwenye Immich tangu Januari 2024 na bado hakimo kwenye programu rasmi; linalisafirisha sasa, linakusanya maoni ya kifaa halisi, na litatoa tena kwa Immich, kwa pull request ndogo, kile ambacho watunzaji wanataka. Kila kitu programu rasmi inachofanya kimehifadhiwa; matawi, mnyororo wa ujenzi na CI vimeelezwa kwenye [Jijenge mwenyewe](../docs/immuch360/build.md).

The 360° photo viewer is based on the upstream pull request [immich-app/immich#31169](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/31169) by dmitry-brazhenko, itself built on the prototype by bencefr in [#30192](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/30192); thanks to both. The remote control fixes of the search filters and the album sorting on TVs started from the work of mlc70 in [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#1](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/1), and the action rail and the filter sheets on 80 percent of the screen height come from mlc70's [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#3](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/3), with their authorship kept; thanks to mlc70. The LG webOS app, also packaged for Samsung TVs, is built on [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) by Seeky91, under the MIT licence, kept in its folder; thanks to Seeky91. The Tapo cameras were written from what [pytapo](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/pytapo), [Home Assistant Tapo Control](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/HomeAssistant-Tapo-Control) and [python-kasa](https://github.com/python-kasa/python-kasa) document. The Meta Quest view relies on the Meta Spatial SDK, which is not open source, so it stays in this fork. The SMB shares use libsmb2, under the LGPL 2.1 or later: from build 27 and desktop build 7, every build carries its notice, its licence texts and its source archive, `Immuch360-libsmb2-sources.tar.gz`, which is also attached to each release. The Windows and macOS builds carry libmpv (the library of [mpv](https://mpv.io), with [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)) built by the project, under the LGPL, with its licences and sources, listed on the licence page of the app (Licenses, at the bottom of the profile menu); the details and the trademarks of the platforms named on this page are in [License and trademarks](../docs/immuch360/platform-support.md#license-and-trademarks).

Picha za simu za ukurasa huu zilipigwa kwenye build ya Android ndani ya kiigaji, za televisheni kwenye kiigaji cha Google TV na za Windows kwenye PC ya Windows 11, zote na panorama za CC0 kutoka [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com); picha za kifaa cha kichwa zilipigwa kwenye Quest 3.

<a id="roadmap"></a>
## Mpango wa kazi

Kile ambacho bado hakijafanywa, kinachowezekana zaidi kwanza. Hakuna hapa ambacho ni ahadi, na maoni kwenye [kifuatiliaji cha masuala](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) yanasaidia kuamua kipi kinakuja kwanza.

- **The stores**: Google Play (build 28 in review; the TV listing in review), the App Store (version 3.3.1 in review, then version 3.3.2 with build 28), the Meta Horizon Store with build 29, the LG Content Store (the submission is in preparation).
- **Kompyuta**: kujaribu kila kazi kwa mkono kwenye PC ya Windows, ikiwemo kuingia kwa simu yako; Spatial 2.5D kwa kamera ya wavuti kwenye Windows, inaendelezwa kwa ajili ya Novemba 2026; kisha kionyeshi cha video za 360° kwenye Linux na macOS, jaribio kwenye Mac halisi na PC halisi ya Linux; kisha masasisho ya kiotomatiki na programu ya macOS iliyosainiwa.
- **TVs**: build 29 on a real Android TV, its video controls and the sign in with your phone included; webOS 0.2.0 on a real LG TV, with its shares, Plex and live view, then the LG Content Store version; the Samsung test build on a real Samsung TV; then the 360° videos on the sphere, the sound of the cameras and their recordings (not before December 2026).
- **Phones and tablets**: the Tapo live view and the sign in of big screens on iPhone and iPad with the next iOS release; a time bar in the iOS 360° video player, previous and next in the 360° players of phones, audio track and subtitle menus in the iOS flat player.
- **Midia ya kuzamisha**: .dng za fisheye mbili, kusawazisha video za GoPro na DJI kutoka data zao za mwendo, video za spatial katika 3D kwenye Quest ikiwa videkoda vyake vinaruhusu, iliyotangulia na inayofuata kati ya picha za spatial kwenye kifaa cha kichwa.
- **Vyanzo**: kusogeza kidole kati ya faili za folda ya hifadhi, uthibitishaji wa Digest kwa WebDAV, msimbo wa QR wa kuleta tokeni ya Plex kutoka kompyuta, IPv6 kwa Plex, rekodi za Tapo za H.265, njia kwenye skrini ya mwanzo ya televisheni.
- **Drones**: a test of the import page with a real DJI Neo 2, then the sound recorded by the phone merged into its clip; the drone's live video relayed from the phone to the Meta Quest. Nothing that sends a command to a drone is planned before those tests.
- **Upstream**: pull request ndogo kwa Immich kwa sehemu ambazo watunzaji wanataka, kuanzia na kitazamaji cha picha za 360°.

Orodha kamili, pamoja na ukaguzi wa kifaa unaosalia kwa kila kipengele, ipo kwenye [Hali ya toleo](../docs/immuch360/release-status.md#roadmap).
