# Immuch360

**Midia yako. Skrini yoyote. Mtazamo wowote.**

Picha na video bapa, za 360° na za 3D kutoka kwenye kifaa chako, NAS yako, seva yako ya Immich, kamera zako na droni yako. Hakuna akaunti inayohitajika. Immuch360 ni programu huria na ya chanzo wazi inayosoma picha na video zako mahali zilipo na kucheza kila moja kwenye kitazamaji kinachoifaa: bapa, kama tufe unalotazama pande zote, kwa 3D, au kote kukuzunguka ndani ya kifaa cha kichwa. Inafanya kazi kwenye simu, kompyuta kibao, televisheni, vifaa vya kichwa vya Meta Quest na kompyuta.

**New: your flat photos, in 3D.** Pick a photo on your computer and get a 3D photo, a 3D scene to step into, or a 360° panorama that imagines the rest of the place around you. In a Meta Quest 3 or 3S, one press on "AI relief" and any flat photo stands out. The AI runs on your own computer and headset, and nothing is sent anywhere. Experimental, and not yet tried in a headset by the author (first test on 30 October). [See how](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama).

<a id="who-it-is-for"></a>
## Ni kwa ajili ya nani

- **Your photos are flat** and you want them in 3D, as a scene to lean into in a headset, or inside a 360° panorama, without a 3D camera: [turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama), new, made by AI on your own computer.
- **Unapiga picha za 360° au 3D** na unataka kutazama pande zote ndani ya picha zako, si kuziona bapa, mara mbili au zimevutwa: [360°, VR180 na 3D](#explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d).
- **Picha na filamu zako ziko kwenye NAS, kompyuta, Plex au Immich** na unazitaka kwenye televisheni bila kunakili chochote: [kwenye televisheni](#watch-on-the-tv-with-the-remote), [NAS, hifadhi na Plex](#play-the-files-of-a-nas-a-computer-a-media-server-or-plex).
- **Una Meta Quest** na unataka kuingia ndani ya picha na video zako mwenyewe: [kwenye kifaa cha kichwa](#step-inside-your-photos-and-videos-with-a-meta-quest).
- **Unaendesha Immich** na unataka programu yake, pamoja na vitazamaji vya kuzamisha na skrini kubwa unazoziingiza kutoka kwa simu yako: [maktaba yako ya Immich](#keep-your-immich-library-with-more), [ikilinganishwa na Immich](#compared-with-immich).
- **Unalinda nyumba yako kwa kamera za Tapo** na unazitaka karibu na picha zako, pia kwenye televisheni (bado haijakaguliwa kwa kamera halisi): [kamera za nyumbani](#watch-your-home-cameras-on-the-big-screen).
- **Unarusha droni ya DJI** na unataka picha yake ya moja kwa moja kwenye simu yako bila DJI Fly, na kwenye Meta Quest au kwenye Android TV kwa watu walio pamoja nawe (ya majaribio): [droni yako](#see-what-your-drone-sees-on-your-phone).

There is no Immuch360 account to create, and nothing goes to us: the app talks only to your devices and to the servers you choose, and downloads an AI component only when you ask for it.

### Turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama

Most of your photos are flat, and you cannot go back and take them again in 3D. Immuch360 gives them depth. On your computer, one photo becomes a **3D photo**, a **3D scene** you lean into in a headset, or a **360° panorama** that imagines the rest of the place around you. A Quest 3 or 3S does the relief itself, for any flat photo. Everything runs on your own computer and headset: no cloud, no account, your originals never changed. New in build 32 and desktop build 13, experimental and on by default. The Immich app has nothing like it.

**On your computer**, with desktop build 13 on Windows, Linux or macOS:

1. Select one or more photos in the library or in a folder, then "Make 3D (AI)".
2. Pick what you want: "3D photo", "3D scene", "360° panorama", or "3D version of a 360° photo" for your own 360° shots. The box says what AI will estimate or invent ("About 96% of this panorama will be invented by AI"), about how long it takes on this computer, and where the file goes: "Next to the original" or "A folder of my choice". A type this computer cannot do stays greyed, with its reason, such as "Not yet on Mac" or "The graphics card has 6.0 GiB of memory, 360° panoramas need 7.5 GiB".
3. The first time, the box offers the components of that type: Download, once. They run on this computer and work offline afterwards.
4. Convert. The jobs queue on two lanes, the processor and the graphics card, with their steps and the time left; Cancel stops one at any moment, and quitting the app asks first.
5. Open the result. A 3D photo shows as "Left eye", "Side by side", "Wiggle" (the animation at the top of this page) or "Red and cyan" for glasses; a scene has "Preview in 3D", turned with the mouse or the touchpad, and "Export for other 3D apps (.glb)"; a panorama opens in the 360° viewer.

Every new file goes next to its original, never over it, or into a folder of your choice when the original's folder takes no new file; on a network folder, only once you say yes for that folder ("Immuch360 writes new files there, never the originals"). The library links each version to its original with its badge, "3D AI", "3D scene" or "360° AI", in the album "Immuch360 AI versions", which stays out of your Immich backup unless you turn on "Also back up AI versions"; careful, "Select all" in the backup albums picks this album too and turns that switch on. To see a file in the Quest, save it on your NAS, back it up to your Immich server or, on Windows, turn on "Share this computer".

**In a Meta Quest**, with build 32:

- **AI relief**: open any flat photo, from your library or a share, and press "AI relief" in the top bar: the headset estimates the relief itself and shows the photo in 3D, "Relief estimated by AI". The model, 99.5 MB, downloads the first time you press it, and the reliefs are kept in a cache. "Open AI reliefs in 2.5D (experimental)", in Settings, Advanced, "AI and 3D", makes near objects move in front of the background as you move your head. On a Quest 2 or a Quest Pro the button stays greyed, "Not tested on this headset yet", until you turn on "AI relief on Quest 2 and Quest Pro (untested)" there.
- **3D photos and scenes from the computer**: "View in 3D" shows a 3D photo in true 3D. "View the 3D scene" on the original, or "Explore the 3D scene" on the scene, puts the scene in your room: lean in, or glide with the left thumbstick inside the rebuilt area ("Room to move: about 0.5 m each way" for the room at the top of this page); the layers flatten and darken at its edge, a click on a thumbstick resets the view, and B or Y goes back.
- **Panoramas**: a generated panorama opens all around you, facing your photo, with "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and a thin outline of the original photo, which fade after a few seconds; its 3D version is in true 3D.

Not yet tried in a headset by the author: the first headset test is on 30 October 2026. The headset's code for the relief, the scenes and the panoramas passed its tests on a computer, and what you see in your own Quest is welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues).

**On phones, tablets and TVs**, the files a computer made open as they are, and nothing is generated there. A panorama opens in the 360° viewer with the "360° AI" badge, the sentence and the outline on Android phones, tablets and Android TV; on iPhone, iPad, LG and Samsung TVs it opens as a plain 360° photo for now. A scene shows as its photo, with a small 3D SCENE mark in its corner; on Android, its details add "Open it on a Meta Quest to explore it". A 3D photo shows its two eyes side by side.

**What is real and what is not, always said.** It is the point, not the small print. The relief of a 3D photo is estimated from one photo, and what the photo hides is filled in and is not real. A scene is "Reconstructed from one photo, explore within its limits". A panorama keeps your real pixels where they were and invents the rest, about 90% of the sphere for a typical photo, and the box says how much before you start. The app says it where you look, with "Relief estimated by AI", "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and the badges, and the files say it too: in our XMP, and for the scenes and the panoramas in the IPTC digital source type of an image made with AI, so that other apps can tell a generated panorama from a real 360° photo. Your originals are never changed, and nothing leaves your computer or your headset. As the box puts it: "A finished conversion does not guarantee a good result."

**What was checked**, by 29 October 2026, with test builds of this code: 3D photos by the Linux app in a container on a Linux PC, offline too; scenes by the Windows app on a laptop with an RTX 4060 Laptop GPU (ten in 2 min 39 s) and by the engine on the Linux PC; on that laptop, four panoramas in 4 min 16 s to 4 min 44 s each, their 3D versions in about 30 s, with Cancel, a killed app, another program holding the graphics card, and no network connection of the app during the tests. The author has not judged the panoramas in a headset yet. Not tried: a Mac, a Linux PC with a dedicated graphics card, AMD and Intel graphics cards for the panoramas, a phone or a TV opening these files, and anything in a headset.

Limits: no 360° panorama on a Mac yet ("Not yet on Mac"), nor without a suitable graphics card, and the box says why ("No graphics card with Vulkan found", "Integrated graphics share the computer's memory: too slow for panoramas", with "Try on integrated graphics (untested)" to try anyway); inside a panorama, the edge of your photo can show as a sharp step in tone; most scenes say "Field of view guessed: shapes may be off"; a scene needs about 2 GB of free memory ("Not enough memory" otherwise); with AI versions backed up, Immich's duplicate finder pairs each photo with its scene and often suggests keeping the scene, the heavier file: keep the original. Phones and TVs make nothing, and the headset makes reliefs only; a scene comes from one photo, not yet from several photos of the same place.

**The components**: on the computer, Settings, "AI and 3D" lists the components of each feature with their size, Download and Remove, then "Remove all AI components" and "Move the AI components" to another drive; "AI features" off hides every AI button and downloads nothing. Each file is checked by its SHA-256. On Windows they sit in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.aprogsys\Immuch360 Desktop\ai-components`, which the uninstaller deletes (not a folder you moved them to); on Linux in `~/.local/share/immuch360/ai-components` on most systems, and on a Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/com.aprogsys.immuch360.desktop/ai-components`. In the Quest, "AI and 3D" is in Settings, Advanced, with the relief cache.

### Ingia kwenye televisheni, kifaa cha kichwa au kompyuta kwa simu yako

**Changanua. Ingia. Tazama.** Kuandika anwani ya seva, barua pepe na nenosiri kwa kidhibiti cha mbali cha televisheni, au ndani ya kifaa cha kichwa, huchukua dakika kadhaa. Ukiwa na Immuch360 kwenye simu yako, iliyoingia kwenye seva yako ya Immich, televisheni, Meta Quest au kompyuta inaingia ndani ya sekunde chache: skrini kubwa inaonyesha msimbo wa QR, simu inauchanganua, unathibitisha, na skrini inafunguka kwenye maktaba yako. Kwa programu ya simu, nenosiri lako linabaki kwenye simu. Programu ya Immich haina kitu kama hiki.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/sign-in-with-your-phone.png" width="720" alt="Mchoro, si picha ya skrini: televisheni inaonyesha msimbo wa QR kwenye ukurasa wake wa kuingia, na simu iliyoshikiliwa mbele yake inachanganua msimbo huo huo">
</p>

1. Kwenye skrini kubwa (Android TV na Google TV, dirisha la Meta Quest, Windows, Linux au macOS), chagua "Unganisha kwenye seva ya Immich": ukurasa wa kuingia unaonyesha "Ingia kwa simu yako", msimbo wa QR na msimbo wa herufi 10, kando ya fomu ya seva.
2. Kwenye simu, iliyoingia kwenye seva yako: Mipangilio, "Unganisha televisheni au kompyuta", kisha "Changanua msimbo". Kwenye Meta Quest, ambayo skrini yake haiwezi kuchanganuliwa na simu, chagua "Weka msimbo badala yake" na uandike msimbo unaoonyeshwa na kifaa cha kichwa.
3. Simu inauliza "Ingiza (skrini)?" na kuonyesha nambari ya tarakimu sita: hakikisha skrini inaonyesha nambari ileile, kisha gusa Ingia. Skrini inafunguka kwenye rekodi yako ya matukio.

Jinsi inavyobaki salama: simu na skrini lazima ziwe kwenye mtandao uleule wa nyumbani, na msimbo unadumu dakika mbili kwa zaidi na unafanya kazi mara moja tu. Simu inaomba seva yako kipindi kipya kwa skrini hiyo, kilichopewa jina lake ("Immuch360 kwenye televisheni ya sebuleni"), na kukituma kwa skrini kikiwa kimesimbwa, kwa hivyo nenosiri lako halitoki kamwe kwenye simu. "Unganisha televisheni au kompyuta" huorodhesha skrini ambazo simu hii imeziingiza, kila moja ikiwa na Toka, na Immich huziorodhesha katika Mipangilio ya akaunti, Vifaa vilivyoidhinishwa, ambapo unaweza pia kuzibatilisha. Upande wa simu unafanya kazi kwenye Android sasa, na kwenye iPhone na iPad kuanzia toleo lijalo la iOS. Bila Immuch360 kwenye simu, changanua msimbo kwa kamera ya simu: ukurasa unaotolewa na skrini unafunguka kwenye kivinjari, kwa barua pepe yako na nenosiri lako, vilivyosimbwa na kutumika mara moja. Televisheni za LG huingia kwa msimbo wao wa QR wenyewe, kwenye kivinjari cha simu; kuanzia build 28 kichanganuzi cha programu ya simu kinautambua na kufungua ukurasa huo. Televisheni za Samsung zinaingia kwa kuandika kwa sasa.

### Endelea na maktaba yako ya Immich, pamoja na zaidi

Ukiwa na seva ya [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich), Immuch360 ni programu ya simu ya Immich, yenye seva ileile na akaunti ileile kama programu ya wavuti: hifadhi rudufu, rekodi ya matukio, albamu, utafutaji, watu, kumbukumbu, kushiriki, washirika, zote zikilandanishwa na seva yako. Kwenye ukurasa wa kukaribisha, gusa "Unganisha kwenye seva ya Immich", kisha ingia kwa anwani ya seva yako, barua pepe yako na nenosiri lako, kama kwenye programu ya Immich; Rudi inarudisha kwenye ukurasa wa kukaribisha. 3. Ili kuonyesha picha ya 360° kwa mtu asiye na programu, ishiriki kwa kiungo cha kushiriki cha Immich: programu ya wavuti ya Immich inaionyesha kama tufe kwenye kivinjari chao.

The current build, build 32, is based on Immich `main` of 10 October 2026 (version 3.3.0-rc.0), like builds 28 to 31; build 28 was tested against a server built from that same Immich `main`, and build 27 also against an Immich 3.2.4 server. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues), not to the Immich project.

Inasikiliza tu. Hakuna amri inayotumwa kamwe kwa kidhibiti cha mbali wala kwa droni: baiti moja ambayo ukurasa huandika kila sekunde, kuanzia sekunde 20 baada ya kidhibiti cha mbali kufunguliwa, inaonyesha tu kama kidhibiti cha mbali kinapokea data, na haibebi amri yoyote.

Kwenye skrini ya moja kwa moja: betri ya droni na muda wa injini na, angani, urefu juu ya mahali pa kuruka, kasi ya wima, kasi na umbali chini ya droni. Thamani inaonekana hapo tu baada ya rekodi zetu wenyewe za safari kuithibitisha: urefu, kasi na umbali chini vilithibitishwa kwenye rekodi za safari mbili za ndani tarehe 25 Oktoba, hadi urefu wa 1.8 m na 1.5 m/s, bado si zaidi ya hapo; kuvionyesha hapo ni jambo jipya katika build 30 na linajaribiwa kwa mara ya kwanza nayo. Urefu unaonekana takriban 1.5 s baada ya kuruka, droni ikishaondoka ardhini au mkononi, na unabaki umefichwa hadi injini zisimame ikiwa wakati wowote utaruka kwa njia ambayo hakuna chombo cha anga kinachoweza. Advanced inaongeza volteji na halijoto ya betri, mwelekeo (sifuri yake haijakaguliwa dhidi ya kaskazini) na injini, na Diagnostics kila namba ambayo kiungo kinatoa, ikiwemo mkondo wa betri. Hakuna setilaiti, mahali wala umbali kutoka nyumbani vinavyoonyeshwa. Namba bado hazijalinganishwa na DJI Fly: usirushe droni kwa kuzitegemea.

**Pete ya vizuizi**, imewashwa kwa chaguo-msingi: vihisi vya droni vinapoona kitu karibu, duara angavu linaonekana kuzunguka katikati ya picha, pamoja na tao linaloelekea kizuizi, la rangi ya kahawia ya dhahabu linalogeuka jekundu kadiri kinavyokaribia, umbali wake kando yake na mlio mfupi unaoongeza kasi; kukiwa hakuna kitu karibu, hakuna kinachoonekana. Duara na mwelekeo wa tao vilikaguliwa angani, ndani ya nyumba, tarehe 25 Oktoba, kwenye build ya majaribio ambapo mwelekeo ulikuwa chaguo; ikionyeshwa kwa chaguo-msingi, inajaribiwa kwa mara ya kwanza na build 30. Kwenye Advanced unaweka umbali ("Glow from", 3.0 m, na "Strong glow from", 1.0 m), unachagua fremu kwenye kingo za picha badala ya duara, au unazima pete, sauti yake au mtetemo wake. Inaonyesha tu kile ambacho vihisi vya droni vinaripoti: hakuna kitu katika hali ya Sport au kuepuka vizuizi kukiwa kumezimwa, na kutokuwepo kwa mng'ao hakumaanishi njia iko wazi. Taarifa tu: Immuch360 kamwe haiongozi wala kusimamisha droni.

1. Kigae cha Drone kipo kwenye Maktaba, na "DJI live video" kwenye ukurasa wake: "DJI live video (experimental)", kwenye Settings, Advanced, imewashwa kwa chaguo-msingi.
2. Washa droni na kidhibiti cha mbali, funga DJI Fly, kisha fungua "DJI live video" na usome ilani ya usalama.
3. Chomeka kidhibiti cha mbali kwenye simu kwa kebo yake. Android ikiuliza ni programu gani inapaswa kukifungua, chagua Immuch360 bila "Kila wakati", jambo ambalo lingechukua kidhibiti cha mbali kutoka kwa DJI Fly; ikiuliza hali ya USB, chagua kuchaji tu. Baada ya hapo, kuchomeka kidhibiti cha mbali kunafungua skrini hii yenyewe ("Open this screen when the remote is plugged in", imewashwa kwa chaguo-msingi kwenye Advanced).
4. Remote, Drone na Picture huwaka kwa zamu, na picha inakuja kwenye skrini nzima ndani ya sekunde chache. Vitufe vitatu (kutoka, "Send to a headset or a TV" na Advanced) na thamani viko kwenye mikanda myeusi kando ya picha. Maelezo, kama "Live", hufifia baada ya takriban sekunde tano; mguso popote huyarudisha, na hakuna kitu kwenye picha kinachoathiri droni. Hitilafu inayokuomba uchukue hatua hubaki.
5. Advanced ina "Save a capture for a bug report", inayoandika rekodi ya kiungo kwenye `Download/Immuch360/dji-link`, "Play a saved capture", ili kuicheza tena bila kidhibiti cha mbali, na Diagnostics, pamoja na kila namba ambayo kiungo kinatoa.

Picha na video za droni haziwezi kufikiwa kupitia kidhibiti cha mbali, ambacho hakifungui kumbukumbu ya droni: zinakili kutoka kwenye droni yenyewe kupitia USB au kutoka kwenye kadi yake, kama [hapa chini](#bring-a-drones-or-a-camera-cards-photos-and-videos-to-immich). Weka rekodi zilizohifadhiwa kwako mwenyewe, kama ukurasa unavyosema: zina video ya droni na nambari za mfululizo za kidhibiti cha mbali na za chombo cha anga, na zinaweza kuwa na mahali kilipo; ripoti kwenye [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) pamoja na kile Diagnostics inachoonyesha inasaidia, hasa kwa vidhibiti vingine vya mbali vya DJI.

<a id="watch-it-in-a-meta-quest-or-on-an-android-tv"></a>
#### Itazame kwenye Meta Quest au kwenye Android TV

Simu iliyochomekwa kwenye kidhibiti cha mbali inaweza kutuma picha na thamani za droni kwa Immuch360 kwenye Meta Quest au kwenye Android TV au Google TV kwenye mtandao uleule wa ndani, kwa watu walio pamoja nawe. Kifaa cha kichwa kinazionyesha kwenye dirisha au, kubwa zaidi, kwenye mtazamo wa safari mbele yako, na televisheni kwenye skrini nzima, pamoja na thamani na pete ya vizuizi zilezile kama simu. Wao hutazama tu: hakuna kitu ambacho kifaa cha kichwa au televisheni inafanya kinachofika kwenye kidhibiti cha mbali au droni. Maelezo yao, kama "Kutazama tu", hufifia baada ya sekunde chache na kurudi kwa kitufe, mguso au kibonye. Picha ya droni ilifika kwenye Quest 3 mara moja, tarehe 24 Oktoba, kupitia Wi-Fi ya nyumbani; dirisha la kifaa cha kichwa na mtazamo wa safari pamoja na thamani na pete ya vizuizi vinajaribiwa kwa mara ya kwanza na build 30, na Quest 2, Pro na 3S hazijajaribiwa. Duara likificha katikati ya picha kwenye kifaa cha kichwa, "Fremu kwenye kingo" kwenye menyu yake inaacha katikati wazi. Kwenye Android TV ya TCL, kuoanisha na picha vilijaribiwa tarehe 25 Oktoba, kukiwa na kukwama na rangi ya kijani kwenye upitishaji wa awali; tarehe 26 Oktoba, kwa build 30, baada ya kubadili kwenda mtiririko thabiti, televisheni iliendelea kuonyesha picha wakati wote wa safari ambapo droni ilituma 18 Mbit/s, kukiwa na kuganda mara 7, kurefu zaidi 1.7 s; jinsi picha ilivyoonekana haikuandikwa. Kuanzia build 31 ilani ya usalama kwenye televisheni inashikilia uteuzi wa kidhibiti cha mbali (imekaguliwa kwa majaribio ya widget, bado si kwenye televisheni hiyo); katika build 30 mwonekano ulio chini yake ungeweza kuchukua uteuzi, hivyo ilani haikuweza kusomwa wala kukubaliwa, na njia pekee ya kutoka ilikuwa kitufe cha Nyuma, kinachoacha ukurasa. Kurusha droni huku ukitazama kifaa cha kichwa au televisheni kunahitaji mtazamaji karibu nawe anayeiona droni muda wote (kundi la wazi la EU); bila mtu huyo, tazama droni.

1. Kwenye simu, kwenye skrini ya moja kwa moja, gusa "Send to a headset or a TV" na uiwashe.
2. **Meta Quest**: fungua Immuch360, Maktaba, Drone, na uchague simu; andika msimbo ambao simu inaonyesha, mara moja kwa simu hiyo. Picha inafunguka kwenye dirisha; "Open the flight view" inaifanya kubwa zaidi, na B au Y hurudisha dirisha.
3. **Android TV na Google TV**: fungua Immuch360, Maktaba, Drone. Chini ya "Let this TV watch", televisheni inaonyesha msimbo na msimbo wake wa QR: kwenye simu, kwenye paneli ileile, gusa "Add a TV" na uuchanganue, au uuandike. Kisha chagua simu kwenye televisheni na ubonyeze OK: picha inakuja kwenye skrini nzima; OK huonyesha au huficha namba, na Menu hufungua chaguo.
4. Simu na skrini lazima ziwe kwenye mtandao uleule wa ndani. Ukiwa mbali na nyumbani, washa hotspot ya simu, kwenye bendi ya 5 GHz ikiwa inaitoa, na ujiunge nayo kutoka kifaa cha kichwa au televisheni.
5. Kwa ripoti ya hitilafu, menyu ya kifaa cha kichwa na Options ya televisheni zina Diagnostics na "Save a reception record", inayohifadhi kile skrini hiyo ilichopokea.

**Ubora wa kupeleka**, jipya katika build 30, kwenye Mipangilio ya kina ya simu: angani droni hutuma takriban 18 Mbit/s, na tarehe 25 Oktoba njia ya Wi-Fi ya nyumbani kutoka simu kupitia ruta hadi televisheni ilibeba mtiririko huo kamili theluthi moja tu ya muda wa safari; muda uliobaki picha ya televisheni ilikwama, ilivunjika na kupata rangi ya kijani. Kwa Kiotomatiki, chaguo-msingi, skrini ambayo Wi-Fi yake haiwezi kwenda sambamba inapata picha thabiti ambayo simu inaisimba upya kwa ajili yake, hadi 1080p kwa picha 30 kwa sekunde na kwa kasi ambayo Wi-Fi yake inabeba, iliyoundwa ibaki safi, yenye maelezo machache zaidi, badala ya kuvunjika; inarudi kwenye picha ya droni yenyewe katika kutua au kuruka kunakofuata, mara Wi-Fi yake inapokuwa imebeba picha thabiti kwa 30 s bila kukwama; tarehe 26 Oktoba Wi-Fi ya televisheni hiyo haikuweza kamwe, na televisheni ilibaki kwenye picha thabiti, kwa picha 15 kwa sekunde kwa sekunde 44 kati ya 59 zake. Mtiririko kamili daima hutuma picha ya droni jinsi inavyokuja, Mtiririko thabiti daima picha ya simu. Picha thabiti iliendeshwa kwa mara ya kwanza kwenye simu tarehe 26 Oktoba, kwa build 30: Galaxy S24+, ikiisimba kwa HEVC ya maunzi kwa kiwango kisichobadilika cha biti. Televisheni iliyo kwenye kebo ya Ethernet, au skrini iliyojiunga na hotspot ya simu yenyewe, inabaki na hatua moja ya Wi-Fi badala ya mbili.

Si katika build hii: droni kwenye iPhone na iPad (haijaanzishwa), upitishaji kwa televisheni za LG na Samsung, na uthibitisho wa thamani zaidi ya 1.8 m na 1.5 m/s, kisha setilaiti na umbali kutoka nyumbani, vinavyosubiri safari ya nje.

#### Leta picha na video za droni au za kadi ya kamera kwenye Immich

Ingiza picha na video za droni ya DJI kupitia USB, moja kwa moja kwenye seva yako ya Immich: chomeka droni au kisomaji cha kadi kwenye simu ya Android, chagua faili mpya na uzitume, huku video zikiwekwa kwenye ramani kutokana na data ya safari ya droni na tufe za 360° za droni zikionyeshwa kama 360°. Programu ya Immich huhifadhi nakala rudufu ya kile kilicho tayari kwenye albamu ya simu tu, na seva ya Immich bado haiwezi kusoma mahali pa video ya DJI Neo 2. Ukurasa huu ni wa majaribio, upo kwenye simu na kompyuta kibao za Android tu, na bado haujajaribiwa kwa droni halisi.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/dji-drone-import.png" width="720" alt="Mchoro, si picha ya skrini: droni juu ya meza ya baraza, iliyounganishwa na simu kwa kebo, na picha na video zake zikienda kwa tao kutoka droni hadi albamu ya simu">
</p>

1. Fungua kigae cha Drone kwenye Maktaba, kisha "Photos and videos from the drone". Mpangilio wake, "DJI drones (experimental)" kwenye Settings, Advanced, umewashwa kwa chaguo-msingi; uzime hapo ili kuficha ukurasa huo.
2. Chomeka droni (Neo 2 inaweza kubaki imezimwa) au kisomaji cha kadi kwenye simu kwa kebo ya data ya USB-C; kidhibiti cha mbali cha DJI kisicho na skrini (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3) hakina picha. Kwenye ukurasa wa DJI drone, gusa "Choose the drone or the card", ichague kwenye menyu ya pembeni ya kichaguzi, fungua folda yake ya DCIM au acha hifadhi nzima, kisha "Tumia folda hii" na Ruhusu.
3. Ukurasa unaorodhesha picha na video kwa siku, bila faili saidizi za .LRF, .SRT, .THM na .m4a. Gusa "Select all new", kisha "Pakia kwenye Immich"; faili zilizotumwa awali zina alama ya wingu na zinarukwa. "Keep on the device" badala yake hunakili faili kwenye albamu ya simu, katika albamu "Immuch360 import".
4. Ili video iwekwe kwenye ramani, washa Video Subtitles kwenye DJI Fly (hali ya video, Settings, Camera) kabla ya kurusha droni: droni kisha huandika faili ya .SRT kando ya kila video, programu husoma mahali kutoka humo na kupaweka kwenye seva yako baada ya kupakia, wakati Immich haikupata mahali.

Tufe za 360° za droni za DJI (Mini, Air, Mavic na nyingine, zinazotambuliwa kutokana na kamera yao) zinaonekana kama 360° kwenye Immuch360 hata bila lebo za 360°. Immich inahitaji lebo hizo: chaguo la upakiaji "Tag drone spheres as 360° for Immich", lililozimwa kwa chaguo-msingi, hutuma picha bila kuibadilisha pamoja na faili ndogo ya .xmp kando yake, ili Immich nayo iionyeshe kama tufe. Neo 2 haina hali ya tufe wala ya panorama: picha na video zake ni bapa. Simu iliporekodi sauti ya klipu ya Neo 2, sauti hiyo iko kwenye faili tofauti ya .m4a: ukurasa unaiwekea klipu alama, na Immich inaipokea bila sauti (ipakue kwa DJI Fly ili kubaki na sauti). Kadi yoyote ya kamera yenye folda ya DCIM inafanya kazi vivyo hivyo kwenye kisomaji cha kadi. Hakuna kinachotumwa kwa DJI.

Zaidi: [droni za DJI kupitia USB](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#dji-drones-photos-and-videos-over-usb-experimental), [uchunguzi wa kiungo cha DJI](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#dji-link-diagnostics-experimental).

### Fungua faili ghafi za kamera za 360°

Picha ghafi za .insp na video za .insv za kamera za Insta360 (lenzi moja au mbili, katika njia moja, njia mbili au faili mbili), faili za .360 za GoPro MAX na faili za .osv za DJI Osmo 360 zinashonwa na programu wakati zinachezwa, pamoja na urekebishaji ulioandikwa kwenye faili, bila chochote cha kusakinisha popote.

1. Weka faili ghafi mahali programu inapozisoma: hifadhi ya SMB au WebDAV, seva yako ya Immich (inapokea .insp na .insv tu), au folda za kompyuta. Albamu ya simu ya Android haiorodheshi faili ghafi, hivyo faili ghafi iliyonakiliwa kwenye simu haionekani kwenye albamu yake wala kwenye programu.
2. Weka pamoja faili mbili za rekodi iliyoundwa na faili mbili: programu inatafuta nyingine kando ya ya kwanza.
3. Fungua faili na gusa 360°. Picha inaonyesha "Faili ghafi ya 360°, imeshonwa na programu" pamoja na chanzo cha urekebishaji wake; video inashonwa wakati inachezwa.

Linux na macOS hucheza video ghafi bapa, na faili za .dng za fisheye mbili zinaonyeshwa bapa kwa sasa.

Zaidi: [faili ghafi za kamera za 360°](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#raw-360-camera-files-without-the-cameras-app).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Zungusha picha kwa kitufe kimoja

Zungusha picha kwa 90° kwa kubonyeza mara moja, bila kufungua kihariri: kitufe kwenye skrini, kitufe cha kibodi au kitufe cha rangi cha kidhibiti cha mbali cha televisheni, kwenye kila jukwaa. Programu ya Immich huzungusha picha ndani ya kihariri chake; Immuch360 hufanya hivyo kwa kitufe kimoja, na kwa picha zako mwenyewe kwenye seva ya Immich 2.6 au toleo jipya zaidi hupitia kihariri hichohicho cha seva, hivyo programu ya wavuti na vifaa vyako vingine vinazionyesha zikiwa zimezungushwa. Picha nyingine yoyote huzungushwa kwenye kifaa hiki tu. Picha za 360°, 180° na 3D hazizungushwi kamwe, ili mitazamo ya kuzamisha ibaki imenyooka.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Mchoro, si picha ya skrini: simu inaonyesha picha ikizunguka robo mzunguko kwa mwelekeo wa saa, juu ya safu ya vitufe vya duara huku kitufe cha kuzungusha kikiwaka">
</p>

1. **Simu, kompyuta kibao na dirisha la Meta Quest**: fungua picha na gusa Rotate kwenye safu ya chini, kabla ya Edit, ili kuizungusha kisaa; kubonyeza kwa muda mrefu huizungusha upande mwingine. Kwa kibodi, R na Shift+R.
2. **Android TV na Google TV**: kitufe cha njano cha kidhibiti cha mbali huzungusha picha kisaa na kitufe cha bluu kinyume cha saa, popote ulipo uteuzi ndani ya kitazamaji. Kwenye kidhibiti cha mbali kisicho na vitufe vya rangi, bonyeza Chini: upau ulio chini ya picha unaonekana ukiwa umechagua Rotate (hatua moja ya Kulia zaidi picha ikiwa na kitufe cha maandishi), na kila OK huzungusha picha.
3. **Televisheni za LG (webOS)**: njano na bluu kama kwenye Android TV, R na Shift+R kwenye kibodi, au kitufe cha kuzungusha kwenye vidhibiti vya picha. Televisheni za Samsung pia huzungusha picha kwa kitufe kimoja.
4. **Windows, Linux na macOS**: R huzungusha kisaa, Shift+R kinyume cha saa, au kitufe cha kuzungusha kwenye upau wa juu, kabla ya skrini nzima.
5. **Picha za hifadhi ya mtandao, Plex au kamera**: kitufe cha kuzungusha kikiwa cha kwanza kwenye upau wa juu, R na Shift+R, au vitufe vya njano na bluu.

Mahali mzunguko unapohifadhiwa: picha ya akaunti yako mwenyewe kwenye Immich 2.6 au toleo jipya zaidi huzungushwa kupitia kihariri cha picha cha seva, ambacho huhifadhi faili asili na kutengeneza picha iliyozungushwa (upau mwembamba unaonekana kinapofanya kazi; mizunguko minne, au mzunguko mmoja wa kurudi, hurejesha picha asili). Picha za kifaa, za hifadhi, za mshirika au za albamu iliyoshirikiwa, na kila picha kwenye seva ya zamani zaidi huzungushwa kwenye kifaa hiki tu, kwa picha hiyo, na faili haibadilishwi kamwe; programu inasema hivyo mara moja kwa picha ya Immich. Kwenye programu za Android, iOS na kompyuta, mzunguko uliohifadhiwa kwenye kifaa unaonekana kwenye kitazamaji, lakini bado si kwenye vijipicha, onyesho la slaidi na kumbukumbu. Video, folda iliyofungwa na kila picha ya 360°, 180° au 3D hazina Rotate.

<a id="use-your-computer-as-the-media-player"></a>
### Tumia kompyuta yako kama kicheza midia

Kwenye kompyuta ya Windows, Immuch360 Desktop husoma folda unazochagua bila seva yoyote, hucheza video bapa na za 360° kwenye kicheza chake yenyewe, huvinjari hifadhi na seva zilezile, na inaweza kushiriki kompyuta na kifaa cha kichwa nyumbani. Linux na macOS zinayo kama matoleo ya awali, pamoja na video za 360° zinazochezwa bapa.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/desktop-b4-video.jpg" width="440" alt="Kicheza video cha Immuch360 Desktop kwenye Windows: video ya barabara ikicheza ndani ya dirisha, pamoja na kitufe cha kusitisha, upau wa muda, Shiriki na Pakia kwenye Immich">
</p>

1. Sakinisha toleo la awali, au fungua ZIP yake na uanzishe `immuch360.exe`, tazama [Sakinisha](#windows). Kwenye ukurasa wa kukaribisha, "Tumia bila seva" inafungua uchaguzi wa folda zako, na "Unganisha kwenye seva ya Immich" ukurasa wa kuingia, pamoja na msimbo wake wa QR kwa simu yako.
2. Picha, video au folda inayofunguliwa na programu, au kudondoshwa kwenye dirisha lake, inafunguka papo hapo, ile ya 360° kwenye mtazamo wake wa 360°; faili kadhaa zinafunguka pamoja kwenye ukurasa mmoja. Picha na video zinaipata kwenye "Fungua kwa"; kamwe haijifanyi kuwa programu yao ya chaguo-msingi.
3. Kwenye kitazamaji, kibodi ndiyo inayoendesha: nafasi hucheza na kusitisha, J na L huenda nyuma na mbele, F ni skrini nzima, na R na Shift+R huzungusha picha.

Zaidi: [kwenye kompyuta ya Windows](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#on-a-windows-computer-immuch360-desktop-preview), [msaada wa jukwaa](../docs/immuch360/platform-support.md).

Immuch360 inaweka programu nzima ya simu ya Immich na kuongeza kile kilicho kuizunguka. Kwa ufupi:

| | Immuch360, ikilinganishwa na programu ya Immich |
|---|---|
| **Kilicho sawa** | seva yako ya Immich na akaunti: hifadhi rudufu, rekodi ya matukio, albamu, utafutaji, watu, kumbukumbu, kushiriki na washirika, vyote vikiwa vimelinganishwa na seva yako. Inasakinishwa kando ya programu rasmi. |
| **Seva haihitajiki** | inafanya kazi pia bila seva au akaunti, kwenye picha na video za kifaa, folda za kompyuta na hifadhi za mtandao; Plex na kamera hutumia kuingia kwao wenyewe, kamwe si akaunti ya Immuch360. |
| **360° na kuzamisha** | picha na video za 360° kama tufe, panorama, VR180 na 3D, mtazamo wa kuzamisha kwenye Meta Quest, beji ya 360° na orodha ya 360°. Kutazama 360° kwenye programu ya simu ya Immich kumeombwa tangu Januari 2024 na bado hakupo. |
| **3D from your photos** | on a computer, one photo becomes a 3D photo, a 3D scene or a 360° panorama, and in a Meta Quest 3 or 3S any flat photo gets its relief: made by AI on your own computer and headset, labelled as such in the app and in the files, the original never changed. The Immich app makes nothing in 3D. |
| **Kwenye televisheni** | programu ileile kwenye Android TV na Google TV, na programu ya televisheni kwa televisheni za LG, iliyofungashwa pia kwa televisheni za Samsung kama build ya majaribio. Programu za Immich zenyewe hazisakiniki kwenye televisheni ya LG. |
| **Kuingia kwa simu yako** | televisheni, kifaa cha kichwa au kompyuta inaingia simu yako inapochanganua msimbo wake wa QR, na nenosiri lako linabaki kwenye simu. Programu ya Immich haina kitu kama hiki. |
| **Hifadhi na Plex** | hifadhi za SMB, WebDAV na DLNA na seva za Plex, zinazochezwa mahali zilipo, na faili zake zinazotumwa kwa seva yako kwa mkono. |

## Faragha

Hakuna kinachofika kwa msanidi programu: hakuna akaunti, hakuna uchanganuzi, hakuna huduma ya kuripoti kuvunjika, hakuna matangazo. Programu inazungumza tu na seva unazochagua (seva yako ya Immich, hifadhi zako, seva yako ya Plex, kamera zako) na, bila seva, na chochote kabisa. Manenosiri na tokeni zinabaki kwenye hifadhi salama ya kifaa; televisheni za LG na Samsung hazina hifadhi kama hiyo kwa programu, hivyo huko zinabaki kwenye hifadhi ya programu yenyewe, bila usimbaji. Kamera inatumika kwenye kifaa tu, na kicheza cha Spatial 2.5D na kuchanganua msimbo wa skrini unayoiingiza; picha zake kamwe hazihifadhiwi na kamwe hazitumwi popote. Skrini iliyoingizwa kutoka kwa simu yako inapata kipindi chake yenyewe, kinachotumwa kikiwa kimesimbwa kwenye mtandao wako wa nyumbani, kamwe si nenosiri lako; televisheni ya LG huingizwa kupitia ukurasa kwenye kivinjari cha simu, ambao hutuma barua pepe na nenosiri lako kwa televisheni bila usimbaji kwenye mtandao wako wa nyumbani, na televisheni huhifadhi kipindi tu. Rekodi na kumbukumbu za upokeaji za kurasa za droni hubaki kwenye kifaa isipokuwa uzishiriki: zina video ya droni na zinaweza kuwa na mahali ilipo, na rekodi ina nambari za mfululizo za kidhibiti cha mbali na za chombo cha anga. Picha na thamani za droni huenda kutoka simu kwa vifaa vya kichwa na televisheni unazooanisha tu, kwenye mtandao wako wa ndani, bila usimbaji, kama ilivyo kwa simu inayoshirikiwa kwenye Wi-Fi. Hakuna kinachotumwa kwa DJI, wala kwa TP-Link. Sera kamili ipo kwenye [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), na maelezo kwa kila chanzo kwenye [mwongozo wa mtumiaji](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

**The AI features** work on your devices only. The photo, its relief, its scene and its panorama are computed on your computer or in your headset and sent nowhere. The app connects only to download a component you asked for, from the releases of this project on GitHub or, failing that, from the model's official page when it has one, checks each file by its SHA-256, and then works offline. ONNX Runtime, which runs the models on a computer, sends telemetry to Microsoft in its official builds; Immuch360 turns it off. The new files go next to your originals, never over them, and reach your Immich server only if you turn on "Also back up AI versions" ("Select all" in the backup albums turns it on too).
