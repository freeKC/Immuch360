# Immuch360

**Deng Medien. All Bildschierm. All Bléckwinkel.**

Immuch360 ass eng gratis App mat oppenem Quellcode, fir deng Fotoen a Videoen ze fannen, duerchzeschaffen, ofzespillen an z'entdecken: déi gewéinlech, awer och déi panoramesch, 360°, 3D an immersiv Medien, déi aner Apps flaach, duebel oder verzerrt weisen. Si liest se do wou se sinn, direkt um Apparat, op engem NAS oder Computer, op engem Plex-Server oder op engem Immich-Server, a spillt se op Handys, Tablets, Fernseher, Meta Quest-Headsets a Computeren of. Kee Kont, keng Cloud, kee Server néideg.

- **Wat mécht d'App?** Si weist deng Fotoen a Videoen als Galerie a spillt se am Viewer of, deen zu all Fichier passt: flaach, als Kugel an där s du ronderëm kuckst, a 3D, an engem Headset.
- **Wéi eng Medien?** Flaach Fotoen a Videoen, Panoramen, 360°-Fotoen a Videoen, VR180, stereoskopesch 3D, Apple-Raumfotoen, d'360°-Kugele vun DJI-Drohnen an d'rau Dateien vu 360°-Kameraen (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), déi vun der App zesummegesat ginn.
- **On which devices?** Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Android TV and Google TV, LG TVs with webOS and Samsung TVs with Tizen (a separate TV app, in test builds), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S, Windows (preview), and Linux and macOS as early previews.
- **Brauch ech e Server oder e Kont?** Nee. Immuch360 funktionnéiert eleng mat de Medien vun dengem Apparat. En Immich-Server, en NAS, e Medieserver oder e Plex-Server sinn Optiounen, déi s du derbäisetz wann s du wëlls.
- **Wéi mellen ech e Fernseh, en Headset oder e Computer un?** Mat dengem Handy: scann de QR-Code, deen d'Umeldesäit weist, oder tipp säi Code an, bestäteg, an et geet a puer Sekonnen op denger Immich-Bibliothéik op, ouni eppes mat enger Fernsteierung ze tippen.

<a id="what-you-can-do"></a>
## Wat s du maache kanns

All Benotzung hei drënner seet fir d'éischt wat s du kriss, duerno wéi s du et a puer Schrëtt méchs. Déi komplett Anleitung, mat all Optioun, Message a Limit, steet an der [Benotzerguide](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Deng Fotoen a Videoen kucken

Immuch360 is first a gallery and a player for ordinary photos and videos. The timeline, the albums, the favorites, the memories and the search work with an Immich server; the device's own photos and videos, and the folders of a computer, show without any server. Videos play in a native player with sound, seeking, a buffering indicator and a choice of audio tracks, in the 360°, Spatial and computer players and, from build 29, in the flat player of the Android app, which shows subtitles too. On a TV the remote drives everything; on a computer the mouse and the keyboard do.

1. Mach den Tab Fotoen op: den Zäitstral vun dengem Server, oder vum Apparat ouni Server.
2. Tipp op eng Foto oder eng Video fir se opzemaachen; wëschs no lénks a riets fir zur nächster ze goen, pinnts oder tippelsdoppel fir ze zoomen.
3. Wëschs no uewen op enger Video fir hir Detailer: Codec, Gréisst, Framerate, Bitrate, an ob dëse Apparat se decodéiere kann.
4. On Android phones and TVs, from build 29, a film with several audio languages or with subtitles has an audio button and a subtitles button in its controls: the audio language you pick is kept for the next films, and subtitles stay off until you pick them, except forced ones. The Immich app's Android player has neither.

Méi: [d'Viewers an den Videoplayer](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [Videodetailer a Decoderen](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Eng Foto mat enger Tast dréinen

Dréi eng Foto mat engem eenzegen Drock ëm 90°, ouni en Editeur opzemaachen: e Knäppchen, eng Tast vun der Tastatur oder eng Faarftast vun der TV-Fernsteierung, op all Plattform. D'Immich-App dréint eng Foto an hirem Editeur; Immuch360 mécht et mat enger Tast, a fir deng eege Fotoen op engem Immich-Server 2.6 oder méi nei geet et duerch dee selwechte Server-Editeur, sou datt d'Web-App an deng aner Apparater se gedréint weisen. All aner Foto gëtt nëmmen op dësem Apparat gedréint. 360°-, 180°- a 3D-Fotoe ginn ni gedréint, fir datt déi immersiv Usiichten waagerecht bleiwen.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360 Rotate 90°">
</p>

1. **Handys, Tablets an d'Meta-Quest-Fënster**: maach d'Foto op a tipp op Rotate an der ënneschter Rei, virun Edit, fir se mat dem Auerzeiger ze dréinen; e laangen Drock dréint se an déi aner Richtung. Mat enger Tastatur, R a Shift+R.
2. **Android TV a Google TV**: déi giel Tast vun der Fernsteierung dréint d'Foto mat dem Auerzeiger an déi blo Tast géint den Auerzeiger, egal wou de Fokus am Viewer ass. Op enger Fernsteierung ouni Faarftasten, dréck Erof: d'Leescht ënner der Foto erschéngt mat Rotate ausgewielt (eemol Riets méi wäit, wann d'Foto en Textknäppchen huet), an all OK dréint d'Foto.
3. **LG-Fernseher (webOS)**: giel a blo wéi op Android TV, R a Shift+R op enger Tastatur, oder den Dréiknäppchen an de Steierelementer vun der Foto.
4. **Windows, Linux a macOS**: R dréint mat dem Auerzeiger, Shift+R géint den Auerzeiger, oder den Dréiknäppchen an der ieweschter Leescht, virum Vollbild.
5. **Fotoe vun enger Netzwierkfreigab, vu Plex oder vun enger Kamera**: den Dréiknäppchen als éischten an der ieweschter Leescht, R a Shift+R, oder déi giel a blo Tasten.

Wou d'Dréiung gespäichert gëtt: eng Foto vun dengem eegene Kont op Immich 2.6 oder méi nei gëtt iwwer de Fotoeditor vum Server gedréint, deen d'Original-Datei behält an e gedréint Bild mécht (eng dënn Leescht weist sech, während e schafft; véier Dréiungen, oder eng Dréiung zréck, féieren zum Original zréck). D'Fotoe vum Apparat, vun enger Freigab, vun engem Partner oder engem gedeelten Album, an all Foto op engem eelere Server ginn nëmmen op dësem Apparat gedréint, fir déi Foto, an d'Datei gëtt ni geännert; d'App seet dat eemol fir eng Immich-Foto. An den Android-, iOS- a Computer-Appen weist sech eng um Apparat gespäichert Dréiung am Viewer, nach net an de Miniaturen, der Diashow an den Erënnerungen. Videoen, de gespaarten Dossier an all 360°-, 180°- oder 3D-Foto hunn kee Rotate.

<a id="explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d"></a>
### Panoramen, 360°, VR180 a 3D entdecken

Eng 360°-Foto geet op als Kugel, déi s du mat engem Fanger oder beim Beweegen vum Handy dréis; eng 360°-Video leeft an engem spheresche Player mat Toun; VR180-Dateien ginn op eng Hallefkugel gemoolt; stereoskopesch 3D-Dateien weisen een Aa op engem flaache Bildschierm a béid Aen an engem Headset. Deelweis Panoramen behalen hiren Ausschnëtt, an d'Bild fänkt mat der Usiicht un, déi d'Kamera opgeholl huet. En Fichier, deen den Server net markéiert, kann mat engem Tipp als 360° gewise ginn.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-sphere.jpg" width="260" alt="Eng 360°-Foto vun engem Séiuwer am Kugelbetruechter: de Knäppchen Zoumaachen uewe lénks, d'Knäppercher 360°, 3D-Layout a Gyroskop uewe riets">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-video.png" width="420" alt="En 360°-Video vun enger Küstestrooss am 360°-Player vun engem quer gehalenen Telefon: Zoumaachen an den Numm vum Fichier uewe lénks, 360° an 3D uewe riets, Viregt, Zréckspréngen, Ofspillen, Viraspréngen an Nächst an der Mëtt, d'Zäitleescht ënnen">
</p>

1. Mach d'Foto oder d'Video op. 360°-Dateien hunn en 360°-Badge op hirem Miniaturbild, an den Tab Bibliothéik huet eng 360°-Lëscht, déi se sammelt, mat Filteren no Period, Quell, Typ a Kamera.
2. Tipp op 360° an der ieweschter Leescht. Zéi fir ronderëm ze kucken, pinn fir ze zoomen, tipp op de Gyroskop-Knäppchen fir ronderëm ze kucken andeems s du den Handy dréis.
3. Den 3D-Knäppchen geet duerch „Mono (keen 3D)“, „3D, uewen an ënnen“ a „3D, nieftenee“; den 360°/180°-Knäppchen wiesselt tëscht der ganzer Kugel an der Hallefkugel vum VR180.
4. Fir en Fichier, deen flaach ugewise gëtt, obwuel en 360° ass: ⋮ uewen riets, duerno „Als 360° weisen“.

Op Handys a Tablets kann eng stereoskopesch Video och Déift um flaache Bildschierm kréien: den Spatial-2.5D-Player benotzt d'Frontkamera fir däin Kapp ze verfolgen a weist d'Bild tëscht deenen zwee Ae (experimentell). Apple-Raumfotoen, déi mat engem iPhone gemaach ginn, ginn erkannt an a 3D an engem Meta Quest gewisen.

Méi: [360°-Fotoen a Videoen](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#360-photos-and-videos-as-a-sphere), [3D a VR180](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#3d-and-vr180-photos-and-videos), [Spatial 2.5D](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#depth-on-a-flat-screen-spatial-25d-experimental), [Apple-Raumfotoen a -Videoen](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#apple-spatial-photos-and-videos), [d'360°-Lëscht](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list).

<a id="use-your-own-files-without-an-account"></a>
### Deng eegen Dateien benotzen, ouni Kont

Immuch360 brauch weder e Kont nach en Server. Zënter dem Build 24 geet d'App op enger Wëllkommssäit op, wou "Ouni Server benotzen" als éischt kënnt: si mécht d'App op mat de Fotoen a Videoen vum Apparat, mat all Viewer, der 360°-Lëscht an den Netzwierkquellen. Näischt verléisst den Apparat. En Server kann méi spéit verbonnen ginn, vun den Astellungen oder vum Tab Bibliothéik, an d'Serverfunktiounen erschéngen dann.

<img src="../.github/readme/b19-library-no-server.png" width="260" alt="D'Bibliothéik ouni Server: den 360°-Element uewen, dann On this device mat zwou 360°-Fotoen, an Netzwierkfreeginn mat enger Freeginn mam Numm NAS">

1. Mach d'App op. D'Wëllkommssäit weist "Deng Medien. All Bildschierm. All Bléckwinkel." a dräi Méiglechkeeten: tipp op déi éischt, "Ouni Server benotzen". Bis zum Build 23 ass d'Login-Säit fir d'éischt komm, mat "Ouni Server benotzen" ënner dem Formulaire.
2. Erlaabt den Zougrëff op Är Fotoen a Videoen. Wann Dir refuséiert, seet d'App "D'App gesäit d'Fotoen vun dësem Apparat net", mat "Zougrëff erlaben" an "Astellungen opmaachen".
3. Den Tab Photos (Fotoen) weist d'Timeline vum Apparat. Search (Sichen) an Albums (Alben) bleiwe gro, well se vum Server kommen. Den Tab Bibliothéik huet uewen 360°, dann On this device (Op dësem Apparat, d'Alben vum Apparat) an Netzwierkfreeginn.
4. D'Menü uewe riets seet "Nëmmen dësen Apparat": d'App weist d'Fotoen a Videoe vun dësem Apparat, näischt gëtt iergendwouhi geschéckt.

Op engem Computer spillen d'Dossieren, déi s du auswiels, d'Roll vun der Galerie; op engem Fernseh, deen keng eegen Fotoen huet, maachen dat d'Netzwierkquellen.

Méi: [ouni Server oder Kont](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#without-a-server-or-an-account).

<a id="reach-the-media-of-a-nas-a-computer-or-a-media-server"></a>
### Un d'Medien vun engem NAS, engem Computer oder engem Medieserver kommen

Deng Videoen leien op engem NAS, engem Computer oder engem Medieserver. Immuch360 fënnt d'Servere vun denger Netzwierk eleng, duerchsicht se a spillt hir Fotoen a Videoen op der Plaz of, a all Viewer vun der App, ouni eppes ze kopéieren: SMB (Samba, Windows-Freigaben), WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology an aner) an DLNA-Medieserver (Jellyfin, Emby, minidlna, en NAS, eng TV-Box). ### D'Fotoen a Videoe vun denger DJI-Drohn op dengem Immich-Server

Import the photos and videos of a DJI drone over USB, straight to your Immich server: plug the drone or a card reader into an Android phone, pick the new files and send them, with the videos placed on the map from the drone's flight data and the drone's 360° spheres shown as 360°. The Immich app backs up only what is already in the phone's gallery, and the Immich server cannot read the place of a DJI Neo 2 video yet. This page is experimental, on Android phones and tablets only, and has not been tried with a real drone yet.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/dji-drone-import.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360, a DJI drone and Immich">
</p>

1. Eng Kachel „DJI-Drohn“ ass an der Bibliothéik, Netzwierkfreigaben: „DJI-Drohnen (experimentell)“ ass an den Astellungen, Erweidert standardméisseg ugeschalt. Schalt et do aus, fir d'Kachel ze verstoppen.
2. Plug the drone (a Neo 2 can stay off) or a card reader into the phone with a USB-C data cable; a DJI remote without a screen (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3) holds no photos. On the DJI drone page, tap "Choose the drone or the card", choose it in the side menu of the picker, open its DCIM folder or keep the whole drive, then "Use this folder" and Allow.
3. D'Säit lëscht d'Fotoen a Videoen no Dag op, ouni d'Niewendateien .LRF, .SRT, .THM a .m4a. Tipp op „All nei auswielen“, dann „Op Immich eroplueden“; déi virdru geschéckten Dateien droen e Wollekenzeechen a ginn iwwersprong. „Um Apparat behalen“ kopéiert d'Dateien amplaz an d'Galerie vum Handy, an den Album „Immuch360 import“.
4. Fir datt e Video op der Kaart landt, schalt virum Fluch Video Subtitles an DJI Fly un (Videomodus, Settings, Camera): d'Drohn schreift dann eng .SRT-Datei nieft all Video, d'App liest d'Plaz doraus a setzt se no dem Eroplueden op dengem Server, wann Immich keng fonnt huet.

D'360°-Kugele vun DJI-Drohnen (Mini, Air, Mavic an aner, erkannt un hirer Kamera) weise sech an Immuch360 als 360°, och ouni 360°-Tags. Immich brauch dës Tags: d'Upload-Optioun „Drohnekugelen fir Immich als 360° markéieren“, standardméisseg aus, schéckt d'Foto onverännert mat enger klenger .xmp-Datei dernieft, sou datt Immich se och als Kugel weist. <a id="your-dji-drones-live-video-on-your-phone-experimental"></a>
### Your DJI drone's live video on your phone (experimental)

See what your DJI drone sees on your Android phone, without DJI Fly and without any account: plug a DJI RC-N3 remote controller into the phone with its cable, and Immuch360 shows the drone's live video, H.265 in 1080p, with the battery of the remote and of the drone, the signal, the gimbal and the flight data, read from the cable. The remote keeps full control of the drone. It was tried on the ground with an RC-N3, a DJI Neo 2 and a Samsung Galaxy S24+; other remotes and drones have not been tried. The flight and battery numbers are not compared with DJI Fly yet: do not fly by them.

Si lauschtert nëmmen. Ni gëtt e Befeel un d'Fernsteierung oder un d'Drohn geschéckt: deen eenzege Byte, deen d'Säit all Sekonn schreift, vun 20 Sekonnen nodeems d'Fernsteierung opgaangen ass, seet nëmmen, ob d'Fernsteierung Date kritt, an enthält kee Befeel.

1. Astellungen, Erweidert, "DJI-Link-Diagnos (experimentell)" ass standardméisseg ugeschalt (ni op engem Fernseh); d'Kachel "DJI-Link" ass an der Bibliothéik, Netzwierkfreigaben.
2. Turn on the drone and the remote, close DJI Fly, then open the DJI link tile and read the safety notice.
3. Tap "Start the capture", then plug in the remote with its cable. If Android asks which app should open it, choose Immuch360 without "Always", which would take the remote away from DJI Fly; if it asks for a USB mode, choose charging only.
4. The state reads "Accessory open", then "Remote frames arrive", and the video follows within a few seconds. The button on the picture shows it full screen, and the "Flight and battery" section gives the numbers of the remote and the drone.
5. "Export the capture" writes three files to `Download/Immuch360/dji-link`; "Play a capture" plays an export again on the page, without the remote, to see how well a phone or a headset decodes the drone's video.

The drone's photos and videos cannot be reached through the remote, which does not open the drone's storage: copy them from the drone itself over USB or from its card, as in [the section above](#your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server). On a Meta Quest the page is there and plays a capture, but the drone's live video does not reach the headset yet. Keep the exported files to yourself, as the page says, since they hold the serial number of the remote; a report in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with the states and counters the page shows helps, above all with other DJI remotes.

1. You need a Samsung TV with Tizen 5.5 or later (2020 models and later), a computer on the same network and a Samsung account. On the TV: Apps, App Settings, type 12345, turn Developer mode on, enter the IP address of the computer, then restart the TV.
2. Download `Immuch360-Tizen-0.1.0.wgt` from the [Tizen pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/tizen-v0.1.0) and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. A Samsung TV installs only an app signed for it, so the package must be signed again with a Samsung certificate that lists the DUID of your TV: [Apps2Samsung](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung), a community tool, reads the DUID, signs the package with your Samsung account and installs it; Tizen Studio with the Samsung Certificate Extension does the same by hand.
3. Open the app and sign in by typing your server address, then your email and password or an API key. About this TV shows what the TV supports, as text and as a QR code: that text, posted in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with what does not work, helps the most.

### Windows

1. Download `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-windows-x64-setup.exe` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) (with `SHA256SUMS.txt` to check it) and open it. The ZIP of the same release is the same program as a folder, to unzip anywhere and start `immuch360.exe` from it. On a computer where desktop builds 4 to 8 stopped at the start on "duplicate column name: group_date", build 9 opens that database and keeps your data.

### Linux

On Ubuntu 22.04 or later and Debian 12 or later: `sudo apt install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-amd64.deb`; on Fedora 40 or later: `sudo dnf install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-x86_64.rpm`, both from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9). Then start Immuch360 Desktop from your applications. A preview: the folders of the computer, photos and 360° photos as a sphere, and flat videos through the system's libmpv, which the package installs (360°, 3D and raw videos play flat, with a message); SMB shares work from Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 on. On Fedora, camera videos need the FFmpeg of RPM Fusion. Without GNOME Keyring or KWallet, the passwords are kept for the session only, and the settings of the app say so.

### macOS

Open `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-macos.dmg` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) and drag the app to Applications (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later). It is not signed nor notarized yet: Control click it and choose Open on macOS 14 and earlier, or choose "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security on macOS 15 and later. Nobody working on the project has a Mac: the app was only run on the project's CI Mac, where it opens files and folders and plays flat videos with its own libmpv; its network access has not been tried. Reports are welcome.

<a id="privacy"></a>
## Dateschutz

Nothing goes to the developer: no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising. The app talks only to the servers you choose (your Immich server, your shares, your Plex server, your cameras) and, without a server, to nothing at all. Passwords and tokens stay in the secure storage of the device; LG and Samsung TVs have none for the app, so there they stay in the app's own storage, not encrypted. The camera is used on the device only, by the Spatial 2.5D player and to scan the code of a screen you sign in; its images are never stored and never sent anywhere. A screen signed in from your phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on your home network, never your password; an LG TV is signed in by a page in the phone's browser, which sends your email and password to the TV unencrypted on your home network, and the TV keeps only the session. The full policy is in [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), and the details per source in the [user guide](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

Immuch360 ass en net offiziellen Fork vun der [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich)-Handy-App, ënner der [GNU AGPL v3](../LICENSE), net verbonnen mat an net ënnerstëtzt vum Immich-Team oder vun FUTO. Dëse Fork existéiert, well 360°-Kucken um Handy an Immich scho säit Januar 2024 gefrot gëtt a nach net an der offizieller App ass; hien bitt et elo un, sammelt richteg Réckmeldungen vun Apparater an wäert dem Immich, a klenge Pull Requests, alles zrécksginn, wat d'Maintainer wëllen. Alles, wat déi offiziell App mécht, bleift erhalen; d'Branches, d'Build-Chain an d'CI ginn am [Bau se dir selwer](../docs/immuch360/build.md) beschriwwen.

The 360° photo viewer is based on the upstream pull request [immich-app/immich#31169](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/31169) by dmitry-brazhenko, itself built on the prototype by bencefr in [#30192](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/30192); thanks to both. The remote control fixes of the search filters and the album sorting on TVs started from the work of mlc70 in [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#1](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/1), and the action rail and the filter sheets on 80 percent of the screen height come from mlc70's [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#3](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/3), with their authorship kept; thanks to mlc70. The LG webOS app, also packaged for Samsung TVs, is built on [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) by Seeky91, under the MIT licence, kept in its folder; thanks to Seeky91. The Tapo cameras were written from what [pytapo](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/pytapo), [Home Assistant Tapo Control](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/HomeAssistant-Tapo-Control) and [python-kasa](https://github.com/python-kasa/python-kasa) document. The Meta Quest view relies on the Meta Spatial SDK, which is not open source, so it stays in this fork. The SMB shares use libsmb2, under the LGPL 2.1 or later: from build 27 and desktop build 7, every build carries its notice, its licence texts and its source archive, `Immuch360-libsmb2-sources.tar.gz`, which is also attached to each release. The Windows and macOS builds carry libmpv (the library of [mpv](https://mpv.io), with [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)) built by the project, under the LGPL, with its licences and sources, listed on the licence page of the app (Licenses, at the bottom of the profile menu); the details and the trademarks of the platforms named on this page are in [License and trademarks](../docs/immuch360/platform-support.md#license-and-trademarks).

D'Handy-Screenshots vun dëser Säit goufen um Android-Build an engem Emulator gemaach, déi vum Fernseh um Google-TV-Emulator a déi vu Windows op engem Windows-11-PC, all mat CC0-Panoramen vu [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com); d'Headset-Screenshots goufen op engem Quest 3 gemaach.
