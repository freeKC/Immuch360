# Immuch360

**Media demo. Mano-mano skrin. Satiap pandanga.**

Immuch360 ialah aplikasi percuma nga sumber terbuka untuk cari, selak, maing nga jelajoh gambo nga video demo: hok biaso, nga hok panorama, 360°, 3D nga immersif hok aplikasi lain tunjuk rato, bergando ke tertarik. Dio baco dio kat mano dio ado, kat peranti tu sendiri, kat NAS ke komputer, kat server Plex ke server Immich, nga maing dio kat telefon, tablet, TV, headset Meta Quest nga komputer. Takdok akaun, takdok awan, takdok server hok perlu.

- **Apo dio buat?** Dio tunjuk gambo nga video demo sebagai galeri nga maing dio dale pemapar hok sesuai nga tiap fail: rato, sebagai sfera hok demo leh tengok keliling, dale 3D, dale headset.
- **Media mano?** Gambo nga video rato, panorama, gambo nga video 360°, VR180, 3D stereoskopik, gambo spatial Apple, sfera 360° dron DJI, nga fail mentah kamera 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), hok dicantum dek aplikasi.
- **On which devices?** Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Android TV and Google TV, LG TVs with webOS and Samsung TVs with Tizen (a separate TV app, in test builds), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S, Windows (preview), and Linux and macOS as early previews.
- **Perlu server ke akaun ko?** Tak. Immuch360 jale sendiri nga media peranti demo. Server Immich, NAS, server media ke server Plex ialah pilihe hok demo tamboh bilo demo nok.
- **Macam mano nok log masuk TV, headset ke komputer?** Nga telefon demo: imbas kod QR hok muko log masuk dio tunjuk, ke taip kod dio, sahka, nga dio bukak kat pustako Immich demo dale beberapo saat, takdok apo nok taip nga remote.

<a id="what-you-can-do"></a>
## Apo hok demo leh buat

Tiap guno kat bawoh ni kato apo hok demo dapat, lepah tu macam mano nok buat dale beberapo langkah. Panduan penoh, nga satiap pilihe, mesej nga had, ado dale [panduan pengguno](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Tengok gambo nga video demo

Immuch360 is first a gallery and a player for ordinary photos and videos. The timeline, the albums, the favorites, the memories and the search work with an Immich server; the device's own photos and videos, and the folders of a computer, show without any server. Videos play in a native player with sound, seeking, a buffering indicator and a choice of audio tracks, in the 360°, Spatial and computer players and, from build 29, in the flat player of the Android app, which shows subtitles too. On a TV the remote drives everything; on a computer the mouse and the keyboard do.

1. Bukak tab Gambo: garis maso server demo, ke peranti demo tanpo server.
2. Ketuk gambo ke video untuk bukak dio; leret kiri nga kane untuk pi hok seteruhnyo, cubit ke ketuk duo kali untuk zoom.
3. Leret atah kat video untuk perincia dio: codec, saiz, kadar bingkai, kadar bit, nga samado peranti ni leh nyahkod dio.
4. On Android phones and TVs, from build 29, a film with several audio languages or with subtitles has an audio button and a subtitles button in its controls: the audio language you pick is kept for the next films, and subtitles stay off until you pick them, except forced ones. The Immich app's Android player has neither.

Lagi: [pemapar nga pemaing video](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [perincia video nga penyahkod](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Pusing gambo nga satu kekunci

Pusing gambo 90° nga satu tekan, tanpo bukak penyunting: butang, kekunci papan kekunci ke kekunci warna kat remote TV, kat satiap platform. Aplikasi Immich pusing gambo dale penyunting dio; Immuch360 buat dio nga satu kekunci, nga untuk gambo demo sendiri kat server Immich 2.6 ke hok lagi baru dio lalu penyunting server hok samo tu, jadi aplikasi web nga peranti demo hok lain tunjuk gambo tu doh dipusing. Gambo lain dipusing kat peranti ni jah. Gambo 360°, 180° nga 3D tak sekali-kali dipusing, supayo pandanga immersif kekal lurus.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360 Rotate 90°">
</p>

1. **Telefon, tablet nga tetingkap Meta Quest**: bukak gambo tu nga ketuk Putar kat baris bawoh, sebelum Sunting, untuk pusing dio ikut arah jam; tekan lamo pusing dio arah lain. Nga papan kekunci, R nga Shift+R.
2. **Android TV nga Google TV**: kekunci kuning kat remote pusing gambo ikut arah jam nga kekunci biru lawan arah jam, kat mano-mano pun fokus dale pemapar. Kat remote hok takdok kekunci warna, tekan Bawoh: bar bawoh gambo keluar nga Putar doh dipilih (satu Kane lagi bilo gambo tu ado butang teks), nga tiap OK pusing gambo tu.
3. **TV LG (webOS)**: kuning nga biru macam kat Android TV, R nga Shift+R kat papan kekunci, ke butang pusing dale kawala gambo tu.
4. **Windows, Linux nga macOS**: R pusing ikut arah jam, Shift+R lawan arah jam, ke butang pusing kat bar atah, sebelum skrin penoh.
5. **Gambo dari kongsi rangkaie, Plex ke kamera**: butang pusing hok pertamo kat bar atah, R nga Shift+R, ke kekunci kuning nga biru.

Mano pusinga tu disimpan: gambo akaun demo sendiri kat Immich 2.6 ke hok lagi baru dipusing lalu penyunting gambo server, hok simpan fail asal nga buat gambo hok doh dipusing (bar nipih tunjuk maso dio kijo; empat pusinga, ke satu pusinga balik, bawok balik ko asal). Gambo peranti, gambo kongsi, gambo rakan kongsi ke album kongsi, nga satiap gambo kat server lamo dipusing kat peranti ni jah, untuk gambo tu jah, nga fail tu tak sekali-kali diubah; aplikasi bagitahu sekali untuk gambo Immich. Dale aplikasi Android, iOS nga komputer, pusinga hok disimpan kat peranti tunjuk dale pemapar, belum lagi dale thumbnail, tayanga slaid nga kenanga. Video, folder berkunci nga satiap gambo 360°, 180° ke 3D takdok Putar.

<a id="explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d"></a>
### Jelajoh panorama, 360°, VR180 nga 3D

Gambo 360° bukak sebagai sfera hok demo pusing nga jari ke nga gerakka telefon; video 360° maing dale pemaing sfera nga bunyi; fail VR180 dilukih kat separuh sfera; fail 3D stereoskopik tunjuk satu mato kat skrin rato nga duo mato dale headset. Panorama separuh kekal potonga dio, nga gambo mulo kat pandanga hok kamera rekod. Fail hok server tak tando leh ditengok sebagai 360° nga satu ketuk.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-sphere.jpg" width="260" alt="Gambo 360° tepi tasik dale pemapar sfera: butang tutup kat atah kiri, butang 360°, susune 3D nga giroskop kat atah kane">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-video.png" width="420" alt="Video 360° jale tepi pantai tengoh maing dale pemain 360° fon hok dipegang melinta: tutup nga namo fail kat atah kiri, 360° nga 3D kat atah kane, sebelum, undur, maing, maju nga lepah kat tengoh, bar maso kat bawoh">
</p>

1. Bukak gambo ke video. Fail 360° ado lencana 360° kat thumbnail dio, nga tab Pustako ado senarai 360° hok kumpul dio sumo, nga tapih ikut tempoh, sumber, jenih nga kamera.
2. Ketuk 360° kat bar atah. Tarik untuk tengok keliling, cubit untuk zoom, ketuk butang giroskop untuk tengok keliling nga pusing telefon.
3. Butang 3D pusing-pusing "Mono (buke 3D)", "3D, atah nga bawoh" nga "3D, sebeloh-sebeloh"; butang 360°/180° tukar antaro sfera penoh nga separuh sfera VR180.
4. Untuk fail hok tunjuk rato walaupun dio fail 360°: ⋮ kat atah kane, lepah tu "Lihat sebagai 360°".

Kat telefon nga tablet, video stereoskopik leh juga dapat kedalama kat skrin rato: pemaing Spatial 2.5D guno kamera depa untuk ikut kepalo demo nga tunjuk pandanga antaro duo mato (eksperimen). Gambo spatial Apple hok ditangkap nga iPhone dikenali nga ditunjuk dale 3D dale Meta Quest.

Lagi: [gambo nga video 360°](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#360-photos-and-videos-as-a-sphere), [3D nga VR180](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#3d-and-vr180-photos-and-videos), [Spatial 2.5D](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#depth-on-a-flat-screen-spatial-25d-experimental), [gambo nga video spatial Apple](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#apple-spatial-photos-and-videos), [senarai 360°](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list).

<a id="use-your-own-files-without-an-account"></a>
### Guno fail demo sendiri, tanpo akaun

Immuch360 tak perlu akaun ke server. Dari build 24, aplikasi bukak kat muko mulo, mano "Guno tanpo server" mari dulu: dio bukak aplikasi kat gambo nga video peranti, nga satiap pemapar, senarai 360° nga sumber rangkaie. Takdok apo-apo hok kuar dari peranti. Server leh disambung kemudia, dari Tetapa ke tab Pustako, lepah tu ciri server muncul.

<img src="../.github/readme/b19-library-no-server.png" width="260" alt="Tab Pustako tanpo server: menu 360° kat atah, lepah tu On this device nga duo gambo 360°, nga Kongsi rangkaie nga satu kongsi namo NAS">

1. Bukak aplikasi. Muko mulo tunjuk "Media demo. Mano-mano skrin. Satiap pandanga." nga tigo pilihe: ketuk hok mulo, "Guno tanpo server". Sampai build 23, muko log masuk mari dulu, nga "Guno tanpo server" kat bawoh borang.
2. Bui kebenara ko gambo nga video demo. Kalu demo tolak, aplikasi kato "Aplikasi tak leh tengok gambo dale peranti ni", nga "Bui kebenara" nga "Bukak tetapa".
3. Tab "Photos" (Gambo) tunjuk garis maso peranti. "Search" (Cari) nga "Albums" (Album) kekal kelabu, sebab dio datang dari server. Tab "Pustako" ado 360° kat atah, lepah tu "On this device" (Dale peranti ni) (album peranti) nga "Kongsi rangkaie".
4. Menu kat atah kane kato "Peranti ni jah": aplikasi tunjuk gambo nga video dale peranti ni, takdok gapo hok dihanta ko mano-mano.

Kat komputer, folder hok demo pilih main peranan galeri; kat TV, hok takdok gambo sendiri, sumber rangkaie hok main peranan tu.

Lagi: [tanpo server ke akaun](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#without-a-server-or-an-account).

<a id="reach-the-media-of-a-nas-a-computer-or-a-media-server"></a>
### Capai media NAS, komputer ke server media

Video demo dudok kat NAS, komputer ke server media. Immuch360 cari server rangkaie demo sendiri, selak dio nga maing gambo nga video dio kat tempat dio, dale satiap pemapar aplikasi, tanpo salin apo-apo: SMB (Samba, kongsi Windows), WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology nga lain-lain) nga server media DLNA (Jellyfin, Emby, minidlna, NAS, kotak TV). Telefon leh juga kongsi galeri dio sendiri kat Wi-Fi untuk headset ke mano-mano klien WebDAV, nga komputer Windows leh wak gitu juga.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-add-share.png" width="260" alt="Muko Tambah kongsi: Namo, Namo ke alamat server, Port (kalu nok), Kongsi, Pilih kongsi, Folder mulo (kalu nok), Namo pengguno, Kato lalue, Uji sambunge, nga hasil Doh sambung, 2 bendo dale folder mulo">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-share-folder.jpg" width="260" alt="Folder kongsi rangkaie jadi grid lakare kecik: gambo 360° nga lencana 360° nga video 360° nga tando maing, butang Pilih kat atah kane">
</p>

1. Bukak tab Pustako, lepah tu Kongsi rangkaie, lepah tu + nga "Perkongsian rangkaie (NAS, komputer, server media)". Dari build 24, "Tambah kongsi rangkaie atau server media" kat muko mulo bukak Kongsi rangkaie terus, tanpo server.
2. Ketuk server bawoh "Jumpo dale rangkaie", ke isi borang nga tangan: jenih, namo ke alamat server, kongsi ke laluan, folder mulo, namo pengguno nga kato lalue.
3. Ketuk "Uji sambunge", lepah tu Simpa. Ketuk kongsi tu untuk selak dio: folder dulu, lepah tu gambo nga video, nga lencana 360° kat fail hok dikenali sebagai 360°.
4. Nga server Immich hok disambung, pilih fail dale folder nga ketuk "Muat naik ko Immich" untuk hanta dio ko server demo.

The players read the bytes they need through a bridge inside the app, with byte ranges, so seeking works and nothing is stored on the device; an SMB video is read over up to six connections in parallel for smooth playback. From build 29, films recorded as M2TS (Blu-ray, AVCHD camcorders, TV recorders) play in the Android app from SMB and WebDAV shares and from DLNA servers that list them as transport streams, and a DLNA server that lists a size of 0 no longer gives an empty video.

<a id="your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server"></a>
### Gambo nga video dron DJI demo kat server Immich demo

Import the photos and videos of a DJI drone over USB, straight to your Immich server: plug the drone or a card reader into an Android phone, pick the new files and send them, with the videos placed on the map from the drone's flight data and the drone's 360° spheres shown as 360°. The Immich app backs up only what is already in the phone's gallery, and the Immich server cannot read the place of a DJI Neo 2 video yet. This page is experimental, on Android phones and tablets only, and has not been tried with a real drone yet.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/dji-drone-import.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360, a DJI drone and Immich">
</p>

1. Jubin "Dron DJI" ado dale Pustako, Kongsi rangkaie: "Dron DJI (eksperimen)" doh hidup sejak mulo dale Tetapa, Lanju. Matike dio situ kalu nok sorok jubin tu.
2. Plug the drone (a Neo 2 can stay off) or a card reader into the phone with a USB-C data cable; a DJI remote without a screen (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3) holds no photos. On the DJI drone page, tap "Choose the drone or the card", choose it in the side menu of the picker, open its DCIM folder or keep the whole drive, then "Use this folder" and Allow.
3. Muko tu senarai gambo nga video ikut hari, tanpo fail sampinga .LRF, .SRT, .THM nga .m4a. Ketuk "Pilih sumo hok baru", lepah tu "Muat naik ko Immich"; fail hok doh dihanta dulu ado tando awan nga dilangkau. "Simpan kat peranti" salin fail ko galeri telefon pulok, dale album "Immuch360 import".

Sfera 360° dron DJI (Mini, Air, Mavic nga lain, dikenali dari kamera dio) tunjuk sebagai 360° dale Immuch360 walaupun takdok tag 360°. Immich perlu tag tu: pilihe muat naik "Tando sfera dron sebagai 360° untuk Immich", tutup secaro lalai, hanta gambo tu tak berubah nga fail .xmp kecik sebeloh dio, supayo Immich pun tunjuk dio sebagai sfera. Neo 2 takdok mod sfera ke panorama: gambo nga video dio rato. Bilo telefon rakam bunyi klip Neo 2, bunyi tu ado dale fail .m4a asing: muko tu tando klip tu, nga Immich terimo dio tanpo bunyi (muat turun dio nga DJI Fly untuk simpan bunyi). <a id="your-dji-drones-live-video-on-your-phone-experimental"></a>
### Your DJI drone's live video on your phone (experimental)

See what your DJI drone sees on your Android phone, without DJI Fly and without any account: plug a DJI RC-N3 remote controller into the phone with its cable, and Immuch360 shows the drone's live video, H.265 in 1080p, with the battery of the remote and of the drone, the signal, the gimbal and the flight data, read from the cable. The remote keeps full control of the drone. It was tried on the ground with an RC-N3, a DJI Neo 2 and a Samsung Galaxy S24+; other remotes and drones have not been tried. The flight and battery numbers are not compared with DJI Fly yet: do not fly by them.

Dio dengar jah. Takdok arahan hok dihanta ko alat kawala ke dron: satu bait hok muko tu tulih tiap saat, mulo 20 saat lepah alat kawala dibukak, cuma bagitahu samo ado alat kawala terimo data, nga tak bawok arahan.

1. Tetapa, Lanju, "Diagnostik pauta DJI (eksperimen)" doh hidup sejak mulo (tak sekali-kali kat TV); jubin "Pauta DJI" ado dale Pustako, Kongsi rangkaie.
2. Turn on the drone and the remote, close DJI Fly, then open the DJI link tile and read the safety notice.
3. Tap "Start the capture", then plug in the remote with its cable. If Android asks which app should open it, choose Immuch360 without "Always", which would take the remote away from DJI Fly; if it asks for a USB mode, choose charging only.
4. The state reads "Accessory open", then "Remote frames arrive", and the video follows within a few seconds. The button on the picture shows it full screen, and the "Flight and battery" section gives the numbers of the remote and the drone.
5. "Export the capture" writes three files to `Download/Immuch360/dji-link`; "Play a capture" plays an export again on the page, without the remote, to see how well a phone or a headset decodes the drone's video.

The drone's photos and videos cannot be reached through the remote, which does not open the drone's storage: copy them from the drone itself over USB or from its card, as in [the section above](#your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server). On a Meta Quest the page is there and plays a capture, but the drone's live video does not reach the headset yet. Keep the exported files to yourself, as the page says, since they hold the serial number of the remote; a report in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with the states and counters the page shows helps, above all with other DJI remotes.

## Ciri lanju

Apo hok aplikasi leh baco nga maing, untuk pembaco hok nok tahu sumonyo. Tiap baris diperincika dale [panduan pengguno](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md) nga [laman keserasia](../docs/immuch360/compatibility.md).

- **Format**: gambo nga video rato (apo hok peranti nyahkod: MP4, MOV, MKV nga lain); gambo nga video 360° equirectangular; panorama separuh (potonga GPano); VR180; 3D stereoskopik, atah nga bawoh ke sebeloh-sebeloh, dibaco dari fail (kotak st3d) ke diteko dari bingkai; gambo spatial Apple (pasa stereo HEIC) nga video spatial (MV-HEVC, satu mato); fail mentah Insta360 .insp nga .insv, GoPro .360, DJI .osv; sumber 10 bit HLG nga PQ dipeta tone kat sfera.
- **Unjura nga metadata**: metadata GPano nga sfera dibaco dari fail, nga permintaa julat kat server ke kongsi; pandanga awal kamera; sfera dron DJI dikenali dari kamera dio, nga fail sampinga .xmp pilihe supayo Immich pun tando dio; "Lihat sebagai 360°" diinga kat peranti; VR180 dikenali dari fail ke namo dio; tempat video dron dibaco dari log terbang .SRT dio; metadata kekal utuh sebab fail asal hok dimaing, bukan salina hok ditukar; suku pusinga disimpan lalu penyunting Immich ke disimpan kat peranti, tak sekali-kali kat fail immersif.
- **Native playback**: Media3 on Android and the TV, SceneKit and AVFoundation on iOS, the Meta Spatial SDK in the headset, libmpv on Windows; the original or the transcoded stream chosen from what the device decodes; a list of the device's video decoders in Settings, Advanced; the audio track choice, subtitles in the Android player and a buffering indicator.
- **Fail mentah dicantum kat peranti**: shader fragmen Flutter untuk gambo; kesan GPU dale pemaing untuk video satu trek; duo penyahkod hardware serentak nga compositor GPU untuk video satu lens tiap trek ke tiap fail (OpenGL ES 3 kat Android nga Quest, Metal kat iOS, nga kat Windows, dari desktop build 5, duo pemaing libmpv hok aplikasi cantumka bingkai dio kat cip grafik); kalibrasi kamera dio sendiri (model Mei, V6 nga equidistant) nga rekod akselerometer; patah balik ko satu lens, strim ditranskod, salina LRV kamera ke video hok tak dicantum, bilo peranti tak leh jale duo penyahkod.
- **Sources**: the device's gallery; the folders of a computer; a drone or a camera card over USB, through the system folder picker (Android, experimental); a DJI RC-N3 remote controller over its cable for the drone's live video (Android, experimental); an Immich server; SMB 2 and 3, WebDAV, DLNA/UPnP; Plex over HTTPS checked against the server's own certificate; Tapo cameras over the local network (HTTPS and RTSP); a phone or a computer shared on the Wi-Fi as a WebDAV server; servers found by Bonjour/mDNS, a local network scan, SSDP, GDM and the TP-Link discovery, and followed when they change address.
- **Bacaa ikut permintaa**: jambata tempata layan pemaing nga julat byte nga token tiap sesi; blok besa, baco awal nga sampai nam sambunga SMB selari; thumbnail dari satu bingkai tiap video; takdok apo-apo disalin ko peranti.
- **Hardware**: penyahkod hardware diguno kat mano dio ado, nga had hok diukur (siling H.264 Quest 3, duo lens 2048x2048 paling banyok untuk penyahkod software); kat Windows pandanga 360° dilukih dek cip grafik dari tiap bingkai hok dinyahkod, saiz bingkai diukur kat tiap komputer, cip bersepadu ke berdedikasi dikesan.
- **Keistimewaa platform**: giroskop nga pemaing Spatial 2.5D kat telefon; pandanga immersif, kawala nga tangan kat Quest; susune remote, dialog papan kekunci nga sepanduk TV kat Android TV; kat komputer, tetikuh nga papan kekunci, skrin penoh, satu tetingkap jah, fail nga folder hok dibukak nga program ke dilepah kat tetingkap dio, dibaco macam fail folder demo, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF nga gambo mentah kamera folder bilo Windows ado penyahkod untuk dio, nga perlindunga data Windows untuk kato lalue nga token.
- **Privacy by construction**: no account, no cloud, no analytics; passwords and tokens in the device's secure storage (on LG and Samsung TVs, which have none for the app, in the app's own storage, not encrypted); a screen signed in from a phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on the home network, never the password (an LG TV gets the password from a page in the phone's browser, unencrypted on the home network, and keeps only the session); the camera of the Spatial player and of the code scanner used on the device only; shares, Plex and cameras reached on your network only.

## Pasang

### Telefon nga tablet Android

1. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aprogsys.immuch360), ke APK laman [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases): `Immuch360-v<version>-arm64-v8a-release.apk` untuk telefon; `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` hok universal jale kat mano-mano.
2. Aplikasi pasang sebeloh aplikasi rasmi Immich. APK dari GitHub tak kemaskini sendiri: tengok laman Releases; sekali demo doh pasang aplikasi dari store, amik kemaskini dari store tu.

### iPhone nga iPad

1. Versi App Store tengoh tunggu semaka Apple. Sampai dio terbit, buat aplikasi nga Xcode kat Mac, tengok [Buat sendiri](../docs/immuch360/build.md).
2. Ketuk "Guno tanpo server", ke "Sambung ko server Immich" untuk log masuk nga server demo; duo-duo tu ado kat muko mulo dari build 24.

<a id="android-tv-and-google-tv"></a>
### Android TV nga Google TV

1. Sampai Google Play tawar aplikasi ni kat TV, amik `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` hok universal dari laman [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. Kat TV, hidupke pilihe pembangun nga penyahpepijata USB (kat Google TV: Tetapa, Sistem, Perihal, pilih "Android TV OS build" tujuh kali, lepah tu Tetapa, Sistem, Pilihe pembangun; namo dio lain-lain ikut TV).
3. Dari komputer dale rangkaie samo, nga adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, terimo permintaa kat TV, lepah tu `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Aplikasi tu tunjuk antaro aplikasi TV, nga sepanduk dio. Pasang keluare lepah tu caro samo: `-r` simpang log masuk nga tetapa.

<a id="lg-tvs-webos"></a>
### TV LG (webOS)

1. Demo perlu TV LG nga webOS 4.0 ke hok lagi baru, komputer dale rangkaie samo nga akaun pembangun LG percuma. Kat TV, pasang Developer Mode dari LG Content Store, log masuk, hidupke Dev Mode Status nga biar TV mulo semulo; catat alamat IP dio nga frasa laluan hok aplikasi tunjuk.
2. Kat komputer, pasang webOS CLI (Node.js 18 ke hok lagi baru): `npm install -g @webos-tools/cli`, lepah tu daftar TV nga `ares-setup-device` (alamat IP dio, port 9922, pengguno `prisoner`) nga `ares-novacom --device <name> --getkey`, hok mintak frasa laluan.
3. Muat turun `Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk` dari [pra-keluara webOS](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/webos-v0.2.0), semak dio nga `SHA256SUMS.txt`, lepah tu `ares-install --device <name> Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk`. [webOS Dev Manager](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop) dari webosbrew buat bendo samo tanpo baris arahan.
4. Sesi Developer Mode tahan 1000 jam nga butang EXTEND dale aplikasi Developer Mode sambung dio; bilo dio tamat, TV buang aplikasi hok dipasang caro ni. Perincia ado dale [webos/README.md](../webos/README.md#install-on-a-tv-in-developer-mode).

<a id="samsung-tvs-tizen"></a>
### Samsung TVs (Tizen)

1. You need a Samsung TV with Tizen 5.5 or later (2020 models and later), a computer on the same network and a Samsung account. On the TV: Apps, App Settings, type 12345, turn Developer mode on, enter the IP address of the computer, then restart the TV.
2. Download `Immuch360-Tizen-0.1.0.wgt` from the [Tizen pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/tizen-v0.1.0) and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. A Samsung TV installs only an app signed for it, so the package must be signed again with a Samsung certificate that lists the DUID of your TV: [Apps2Samsung](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung), a community tool, reads the DUID, signs the package with your Samsung account and installs it; Tizen Studio with the Samsung Certificate Extension does the same by hand.
3. Open the app and sign in by typing your server address, then your email and password or an API key. About this TV shows what the TV supports, as text and as a QR code: that text, posted in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with what does not work, helps the most.

### Meta Quest

1. Dari [Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/): versi store, dikemaski bilo Meta semak tiap serahan.
2. Ke sideload fail `-quest-release.apk` [release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases) dale mod pembangun nga adb ke SideQuest: `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-quest-release.apk`, lepah tu bukak Pustako headset tu, "Unknown sources".
3. Versi store nga APK GitHub ditandata nga kunci hok lain: nok tukar dari satu ko satu lagi, buang aplikasi dulu, hok nok hapuh log masuk, tetapa nga senarai kongsi rangkaie.

<a id="windows"></a>
### Windows

1. Download `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-windows-x64-setup.exe` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) (with `SHA256SUMS.txt` to check it) and open it. The installer and the ZIP are signed; Windows shows APROGSYS as the publisher. While the publisher is new to SmartScreen, it may still ask once: "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Pilih "Install for me only" (takdok hak pentadbir) ke "Install for all users", lepah tu bahaso pemasang, hok Windows doh pilih.
3. Mulaka Immuch360 Desktop dari Start menu. Gambo nga video ado dio dale "Open with" (klik kane fail, Open with); dio tak sekali-kali jadi program default demo. Kali pertamo build baru dimulaka, leh ambik sampai satu minit sementaro Windows imba fail.
4. Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit. To update, open the installer of the next build: your sign in, folders and settings stay. The ZIP of the same release is the same program as a folder, to unzip anywhere and start `immuch360.exe` from it. On a computer where desktop builds 4 to 8 stopped at the start on "duplicate column name: group_date", build 9 opens that database and keeps your data.

### Linux

On Ubuntu 22.04 or later and Debian 12 or later: `sudo apt install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-amd64.deb`; on Fedora 40 or later: `sudo dnf install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-x86_64.rpm`, both from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9). Then start Immuch360 Desktop from your applications. A preview: the folders of the computer, photos and 360° photos as a sphere, and flat videos through the system's libmpv, which the package installs (360°, 3D and raw videos play flat, with a message); SMB shares work from Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 on. On Fedora, camera videos need the FFmpeg of RPM Fusion. Without GNOME Keyring or KWallet, the passwords are kept for the session only, and the settings of the app say so.

### macOS

Open `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-macos.dmg` from the [desktop pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) and drag the app to Applications (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later). It is not signed nor notarized yet: Control click it and choose Open on macOS 14 and earlier, or choose "Open Anyway" in System Settings, Privacy & Security on macOS 15 and later. Nobody working on the project has a Mac: the app was only run on the project's CI Mac, where it opens files and folders and plays flat videos with its own libmpv; its network access has not been tried. Reports are welcome.

<a id="privacy"></a>
## Privasi

Nothing goes to the developer: no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising. The app talks only to the servers you choose (your Immich server, your shares, your Plex server, your cameras) and, without a server, to nothing at all. Passwords and tokens stay in the secure storage of the device; LG and Samsung TVs have none for the app, so there they stay in the app's own storage, not encrypted. The camera is used on the device only, by the Spatial 2.5D player and to scan the code of a screen you sign in; its images are never stored and never sent anywhere. A screen signed in from your phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on your home network, never your password; an LG TV is signed in by a page in the phone's browser, which sends your email and password to the TV unencrypted on your home network, and the TV keeps only the session. The full policy is in [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), and the details per source in the [user guide](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

The 360° photo viewer is based on the upstream pull request [immich-app/immich#31169](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/31169) by dmitry-brazhenko, itself built on the prototype by bencefr in [#30192](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/pull/30192); thanks to both. The remote control fixes of the search filters and the album sorting on TVs started from the work of mlc70 in [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#1](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/1), and the action rail and the filter sheets on 80 percent of the screen height come from mlc70's [mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV#3](https://github.com/mlc70/Immuch-Android-TV/pull/3), with their authorship kept; thanks to mlc70. The LG webOS app, also packaged for Samsung TVs, is built on [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) by Seeky91, under the MIT licence, kept in its folder; thanks to Seeky91. The Tapo cameras were written from what [pytapo](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/pytapo), [Home Assistant Tapo Control](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/HomeAssistant-Tapo-Control) and [python-kasa](https://github.com/python-kasa/python-kasa) document. The Meta Quest view relies on the Meta Spatial SDK, which is not open source, so it stays in this fork. The SMB shares use libsmb2, under the LGPL 2.1 or later: from build 27 and desktop build 7, every build carries its notice, its licence texts and its source archive, `Immuch360-libsmb2-sources.tar.gz`, which is also attached to each release. The Windows and macOS builds carry libmpv (the library of [mpv](https://mpv.io), with [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)) built by the project, under the LGPL, with its licences and sources, listed on the licence page of the app (Licenses, at the bottom of the profile menu); the details and the trademarks of the platforms named on this page are in [License and trademarks](../docs/immuch360/platform-support.md#license-and-trademarks).

Tangkapa telefon laman ni diambik kat build Android dale emulator, hok TV kat emulator Google TV nga hok Windows kat PC Windows 11, sumo nga panorama CC0 dari [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com); tangkapa headset diambik kat Quest 3.

## Pelan jale

Gapo hok belum siap, hok paling mungki dulu. Takdok gapo sini janji, nga maklum balas dale [penjejak isu](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) tolong putuh gapo mari dulu.

- **The stores**: Google Play (build 28 in review; the TV listing in review), the App Store (version 3.3.1 in review, then version 3.3.2 with build 28), the Meta Horizon Store with build 29, the LG Content Store (the submission is in preparation).
- **Komputer**: uji satiap fungsi nga tange kat PC Windows, termasuk log masuk nga telefon; Spatial 2.5D nga webcam kat Windows, dale pembangunan untuk November 2026; lepah tu perender video 360° kat Linux nga macOS, uji kat Mac sebenar nga PC Linux sebenar; lepah tu kemas kini automatik nga aplikasi macOS bertandatangan.
- **TVs**: build 29 on a real Android TV, its video controls and the sign in with your phone included; webOS 0.2.0 on a real LG TV, with its shares, Plex and live view, then the LG Content Store version; the Samsung test build on a real Samsung TV; then the 360° videos on the sphere, the sound of the cameras and their recordings (not before December 2026).
- **Phones and tablets**: the Tapo live view and the sign in of big screens on iPhone and iPad with the next iOS release; a time bar in the iOS 360° video player, previous and next in the 360° players of phones, audio track and subtitle menus in the iOS flat player.
- **Media immersif**: .dng fisheye ganda, pelarasa paras video GoPro nga DJI dari data gerak dio sendiri, video spatial dale 3D kat Quest kalu penyahkod dio bui, sebelum nga seteruh antaro gambo spatial dale headset.
- **Sumber**: leret antaro fail folder kongsi, pengesaha Digest untuk WebDAV, kod QR nok bawo token Plex dari komputer, IPv6 untuk Plex, rekoda Tapo H.265, saluran kat skrin utamo TV.
- **Drones**: a test of the import page with a real DJI Neo 2, then the sound recorded by the phone merged into its clip; the drone's live video relayed from the phone to the Meta Quest. Nothing that sends a command to a drone is planned before those tests.
- **Hulu**: pull request kecik ko Immich untuk bahagian hok penyelenggara nok, mulo nga pemapar gambo 360°.

Senarai penoh, nga semaka peranti hok tingga untuk tiap ciri, ado dale [Status keluara](../docs/immuch360/release-status.md#roadmap).
