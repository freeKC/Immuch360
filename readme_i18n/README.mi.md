# Immuch360

Ko Immuch360 te taupānga waea a Immich me ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata 360° ka taea e koe te titiro huri noa, me tētahi pūrei utu kore mō ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata papatahi, 360°, 3D me VR180, i ngā waea me ngā papahiko Android, i ngā iPhone me ngā iPad, i ngā pōtae Meta Quest (te Quest 3 me te 3S, ā, mai i te hanga 21 te Quest 2 me te Quest Pro, kāore anō kia whakamātauria), ā, mai i te hanga 20 i Android TV me Google TV. Kua puta a Immuch360 Desktop, te taupānga ōrite i tētahi rorohiko Windows, hei arokite, tirohia [I tētahi rorohiko Windows](#on-a-windows-computer-immuch360-desktop-preview).

Mō te hunga e hopu ana ki tētahi kāmera 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360, Ricoh Theta, Samsung Gear 360), ki te aratau pōro whakaahua rānei o tētahi waea, mō te hunga rānei he pōtae ō rātou, ā, e hiahia ana ki te mātakitaki i ā rātou ake hopu mai i tētahi tūmau Immich, i te waea tonu, i tētahi NAS, i tētahi tūmau pāpāho, i tētahi tūmau Plex rānei: te tūmau ōrite, te pūkete ōrite, kāore he mono tūmau, kāore rānei he tūmau. Mai i te hanga 20 ka whakaatu anō i ngā kāmera Tapo, ora tonu, me ngā hopukanga o tō rātou kāri pūmahara.

## Ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata 360° hei pōro

Ka taurua koe i ō whakaahua ki tētahi tūmau [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich), ā, ko ētahi nō tētahi kāmera 360°, nō te aratau pōro whakaahua rānei o tētahi waea. I te taupānga waea ōkawa ka puta aua whakaahua hei tīpae papatahi, kua toro, ā, ka purei papatahi anō ngā ataata 360°. Ka taea e te taupānga tukutuku Immich te whakaatu i tētahi whakaahua 360° hei pōro, kāore e taea e te taupānga waea: kua tonoa mai i te Kohitātea 2024 i te [kōrerorero #6572](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/6572).

Ka whakatuwhera a Immuch360 i aua mea hei pōro e titiro huri noa ai koe, i ngā waea me ngā papahiko Android me iOS.

Ka huri tētahi whakaahua ina tō koe, ka topa ki te kōmiri, ki te pātō rua rānei, ka huri tonu iti nei i muri i te tō tere, ka tīmata i te tirohanga tuatahi i hopukina e te kāmera (raraunga GPano), ā, ka koi ake te kakano ina topa koe; ka whakahaeretia ngā tirohanga whānui hautanga (tapahi GPano). Ka purei tētahi ataata i tētahi pūrei pōro taketake me te oro, te tō me te pūhurihuri.

Ka mahi ngā kōnae 360° kua tuia ki hea: ngā kaweake mai i te taupānga Insta360, i Studio rānei, GoPro Player, Ricoh Theta, me ngā pōro whakaahua o ngā waea. Ko ngā kōnae mata tōtika mai i te kāmera ka tuia e te taupānga, tirohia [Ngā kōnae kāmera 360° mata](#raw-360-camera-files-without-the-cameras-app).

- **He whakaahua 360° hei pōro**: kati kei runga mauī; kei runga matau te pātene 360°/180°, te pātene tahora 3D me te pūhurihuri.
- **He ataata 360° i te pūrei 360°**: pātōtia te pikitia mō ngā mana; 360° me 3D kei runga matau.

### Whakatuwhera i tētahi whakaahua 360° hei pōro

1. Whakatuwheratia te whakaahua mai i te rārangi wā, i tētahi pukaemi, i te [rārangi 360°](#find-your-360-shots-the-360-list), i tētahi kōpaki tiritiri rānei. He tohu 360° kei ngā karakōnui o ngā whakaahua 360°.
2. Pātōtia te 360° i te pae o runga o te mātaki. Ka tuwhera te mātaki pōro, me tētahi tohu uta i te wā e uta ana te pikitia katoa; ka mau tonu te topa i a koe i te tirohanga papatahi.
3. Tōia kia titiro huri noa, kōmiria kia topa, pātōtia kia rua hei topa atu. I muri i te tō tere, ka huri tonu iti nei te tirohanga, ka pōturi haere.
4. Kia titiro huri noa mā te huri i te waea, pātōtia te pātene pūhurihuri kei runga matau (kua weto hei taunoa).
5. Ko ērā atu pātene e rua kei runga matau ko te 360°/180° (pōro katoa, haurua pōro VR180 rānei, kāore e whakaaturia mō ngā tirohanga whānui hautanga) me te 3D (te tahora o tētahi whakaahua stereo), tirohia [3D me VR180](#3d-and-vr180-photos-and-videos). Ko te pūhurihuri anake kei tētahi whakaahua Insta360 mata.
6. Katia ki te ripeka kei runga mauī.

### Purei i tētahi ataata 360°

- **Te kimi tūmau**: ka titiro te matawai whatunga me ngā rapu DLNA, Plex me Tapo ki te whatunga ā-rohe /24 anake, ā, me whai whakaaetanga whatunga ā-rohe i iOS. I te iPhone me te iPad ka tuku te rapu DLNA i ngā tono unicast anake, kia tuku rā anō a Apple i te mana multicast ki te taupānga, ā, kāore ētahi tūmau e whakautu (minidlna i Linux): tāpirihia ērā mā tō rātou wāhitau whakaahuatanga.
- **DLNA**: ko tētahi tūmau e tuku ana i tētahi tārua kua hurihia anake o tētahi kōnae ka hoatu i taua tārua; ka whakarārangi tētahi kōpaki i te 20,000 tāurunga te nui rawa.
- **Ngā karakōnui**: ka wetewete ngā karakōnui whakaahua i te kōnae katoa, ā, kāore he karakōnui mō ngā whakaahua nui ake i te 30 MB.
- **Ngā pūrei**: kāore anō te kōwhiringa ara ororongo kia tae ki te pūrei papatahi. I tētahi waea kāore anō kia taea te kōrere mai i tētahi kōnae o tētahi kōpaki ki tērā e whai ake ana (he o mua me o muri tō te mātaki rumaki o te Quest mō ngā kōnae 360° o te kōpaki). Kāore e maumaharatia te kōwhiringa 3D, 180° rānei i tētahi kōnae whatunga.

## Plex Media Server, me te kore plex.tv

Kei Plex ō ataata, ā, ka whakaatu papatahi a Plex i ō whakaahua me ō ataata 360°, e ai ki ngā tono i tōna paepae (kua tuwhera tētahi mai i te 2017). Mai i te hanga 20 ka takirua tōtika a Immuch360 ki tō Plex Media Server, me te kore plex.tv: ka kitea e ia te tūmau i te whatunga, ka whakapiri koe i tētahi tohu kotahi te wā, ā, ka pānui ora te taupānga i ngā kōnae taketake o ō pātaka, i ia mātaki o te taupānga (360°, 3D, VR180, Spatial 2.5D, te mātaki rumaki o te Quest, te mamao o te pouaka whakaata), i te kāinga, ā, mā tō whakawhiti tauranga, i waho atu i te kāinga. Kāore he mea e tāruatia, ā, ka taea ngā kōnae ka kōwhiria e koe te tuku ki Immich.

### Tāpirihia he tūmau Plex

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa “Pātaka”, kātahi ko “Ngā tiritiri whatunga”, kātahi ko + me “He Plex Media Server”. Ka tuwhera i te whārangi ōrite te momo “Plex Media Server” o te puka tiritiri, me tētahi tūmau he tūtohu Plex tōna i raro i “I kitea i te whatunga”.
2. Ka rapu te whārangi “Tāpirihia he tūmau Plex” i ngā tūmau Plex o tō whatunga (“E rapu ana i ngā tūmau Plex i tō whatunga”), ā, ka whakarārangi i a rātou i raro i “I kitea i te whatunga”. Pātōtia tōu: ka whakaatu tētahi kāri i tōna ingoa, i tōna putanga Plex me tētahi id poto.
3. Kāore i te rārangi? Patohia tōna “Wāhitau tūmau”, pēnei i `192.168.1.20`, `192.168.1.20:32400`, `https://...plex.direct:32400` rānei, kātahi ka pātō i “Rapua”. Ka pānui te taupānga i te tiwhikete o te tūmau, ā, kāore e tuku i tētahi atu mea.
4. Tikina te tohu, i tētahi rorohiko: whakatuwheratia a Plex i tētahi pūtirotiro tukutuku ka takiuru, whakatuwheratia tētahi whakaahua, ataata rānei o tō pātaka, kātahi ko “...”, “Get Info”, “View XML”. Ka tuwhera tētahi ripa hou ki tētahi wāhitau e mutu ana ki `X-Plex-Token=...`. Tāruatia taua wāhitau katoa.
5. Whakapiria ki “Tohu urunga”, tukuna rānei ki te waea ka pātō i “Whakapiri mai i te papatopenga”: ka tangohia e te taupānga te tūmau me te tohu mai i reira. Ka mahi anō ko te kupu anake i muri i `X-Plex-Token=`. He ōrite te kōrero a “Me pēhea te whiwhi”, i te whārangi; ka taea hoki e ngā kaiwhakahaere tūmau te uara `PlexOnlineToken` o te kōnae `Preferences.xml` o te tūmau te whakamahi, kāore nei e pau. I tētahi pouaka whakaata, ka tuwhera te OK i te kōrero papapātuhi: patohia te tohu ki reira (20 pūāhua te roa i te nuinga o te wā).
6. Pātōtia “Whakamātauria te tūhononga”. Ka whakautu “Kua hono: N pātaka”, ka kī rānei he aha te hē, pēnei i “Kua whakakāhoretia e te tūmau Plex tēnei tohu.”

Kātahi ka whakaatu a “Urunga mai i waho o te kāinga” i te kōrero a te tūmau: “E ai ki te tūmau ka taea ia te toro i (wāhitau):(tauranga).”, “Kāore te tūmau i kī mai i tōna wāhitau i waho o te kāinga.” rānei. Hei whakamahi i te tūmau i waho atu i te kāinga, patohia tō wāhitau tūmatanui, tō ingoa DynDNS rānei ki “Wāhitau i waho o te kāinga (IP, ingoa rānei)”, me te tauranga kua whakawhitia i Plex (Ngā tautuhinga, Urunga mamao) ki “Tauranga i waho o te kāinga”. I te taupānga Immich ka whakaatu tētahi whakaahua, ataata rānei 360° stereo i ngā karu e rua i te wā kotahi, he pikitia rua, ā, ko tētahi kōnae VR180, e hīpoki ana i te haurua o mua anake, ka torona huri noa i te pōro.

Ka mōhio a Immuch360 ki ngā tahora 3D, runga me raro, taha ki te taha rānei, mai i te kōnae (te pouaka st3d o tētahi ataata), ka matapae rānei mai i te āhua o te anga, ā, he pātene 3D tō ia mātaki hei panoni. Ka whakaatu tētahi waea i te karu mauī; ka whakaatu tētahi Meta Quest ki ia karu tōna ake haurua, i te 3D tūturu. Ka tuhia ngā kōnae VR180 ki tētahi haurua pōro, he pango te tuarā, kaua he pikitia kua toro. Ka mōhiotia mai i te kōnae (ngā rohe pōro, te kupenga rānei, te tapahi GPano), mai i tētahi “vr180”, “180” rānei i te ingoa, ā, he pātene 360°/180° tō ia mātaki.

### Panoni i te tahora, i te hīpoki rānei

1. Whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, te ataata rānei hei pōro (te pātene 360°).
2. Ka huri te pātene 3D i te “Mono (ehara i te 3D)”, “3D, runga me raro” me “3D, taha ki te taha”; ka whakaingoa tōna kupu āwhina i te tahora o nāianei.
3. Ka whakawhiti te pātene 360°/180° i waenga i te “360°, pōro katoa” me te “180°, haurua pōro (VR180)”. Kāore e whakaaturia mō ngā tirohanga whānui hautanga.
4. I te pōtae, kei te paewhiri mōhiohio o te mātaki rumaki aua pātene e rua.

Ka maumaharatia te kōwhiringa 360°/180° i te pūrere mō te pāpāho o te pātaka, kāore anō mō tētahi kōnae o tētahi tiritiri whatunga. Kāore te kōwhiringa 3D e maumaharatia ina kati te mātaki: ka pā anō te tahora e kīia ana e te kōnae, te matapae rānei, ā tērā wā (ka maumahara te pūrei Spatial 2.5D i raro i tōna ake).

I tirohia i tētahi Galaxy S24+ me tētahi Quest 3 me ngā ataata tauira 3D 360° tūturu (VRTogether, Vuze, Kandao) me tētahi whakaahua 3D; VR180 i tētahi kaiwhaihanga Android me tētahi Galaxy S24+ me ngā pāpāho waihanga. Nau mai ngā pūrongo mai i ētahi atu kāmera.

## Hōhonutanga i runga i te mata papatahi: Spatial 2.5D (whakamātautau)

Ka taea e tētahi ataata stereo te whiwhi hōhonutanga i runga i te mata papatahi. Nā ngā karu e rua o te ataata te hōhonutanga, ka whai te kāmera o mua i tō māhunga, ā, ka waihanga te taupānga i te tirohanga i waenganui, nō reira ka rite te mata ki tētahi matapihi ki te whakaaturanga: nekehia tō māhunga, ā, ka neke ngā mea tata ki te papamuri. Ngā waea me ngā papahiko anake.

### Whakamahia

1. Tirohia mēnā kua kā: “Settings”, “Asset Viewer”, “Videos” (Ataata), “Spatial 2.5D (whakamātautau)”. Kua kā hei taunoa; mā te weto ka tangohia te pātene Spatial i ngā wāhi katoa.
2. Whakatuwheratia tētahi ataata. I te wā e kā ana te tautuhinga, ka whakaatu te pae o runga i te pātene Spatial (he ata 3D hurihuri) i ia ataata; i tētahi kōpaki tiritiri whatunga ko ngā kōnae stereo anake ka whiwhi, ā, kei te tahua ⋮ o ērā atu te “Spatial 2.5D”.
3. Pātōtia. Ka tonoa te whakaaetanga kāmera o mua i te wā tuatahi; ka whakamahia anake te kāmera ina pēhi koe i te pātene.
4. Runga matau: “Ara ororongo” ina e rua neke atu ngā ara o te ataata, te tahora o nāianei (pātōtia mō te rārangi: “Aunoa”, “Ehara i te stereo”, “Taha ki te taha”, “Runga me raro”, ko ngā mea whakamutunga e rua me ngā karu kua whakawhitia hoki), 360°/180° i tētahi ataata 360°, me “Whakapokapū anō”, e tango ana i te tūnga o nāianei o tō māhunga hei pokapū.
5. Raro: pūrei, te rārangi wā, kātahi ko “Te pāwera o te māhunga” i te wā e arohia ana tō māhunga, me te rēreti tirohanga mai i te L ki te R, e neke ā-ringa ana i te tirohanga; ka puta ina weto te aroturuki māhunga, kua ngaro rānei tō kanohi i a ia.
6. Katia ki te ripeka, ki te hoki a te pūnaha rānei.

Me “Settings”, “Advanced” (Arā atu), “Troubleshooting” (Rapurongoā) kua kā, ka tāpiri te pūrei i tētahi paparanga kei runga mauī (ngā pāpātanga whakaputa me te rerekētanga, te taumata kounga, te tirohanga, te āhua aroturuki), ka whakaatu tonu i te rēreti tirohanga, ā, ka tāpiri i tētahi pouaka tohu “Disparity map” (mahere rerekētanga) e whakaatu ana i te hōhonutanga matapae, kaua i te pikitia.

### Ngā hōputu, te tūmataiti me ngā tepe

- **Ngā hōputu**: taha ki te taha me runga me raro, me ngā karu kua whakawhitia hoki, whānui katoa, haurua rānei, papatahi, 360° me VR180. Ka pānuitia te tahora mai i te kōnae, ki te kore ka matapaetia mai i te āhua o te anga, i te ingoa kōnae rānei (sbs, ou, tb me ērā atu; ka mōhiotia tētahi kōnae haurua whānui mā tōna ingoa anake), ā, ka taea te kōwhiri ā-ringa i te pūrei. Ka maumaharatia te kōwhiringa mō tētahi ataata o te pātaka, kaua mō tētahi kōnae o tētahi tiritiri whatunga.
- **Kāmera**: ka tukatukahia ngā whakaahua i te pūrere anake, kāore rawa e penapenatia, kāore rawa e tukuna ki hea. Kāore rawa he whakaaetanga kāmera o te hanga Quest (kāore he kāmera o te pōtae ka taea e tētahi taupānga te whakamahi), ā, kāore te pūrei Spatial e tukuna ki reira: kua hunaia tōna pātene me tōna tautuhinga i te pōtae.
- **Ngā tepe**: whakamātautau, ngā waea me ngā papahiko anake (kaua i te Meta Quest), me whai OpenGL ES 3.0, Metal rānei. He matapae te hōhonutanga. Ka pai rawa i te whakapae me te mārama o tō kanohi. I tirohia i tētahi Galaxy S24+; nau mai ngā urupare iPhone.
- **Whakakapi**: ki te hē tētahi mea (kāore he kāmera, he pūrere kāore e tautokona, he tahora kāore e taea te pānui), ka hoki koe ki te pūrei noa.

Ka hopu tētahi iPhone i ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata mokowā, ā, kāore te tūmau Immich e kōrero mō rātou: i te taupānga Immich he whakaahua, he ataata papatahi rānei. - **Ngā whakaahua mokowā** he kōnae HEIC (`.heic`, `.heif`, `.hif`) e pupuri ana i ngā pikitia e rua, kotahi mō ia karu, kua whakarōpūtia hei takirua stereo. ### Kite i tētahi whakaahua mokowā i te 3D i te pōtae

Ka tuwhera te mātaki rumaki me te whakaahua i runga i tētahi anga papatahi 2 m ki mua i a koe, ka whiwhi ia karu i tōna ake pikitia, i tētahi whānui i tangohia mai i te āpure tirohanga o te kāmera ina hoatu e te kōnae (48° ki te kore). 4. Mā te rākau kōnui whakarunga, whakararo rānei ka nui ake, ka iti iho rānei (ngā hipanga 6°, mai i te 30° ki te 90°); mā te rākau kōnui matau whakamauī, whakamatau rānei ka whakahokia ki mua i a koe.

## Mātakitaki i tō pouaka whakaata (Android TV me Google TV)

E hiahia ana koe ki ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata 360°, ō pukaemi me ngā ataata o tō NAS, o tō tūmau Plex rānei i te mata nui, mō te whānau i runga i te hōpa, me te mamao o te pouaka whakaata. Mai i te hanga 20 ka rere te taupānga Android ōrite i Android TV me Google TV, ko te mamao anake te tāuru: kotahi te APK mō ngā waea me ngā pouaka whakaata, ka hurihia ngā mātaki 360° me te 3D ki ngā pere, ā, kei te pouaka whakaata ngā tiritiri whatunga, a Plex me ngā kāmera Tapo, me te tūmau Immich, me te kore rānei.

<a id="install-it-on-the-tv"></a>
### Tāuta ki te pouaka whakaata

1. Kia tuku rā anō a Google Play i te taupānga ki ngā pouaka whakaata, e tatari ana ki te arotake a Google i te tuku pouaka whakaata, tangohia te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui o te whārangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. I te pouaka whakaata, whakakāngia ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake me te patuiro USB (i tētahi Google TV: Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Mō, tīpakohia te “Android TV OS build” e whitu ngā wā, kātahi ko Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake; he rerekē ngā ingoa i ia pouaka whakaata).
3. Mai i tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite, me te adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, whakaaetia te tono i te pouaka whakaata, kātahi ko `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Ka puta te taupānga i waenga i ngā taupānga o te pouaka whakaata, me tōna haki. Tāutahia te tuku e whai ake ana mā te huarahi ōrite: ka pupuri a `-r` i te takiuru me ngā tautuhinga.

### Te tīmatanga tuatahi

1. Takiuru pērā i tētahi waea: ka neke ngā pere i tētahi tāpare mai i tētahi āpure ki tētahi, ā, mā te OK i tētahi āpure (“Pēhia te OK hei patopato”) ka tuwhera te papapātuhi o te pouaka whakaata i tētahi kōrero, mō te wāhitau tūmau, te īmēra me te kupuhipa.
2. Kōwhiria rānei te “Whakamahia me te kore tūmau”. Kāore he whakaahua ake o tētahi pouaka whakaata, nā reira ka kī te ripa “Photos” (Whakaahua) “Kāore he whakaahua, he ataata rānei nō tēnei pouaka whakaata: whakatuwherahia he tiritiri whatunga mai i te Pātaka.” me tētahi pātene “Ngā tiritiri whatunga”. Tāpirihia tētahi tiritiri, tētahi tūmau Plex, tētahi kāmera rānei ki reira, pērā i tētahi waea (tirohia [Ngā tiritiri whatunga](#network-shares-a-nas-a-computer-or-a-media-server)).

### Te nekeneke me te mamao

- Ka neke ngā pere i te tāpare, ka whakatuwhera te OK i te mea kei raro, ka hoki te Hoki. Mai i tētahi ripa, ka haere te Hoki ki te tahua taha, kātahi ki “Photos” (Whakaahua), kātahi ka puta i te taupānga.
- Ka panuku te hongere ake me te hongere iho i tētahi whārangi i te wā kotahi.
- I ngā mātaki, ka mahi ngā pātuhi purei me te tatari, whakamua tere, whakamuri, o muri me o mua o te mamao, ā, ka whakaatu te pātuhi mōhiohio i ngā taipitopito o tētahi whakaahua, ataata rānei.

### Ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata me te mamao

1. **He whakaahua**: ka haere te mauī me te matau ki te mea o mua, o muri rānei, ka whakaatu, ka huna rānei te OK i ngā mana, ka haere te Ake ki te pae o runga (kei reira te pātene 360°) me te Iho ki te pae o raro.
2. **He ataata**: ka tatari te OK, me te whakaatu i ngā mana, ka purei rānei. I te wā e purei ana, ka peke te mauī me te matau 10 hēkona whakamuri, whakamua rānei; i te wā kua tatari, ka haere ki te tūemi o mua, o muri rānei, pērā i te kī a te tohu: “Kua tatari: ka haere ngā pere ki te tūemi o mua, o muri rānei”.
3. **He whakaahua 360°**, mai i te pātene 360°: ka titiro huri noa ngā pere, ka tere ake ina puritia, ā, ka āta mutu te tirohanga. Ka neke te OK ki ngā pātene o te pae o runga, ki “Topa atu”, i te taha o “Topa mai”, te āpure tirohanga me te 3D; ka topa anō te hongere ake me te iho. Ka hoki te Hoki mai i ngā pātene ki te pikitia, kātahi ka kati. Ko te tohu: “Ngā pere hei titiro huri noa, OK mō ngā mana, Hoki hei kati”.
4. **He ataata 360°**, mai i te pātene 360°: ka titiro huri noa ngā pere i te wā e huna ana ngā mana, ka tatari te OK me te whakaatu i a rātou, ka huna te Hoki i a rātou, kātahi ka kati. Ko te tohu: “Ngā pere hei titiro huri noa, OK hei tatari me te whakaatu i ngā mana, Hoki hei kati”.
5. **Ngā maumahara**: ka haere te mauī me te matau i ngā whakaahua, ā, ki te maumahara e whai ake ana, ka haere te Ake ki te pātene kati me te Iho ki “View in timeline” (Tirohia i te rārangi wā).
6. **Ngā tiritiri me Plex**: ko ngā pātuhi ōrite i ngā whārangi whakaahua me ngā whārangi ataata o tētahi tiritiri, ka whakatuwhera ai te mauī me te matau i te kōnae o mua, o muri rānei o te kōpaki.

“Settings” (Tautuhinga), “Preferences” (Manakohanga), “Tahora mō te mamao”: “Ngā tāpare arotahi nui me ngā pātuhi o te mamao, me te kore o ngā mana e hiahia ana ki te mata pā. Ka whakakāngia e Aunoa ki Android TV me Google TV.” Ko “Aunoa” te taunoa; e hāngai ana a “Kā” ki tētahi papahiko e whakahaeretia ana e tētahi papapātuhi, e tētahi papatākaro rānei; ka weto a “Weto” i tētahi pouaka whakaata. Kei Android anake te tautuhinga. Ka mahi ngā pere me te OK i ngā mātaki me tētahi papapātuhi, papatākaro rānei, ahakoa te tautuhinga.

Me whakaū: te ahunga e huri ai ngā pere i tētahi ataata 360°, te papapātuhi o te pouaka whakaata (Gboard) i te kōrero kupu, te pātuhi OK o ngā mamao pōkākā, ngā tawhē me te haki i te mata kāinga o te pouaka whakaata. ## Ngā taipitopito ataata, ngā pūwetewete, ā, he aha e haukotikoti ai tētahi ataata

Ka pātai te tangata ko tēhea codec, rahi, pāpātanga moka hoki ka purei i te Quest 3, ā, he aha e haukotikoti ai tētahi kaweake 5.7K i te pōtae engari ka purei i te waea. Ko te whakautu ko te pūwetewete pūmārō: ka mutu te pūwetewete H.264 o te Quest 3 (XR2 Gen 2) ki te 4096x2304 pea, nō reira ko tētahi ataata H.264 5760x2880 (taumata 6.0, tata ki te 200 Mbit/s, te kaweake Insta360 noa) ka wetewetehia ki te 17 fps pea i te pōtae, me ngā tīhoihoi poraka, ahakoa ka pai te purei o taua kōnae anō i tētahi waea.

Ka pai te purei o taua ataata anō i te HEVC (H.265) i te pōtae: ko tētahi ataata Insta360 X4 8K HEVC (7680x3840, 29.97 fps, 210 Mbit/s, kōtaha Main taumata 6.1, 8 moka) ka purei māeneene i te mātaki rumaki, i tōna taumira taketake, kāore he whakawaehere anō (i pūrongotia e tētahi kaiwhakamahi i tētahi Quest 3).

Kotahi te pana “Force original video” o te taupānga Immich, ā, ko te codec anake ka whakaaturia. Ka whakaatu a Immuch360 he aha te ataata, he aha ka wetewetehia e te pūrere, ā, ka kōwhiri i te kōnae ka purei.

<img src="../.github/readme/b19-video-details.png" width="260" alt="Ngā taipitopito o tētahi ataata 360°: Codec H.264 High (avc1.640033), 3840 x 1920, 30 fps; Pāpātanga moka 16 Mbit/s; Pikitia 8 moka, SDR, BT.709; Ka wetewetehia ki tēnei pūrere: Yes, Pūmārō, tae atu ki 4096 x 2304">

### Pānui i ngā taipitopito o tētahi ataata

1. Whakatuwheratia te ataata ka kōrere whakarunga: ka puta ngā taipitopito i raro i te pikitia.
2. “Codec”: te codec me tōna kōtaha me te aho codecs, te rahi anga me te pāpātanga anga, hei tauira “H.264 High (avc1.640033) • 3840 x 1920 • 30 fps”, pērā i te pikitia i runga ake.
3. “Pāpātanga moka” (mai i te hanga 18): te pāpātanga moka o te ara ataata, tērā rānei e kīia ana e te kōnae; ki te kore ka tohua “te kōnae katoa, me te oro”, “he whakatau tata mai i te rahi o te kōnae” rānei.
4. “Pikitia” (mai i te hanga 18): te hōhonu moka, SDR, te whakawhitinga HDR rānei (HLG, PQ, Dolby Vision) me te mokowā tae.
5. “Ka wetewetehia ki tēnei pūrere”: āe, kāo rānei, pūmārō, pūmanawa rānei, te rahi nui rawa ka whakaaetia e te pūwetewete, me “Kāore he wetewete waehere mō (kōtaha)” ina kei te pūrere te codec engari kāore taua kōtaha.

Ka puta tētahi rārangi i muri anake i te pānuitanga o te kōnae, ā, mō ngā mea anake e kīia ana e te kōnae. Ka whiwhi tētahi whakaahua, ataata mokowā Apple rānei i tōna ake rārangi, tirohia [Ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata mokowā Apple](#apple-spatial-photos-and-videos).

### Kite i ngā mea ka wetewetehia e te pūrere

Ka whakarārangi a “Settings”, “Advanced”, “Ngā pūwetewete ataata o tēnei pūrere” (mai i te hanga 15) i ngā mea ka wetewetehia e te waea, e te pōtae rānei: te codec, pūmārō, pūmanawa rānei, te rahi nui rawa, te pāpātanga anga, ā, mai i te hanga 18 ngā kōtaha (Main, Main 10, HDR10). Mai i te hanga 19 he tāurunga MV-HEVC tōna, te codec o ngā ataata mokowā Apple. Ka whakatakoto te pātene “Tāruatia” i te rārangi ki te papatopenga mō tētahi pūrongo pepeke.

### Kōwhiri i te taketake, i te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō rānei

1. Whakatuwheratia “Settings”, “Asset Viewer”, kātahi ko “Videos”.
2. I raro i “Te mātāpuna ataata” (“Ko tēhea kōnae ka whakatangihia ina whai tārua kua whakawaehere anō te tūmau”), kōwhiria “Te taketake ina wetewetehia e tēnei pūrere”, “Te taketake i ngā wā katoa”, “Te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō i ngā wā katoa” rānei.

Mai i te hanga 15 ka pā te kōwhiringa ki ia ataata tūmau: te pūrei papatahi, ngā pūrei 360° me Spatial, me te mātaki rumaki o te Quest. Kia kōwhiri rā anō koe, ka pupuri tētahi waea i tā te pana tawhito “Force original video” i kī ai (kua weto hei taunoa: te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō, ko te taketake tonu ina kāore te tūmau i whakawaehere anō), ā, ka purei te Quest i te taketake ina wetewetehia e te pōtae.

Ka pānui te arowhai i te codec, te rahi me te pāpātanga anga mai i te kōnae, ka whakatairite ki ngā pūwetewete pūmārō (ka herea te H.264 i te Quest 3 ki te 4096x2304 kua inea). Ka huri tētahi pūrei kāore e taea te wetewete i te taketake ki te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō me tētahi karere: “E whakatangi ana i te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō: ka nui ake te taketake (codec me te rahi) i ngā mea ka wetewetehia e tēnei pūrere”.

I te pōtae ka tīmata te mātaki rumaki i te taketake, ā, i ōna anga tuatahi, ka huri ki te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō o te tūmau ina nui ake te taketake i ngā pūwetewete, ka kī pērā i te paewhiri mōhiohio; ina kore he rerenga kua whakawaehere anō, ina nui rawa tonu, ina nō te pōtae, nō tētahi tiritiri whatunga rānei te kōnae, ka kī pērā te paewhiri mōhiohio mō te 10 hēkona, me te mea hei panoni. Ko te hanga 14, i tukuna ki te Horizon Store, ka tirotiro anake i te H.264 i runga ake i te 4096x2304, kātahi ka whakamātau i te rerenga purei o te tūmau pērā anō.

Mō ngā ataata arotahi rua mata ka tono te arowhai kia rua ngā pūwetewete i te wā kotahi, ā, mai i te hanga 19 ka whakakāhore i ngā rerenga e rua nui ake i te 2048x2048 ia mea ki tētahi pūwetewete pūmanawa, tirohia [Ngā kōnae kāmera 360° mata](#raw-360-camera-files-without-the-cameras-app).

### Hoatu ki te pōtae tētahi ataata ka taea e ia te wetewete

I Immich, haere ki “Administration” (Whakahaere), “Settings”, “Video Transcoding Settings” (Ngā tautuhinga whakawaehere anō ataata), ka kōwhiri i tētahi o ēnei:

- **Ka whakawaehere anō ia ataata H.264 ki te HEVC**: tautuhia te “Video codec” (Codec ataata) ki te HEVC me te “Target resolution” (Taumira ūnga) ki te “original” (taketake) (ka whakaiti te 720p taunoa i tētahi ataata 360° ki te 1440x720), ā, ko te HEVC anake hei pupuri i te “Accepted video codecs” (Ngā codec ataata e whakaaetia ana). Ka whakawaehere anō ia ataata H.264 o te pātaka, kaua ko ngā mea 360° anake, ā, ka whiwhi te pōtae i te HEVC i te taumira katoa.
- **Ko ngā mea nui ake i te 1440p anake**: puritia te H.264 i te “Accepted video codecs”, tautuhia te “Transcode policy” (Kaupapahere whakawaehere anō) ki te “Videos higher than target resolution or not in an accepted format” (Ngā ataata teitei ake i te taumira ūnga, kāore rānei i tētahi hōputu e whakaaetia ana) me te “Target resolution” ki te 1440p, me te “Video codec” kua tautuhia ki te HEVC mēnā ka taea. Ka whakawaehere anō ngā mea nui ake, ā, ka whiwhi te pōtae i te 2880x1440. Ka whakawaehere anō hoki ngā ataata 4K noa ki te 1440p.

Ka pā ēnei tautuhinga ki te tūmau katoa, ki ia kaiwhakamahi me ia taupānga, ā, ka roa te whakawaehere anō o tētahi pātaka nui, he haora maha o te wā CPU me ētahi atu mokowā kōpae; kāore ngā taketake e panonitia. Kāore ngā pūtirotiro kāore e tautoko ana i te HEVC (Firefox i te nuinga o ngā pūnaha, Chrome me te kore HEVC pūmārō) e purei i tētahi whakawaehere anō HEVC i te taupānga tukutuku Immich. Ko te rongoā māmā rawa mō ngā ataata hou ko te kaweake i te H.265 mai i te taupānga Insta360, i Studio rānei. I muri i te panoni i ēnei tautuhinga, me whakawaehere anō ngā ataata o nāianei: “Administration”, “Job Queues” (Ngā tūtira mahi) (“Jobs” (Ngā mahi) i ngā putanga tawhito o Immich), “Transcode videos” (Whakawaehere anō i ngā ataata), “All”.

Ko tērā atu kōwhiringa, ā, ko te mea kotahi mō ngā kōnae o tētahi tiritiri whatunga, o te pōtae rānei (kāore he rerenga kua whakawaehere anō), ko te whakawaehere anō i te kaweake ki te HEVC i mua i te tukuake, i te tārua rānei ki te tiritiri:

```bash
ffmpeg -i VID_360.mp4 -c:v libx265 -crf 20 -preset medium -tag:v hvc1 -c:a copy -movflags +faststart VID_360_hevc.mp4
exiftool -overwrite_original -tagsFromFile VID_360.mp4 -XMP-GSpherical:all VID_360_hevc.mp4
exiftool -ProjectionType VID_360_hevc.mp4        # must print: equirectangular
```

Ka tapa a `-tag:v hvc1` i te ara HEVC kia rite ki tā ngā pūrere Apple me ngā pūtirotiro e tūmanako ana, ka pupuri a `-c:a copy` i te ororongo kia pērā tonu. Ka maka a ffmpeg i te tūtohu 360° o te kaweake, ka tārua anō te rārangi exiftool; ki te kore, ka whakaatu a Immich i te ataata hei ataata papatahi.

### Ngā taketake kāore e taea te rere

Ina whakahapa te tūmau i ngā tono HTTP Range i te taketake, ā, kei te mutunga o te kōnae te taupū MP4 (moov), me tikiake te kōnae katoa i mua i te anga tuatahi, nō reira ka hoki te taupānga ki te rerenga purei o te tūmau. Ka taea tēnei e tētahi takawaenga kōaro i mua i a Immich e pūrua ana i ngā whakautu, e tango ana rānei i ngā pane Range.

### Tikiake me te tāuta i Windows

Ko te hanga o nāianei te tuku-mua GitHub [Immuch360 Desktop 3.3.0-rc.0, desktop build 3 (Windows preview)](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-3). I hangaia mai i te peka matua, `immuch360`, i muri i te hanumitanga o te pūrei ataata 360° o te rorohiko ki roto, i te commit e whakahuatia ana e ōna tuhipoka tuku: ngā pūtake o te hanga waea 21 me te putanga rorohiko. E hiahia ana ki Windows 10, 11 rānei, 64 moka.

1. Tikiake te ZIP, ka wetekōpaki ki hea rānei, hei tauira ki Documents. Hei whakakapi i te hanga papamahi 2, mukua tōna kōpaki, wetekōpakihia rānei ki tētahi kōpaki hou: ka noho tō takiuru, ō kōpaki me ō tautuhinga ki tō kōtaha Windows, kaua ki taua kōpaki.

### Ngā ataata 360° me te maramara whakairoiro

I te pūrei 360° (mai i te hanga papamahi 3), ka tuhi te maramara whakairoiro i te tirohanga e titiro ana koe mai i ia anga o te ataata. Ka tono te mono ataata ki a libmpv kia tuhia ia anga ki tētahi pikitia o te rahi i kōwhiria, kātahi ka tuhi te hipanga tuhi ake o te taupānga mai i taua pikitia i te wāhanga o te pōro e titiro ana koe. Mā te huri i te tirohanga ka panoni tētahi uara o taua hipanga, kaua ko te pūrei: kāore te tō e utu pūmahara, ā, ka huri tētahi ataata kua whakatārewatia me te kore e wetewete i tētahi mea.

Ka ine te taupānga i ngā hēkona tuatahi o ia ataata 360°, ka kōwhiri i te rahi o te anga ka mahia e te maramara: te anga katoa i tētahi maramara motuhake, 2880 pika te whānui rawa i tētahi maramara kua hangaia ki roto i te tukatuka (ka kī a Windows ki te taupānga ko tēhea momo, nō reira ka kīia he maramara whakauru te maramara Intel Arc o tētahi rorohiko pōnaho Core Ultra), kotahi hipanga iti iho ina kore e mau te maramara. Ka puritia tāna i ine ai mō ngā ataata e whai ake nei o te momo ōrite (codec, rahi, pāpātanga anga me te pūwetewete), kaua mō ia ataata.

Ina kore te hipanga iti rawa e māeneene, ka kī te pūrei “This graphics card cannot show the 360° view of this video smoothly” (Kāore e taea e tēnei kāri whakairoiro te whakaatu māeneene i te tirohanga 360° o tēnei ataata); ina tawhiti rawa, ina kore rānei e taea e te maramara te tuhi i te tirohanga (kāore he OpenGL ES 3.0, he taraiwa e whakakāhore ana), ka pūrei papatahi te ataata, ā, ka kī te pūrei he aha ai. Ko tētahi ataata i pūrei papatahi kāore e pūrei papatahi i te wā e whai ake nei: ka inea anō te ataata e whai ake nei o tōna momo.

Ina ko te wetewete o tētahi ataata tūmau kāore e mau, kaua ko te tuhi (he ataata ka wetewetehia e te tukatuka, pērā i te 5.7K H.264), ka huri te pūrei ki te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō o te tūmau mai i te wāhi i tae ai ia, me te karere o ngā waea: “E whakatangi ana i te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō: ka nui ake te taketake (codec me te rahi) i ngā mea ka wetewetehia e tēnei pūrere”.

- **Whakamahia te maramara whakairoiro motuhake o tētahi rorohiko pōnaho.** Ka whakahaere a Windows i te taupānga i te maramara whakauru ki te kore e kīia kētia. I ngā tautuhinga Windows, System, Display, Graphics, tāpiritia te `immuch360.exe`, ka kōwhiri i te “High performance”, kātahi ka tīmata anō i te taupānga. I te rorohiko pōnaho whakamātautau (he maramara Intel UHD me tētahi NVIDIA RTX 4060 Laptop), i tuhi te maramara NVIDIA i ngā ataata 360° 4K, 5.7K me 8K i te rahi katoa me ngā anga 30 ia hēkona i te wā e huri ana te tirohanga. I tuhi te maramara Intel i a rātou ki te 2880 pika te whānui rawa: he ataata 4K H.264 me tētahi 8K HEVC i ngā anga 30 pea ia hēkona me tētahi hanga 2026 o libmpv, he ataata 4K i te 23 ki te 27 me te libmpv 2024 o media-kit (tirohia [Kāore anō kia tae](#not-there-yet)), me tētahi ataata 5.7K H.264, ka wetewetehia e te tukatuka, i te 20 pea: mai i te tūmau, ka huri te pūrei ki te rerenga kua whakawaehere anō; ko tētahi ataata kāore he rerenga kua whakawaehere anō, mai i tētahi kōpaki hei tauira, ka pūrei tonu me te karere “This graphics card cannot show the 360° view of this video smoothly”.
- **Kōwhiria e koe ake te kaiwhakaputa.** I “Settings”, “Advanced”, whakakāngia te “Troubleshooting” (Rapurongoā): ka puta he tāurunga “360° video renderer” (Kaiwhakaputa ataata 360°), ko “Automatic” (Aunoa) te taunoa. Ka tuku anō ia i te “Plugin, full size” (Mono, rahi katoa), “Plugin, at most 4096 wide” (Mono, 4096 te whānui rawa), “Plugin, at most 2880 wide” (Mono, 2880 te whānui rawa) me “Flat, without the 360° view” (Papatahi, kāore he tirohanga 360°), mō te ataata 360° e whai ake nei ka whakatuwheratia e koe. I raro iho, ka whakaingoa te “Measured last” (Inenga whakamutunga) i te rahi i tuhia ai te ataata 360° whakamutunga, ōna anga ia hēkona, te maramara whakairoiro me te pūwetewete: tāruatia ki tētahi pūrongo hapa. Mā te kōwhiri anō i te “Automatic”, ahakoa koia kē te kōwhiringa, ka warewaretia ngā mea i inea, ā, ka inea anō ngā ataata 360° e whai ake nei.
- **Ngā mea ka wetewetehia e te maramara.** Ka whakarārangi a “Settings”, “Advanced”, “Ngā pūwetewete ataata o tēnei pūrere” i ngā mea ka wetewetehia e te maramara whakairoiro e whakamahia ana, pērā i tā Direct3D 11 e pūrongo ana, ā, ka whakatika te taupānga i taua rārangi ki āna i ine ai i a ia e pūrei ana: ka whai te tautuhinga “Te mātāpuna ataata” me te arowhai arotahi-rua o ngā ataata mata i a ia. Ko te H.264 whānui ake i te 4096 pika (te 5.7K o ngā kāmera 360°) ka wetewetehia e te tukatuka, i te mea kāore tētahi o ngā maramara o te rorohiko pōnaho whakamātautau e tango ana.

<a id="build-it-yourself-on-windows"></a>
### Hangaia e koe anō i Windows

E hiahia ana koe ki Windows 10, 11 rānei i x64, Flutter 3.47.2 mō Windows, Visual Studio 2022, ōna Build Tools rānei me te kawenga mahi “Desktop development with C++”, Developer Mode kua whakakāngia i ngā tautuhinga Windows (e hiahia ana a Flutter mō ngā mono), me Python 3 mō te hōtuhi pūtea.

1. Tikina ngā pūtake, ka whakahaere i te whakaputa waehere. Ka hanga hoki te peka matua, `immuch360`, i te putanga rorohiko: kāore he peka hei huri atu. Ka whakamahi te whakaputa waehere i Java me Node, nō reira whakahaerehia i Linux, i macOS, i WSL rānei; me WSL, puritia te tārua ki tētahi puku Windows, ka kitea e WSL i raro i `/mnt/c`, `/mnt/d` rānei:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360.git
   cd Immuch360/mobile
   mise install
   mise run install
   mise run codegen
   ```

2. I Windows, i te kōpaki `mobile` ōrite, hangaia te taupānga. Ka tikiake te hanga tuatahi i ngā whare pukapuka o te pūrei ataata (libmpv me ANGLE) mai i GitHub, ā, ka tirotiro i ia pūranga mā tōna SHA-256; ka whakahiato anō ia i te mono Direct3D 11 iti e pānui ana i ngā pūwetewete o te maramara whakairoiro, `immuch_desktop_video`, me te kawenga mahi C++ ōrite:

   ```bat
   flutter pub get
   REM try it, with hot reload: a debug build, with a profile of its own (Immuch360 Desktop Debug), so it runs next to the app
   flutter run -d windows -t lib/main_desktop.dart
   REM the app, in build\windows\x64\runner\Release\immuch360.exe
   flutter build windows --release -t lib/main_desktop.dart
   ```

3. Ka rere te kōpaki `Release` i te PC nāna i hanga. Mō tētahi atu PC, puritia te kōpaki katoa, ka tāpiri i te wāhanga whakahaere Visual C++ ki te taha o `immuch360.exe`. Mai i te pūtake o te tārua, ka tāruatia e te hōtuhi pūtea, ka waiho ngā mea mō Android anake, ka tāpiri i ngā raihana o te pūrei ataata, ka tirotiro kei te kōpaki, kei Windows tonu rānei ia DLL ka utaina e te taupānga, ā, ka hanga i te ZIP:

   ```bat
   python .github\desktop\windows_bundle.py --release mobile\build\windows\x64\runner\Release --out <folder> --zip <file.zip>
   ```

Ka rere te CI o Immuch360 Desktop (`.github/workflows/immuch360-desktop.yml`) i te peka `desktop`, e whai ana i `immuch360`: ngā kēti waea (kāore he mea e panoni i ngā hanga waea) me te huinga whakamātautau katoa i Linux, ngā whakamātautau papamahi i Windows, me te ZIP ōrite, kātahi i runga i taua hanga ngā whakamātautau e hiahia ana ki tōna libmpv me ANGLE (te takiuru o te tirohanga ora Tapo, ngā mea kāore e āhei kia whakatuwheratia e tētahi kōnae ataata, ngā atarangi 360°); ka hanga tāna mahi Linux i te taupānga i Ubuntu, ā, ka whakahiato tāna mahi macOS me Xcode 26.3 me te SDK macOS 26 (e rua me te `-t lib/main_desktop.dart` ōrite), hei arowhai whakahiato anake: kāore anō tētahi kia tīmataria i aua pūnaha. Ka hanga anō tētahi rerenga mahi tuarua (`.github/workflows/immuch360-libmpv.yml`) i te libmpv mō Windows x64 me arm64 mai i ngā pūtake kua titia, ā, ka whakaputa i ngā pūranga me ō rātou SHA-256; kei ngā ZIP tonu te pūranga media-kit tae noa ki te whakakātanga o taua hanga, nō reira ka pā hoki ngā tepe o te libmpv 2024 i [Kāore anō kia tae](#not-there-yet) ki tō ake hanga.

<a id="build-it-yourself"></a>
## Hangaia e koe anō

Ko te mekameka hanga ko tā Immich waea (Flutter 3.47, ka whakahaerehia ki a [mise](https://mise.jdx.dev)):

```bash
git clone https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360.git
cd Immuch360/mobile
mise install
mise run install
mise run codegen
flutter build apk --release --flavor phone                                   # phones: the universal APK of the GitHub releases (add --split-per-abi for the per ABI files)
flutter build appbundle --release --flavor phone                             # the App Bundle sent to Google Play (also mise run build:android)
flutter build apk --release --flavor quest --target-platform android-arm64 --android-project-arg arm64only=true   # Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S: the -quest-release.apk of the releases (also mise run build:quest)
flutter build ios --release                                                  # iPhone and iPad, on a Mac with Xcode and your own signing team
```

Ka tangohia ngā hopuāhua toa i ngā hanga kaiwhaihanga patuiro i hangaia ki a `--dart-define=IMMUCH_SCREENSHOTS=true`, ka huna noa i te haki patuiro.

He taupānga ōrite ngā kakara Android e rua. Mai i te hanga 20 ka kī anō te `phone` he taupānga pouaka whakaata ia (he tāurunga kaiwhakarewa pouaka whakaata me tētahi haki, kāore he mata pā e hiahiatia ana), ka waiho e te `quest`.

Ko te `quest` ka whāia te SDK 34, ā, ka pupuri noa i ngā whakaaetanga e whakamahia ana e te pōtae (ngā whakaahua, ngā ataata, ngā pānui): ko te whakahaere pāpāho, te tauwāhi papamuri, te penapena tawhito, te ororongo, te tauwāhi pāpāho, te tauwāhi pūrere me te kāmera kua tangohia i `android/app/src/quest/AndroidManifest.xml`, nō te mea ka whakakāhore te Meta Horizon Store i ngā mea tuatahi e rua, ā, ka tono i tētahi parahau mō ia whakaaetanga tairongo atu; ka whakaingoa taua kōnae anō i te Quest 2, te Quest Pro, te Quest 3 me te 3S hei pūrere e tautokona ana, ā, ka here i te HTTP noa ki te pōtae tonu me ngā ingoa o te whatunga kāinga. He 64 moka anake te APK nā ngā tohenga tāpiri e rua o tōna rārangi tono (`--target-platform android-arm64 --android-project-arg arm64only=true`). Ko te `phone` te mea e hiahiatia ana e Google Play.

Hei hanga mō iOS i tō ake Mac, whakamahia a Xcode me tō ake tīma haina; me Xcode 26, whakahaerehia a `xcodebuild -downloadComponent MetalToolchain` kotahi te wā i te tuatahi, nō te mea e hiahiatia ana e ngā kaiwhakamarumaru Spatial. Me te kore Mac, ka rere ngā hanga iOS i Codemagic (he Mac kua manaakitia) mai i te kōnae `codemagic.yaml` o tēnei pūkete. Ka rere ngā hanga tuku Android i GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/immuch360-release.yml`).

Ka hangaia a Immuch360 Desktop, te putanga Windows, mai i ngā pūtake ōrite, te peka `immuch360`, me Flutter mō Windows: kei [Hangaia e koe anō i Windows](#build-it-yourself-on-windows) ngā hipanga.

Kāore he muna e noho ana i tēnei pūkete: ka penapenatia te kī haina Android hei muna GitHub Actions kua whakamunatia, ā, ka penapenatia ngā rauemi haina Apple hei taurangi kua whakamunatia i Codemagic. Ka tohu noa ngā kōnae rerenga mahi ki a rātou mā te ingoa. Me te kore tō ake `android/key.jks`, ka hainatia tētahi hanga tuku ki te kī patuiro, ā, kāore e taea te tāuta ki runga i tētahi tārua nō GitHub, nō tētahi toa rānei (wetetāutahia tērā i te tuatahi); ka tāuta tētahi hanga patuiro i tōna taha hei Immuch360 debug. Ko te tārua Meta Horizon Store ko te APK `quest` o te tuku kua hainatia ki tētahi atu kī, te kī i rēhitatia tuatahitia ai te taupānga toa, nō reira kāore hoki e taea te tāuta ki runga i tētahi APK kua sideload, i te huringa kōaro rānei.

## Ngā rangitaki

I Android me te Quest, ka whakaatu a `adb logcat` i te mātaki rumaki o te pōtae i raro i te tūtohu `Immuch360` (mai i te hanga 19 ngā karu e rua hoki o tētahi whakaahua mokowā Apple: te āhua o te wetewete i te karu tuarua, te tapahi, te wāhi i whakatakotoria ai te whakaahua), ngā arowhai pūwetewete i raro i `VideoDecoders`, te tuitui o tētahi ataata mata taha ki te taha i raro i `DualFisheyeEffect`, me te purei arotahi rua o te hanga 18 i raro i `TwoLensPlayer`, `TwoLensCompositor` me `LensVideoRenderer`:

```bash
adb logcat -c
# reproduce the problem, then
adb logcat -d -v time -s Immuch360 VideoDecoders DualFisheyeEffect TwoLensPlayer TwoLensCompositor LensVideoRenderer
adb logcat -d -v time > quest-full.log      # everything, including crashes and decoder errors (it can contain your server address, check before sharing)
adb logcat -d -v time -s PhoneShareService PhoneShareApi   # on the phone that shares itself (from build 19)
```

Mai i te hanga 19 ka tuhi anō te kiritaki DLNA, te tiritiri waea me te kitenga o te pāpāho mokowā Apple ki te rangitaki ake o te taupānga (“Logs” (Rangitaki), i te tahua o te pikitia kōtaha kei runga matau), i raro i `Ssdp`, `DlnaFileSystem`, `NetworkBrowserPage`, `PhoneShare`, `PhoneShareServer`, `AppleSpatialService`, `HeicStereoProbe` me `NetworkMediaService`. Mai i te hanga 20 ka tuhi te aratau pouaka whakaata ki reira i raro i `TvMode` me `TvTextEntry`, ngā tūmau Plex i raro i `Gdm`, `UdpTransport`, `PlexClient`, `PlexFileSystem` me `PlexServerEditPage`, ā, ngā kāmera Tapo i raro i `TapoDiscovery`, `TapoHttps`, `TapoLogin`, `TapoControl`, `TapoMedia`, `TapoFileSystem`, `TapoTest`, `TapoCamera`, `CameraPage`, `CameraEditPage` me `CameraLiveView`; kāore rawa ngā rārangi Plex e mau i te tohu, i tētahi wāhitau, i tētahi taitara rānei, ā, ka waiho e ngā rārangi kāmera ngā kupuhipa. Ka noho ngā rārangi rangitaki ki te pūrere ki te kore koe e tārua i a koe anō.

I tētahi rorohiko (Immuch360 Desktop), kei te whārangi “Logs” (Rangitaki) hoki te “Save logs to a file” (Tiaki i ngā rangitaki ki tētahi kōnae): te rangitaki, he ZIP rānei o te rangitaki me ngā pūrongo o ngā tukinga whakamutunga ina he mea anō (ka mutu tōna ingoa tūtohu ki te “with-crash-reports”). He minidump iti tētahi pūrongo tukinga: ngā miro, te wāhi i tū ai rātou me ngā mea anake e hiahiatia ana hei whai i ō rātou karanga, me ngā ingoa o ngā kōnae papatono engari kaua ō rātou kōpaki; kaua te pūmahara o te taupānga.

Mai i te hanga papamahi 2 ka tuhi te pūrei ataata o te rorohiko ki reira i raro i `DesktopVideo`, `DesktopPlayer`, `DesktopVideoController`, `PlayerPool` me `VideoThumbnailGrabber`, tae atu ki ngā whakatūpato ake a mpv, kua tangohia ngā tohu me ngā kupuhipa. Mai i te hanga papamahi 3 ka tuhi anō ia i te pūrei 360° i raro i `SphericalPlayer`, `SphereRenderer` me `PluginRenderer` (te rahi i kōwhiria, ngā anga ia hēkona i inea, te maramara whakairoiro), ngā ataata mata arotahi-rua i raro i `RawTwoStreams`, ngā pūwetewete o te maramara whakairoiro i raro i `DesktopGpuDecoders`, `DesktopVideoDecoderApi` me `DecoderMeasure`, me te tirohanga ora Tapo i raro i `DesktopCameraLive`, kāore ōna rārangi e pupuri i te pūkete kāmera, i tōna kupuhipa rānei. Tirohia te rangitaki i mua i te tiritiri: tērā pea kei roto tō wāhitau tūmau.
