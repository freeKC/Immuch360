# Immuch360

**Āu pāpāho. Ngā mata katoa. Ngā tirohanga katoa.**

He taupānga koreutu, puka tuwhera a Immuch360 hei kimi, hei tirotiro, hei whakatangi, hei torotoro i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata: ngā mea māori, me ngā mea horopaki, 360°, 3D, kaipuku hoki e whakaatuhia papatahi, ruarua, kumea rānei e ētahi atu taupānga. Ka pānuihia e ia i te wāhi e noho nei, i runga i te taputapu tonu, i te NAS, i te rorohiko, i te tūmau Plex, i te tūmau Immich rānei, ā, ka whakatangihia i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, papapātuhi, pouaka whakaata, ngā taputapu mahunga Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko. Kāore he pūkete, he kapua, he tūmau e hiahiatia.

- **He aha tāna mahi?** Ka whakaatuhia āu whakaahua me āu kiriata hei whare whakaahua, ā, ka whakatangihia i te kaimātakitaki e tau ai ki tēnā, ki tēnā kōnae: papatahi, hei porotaka ka titiro haere koe, i roto i te 3D, i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga.
- **He aha ngā pāpāho?** He whakaahua me he kiriata papatahi, he whakaahua whānui, he whakaahua me he kiriata 360°, VR180, 3D taurua-tirohanga, he whakaahua mokowā a Apple, ngā porotaka 360° o ngā wakarererangi DJI, me ngā kōnae mata o ngā kāmera 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), e tuitui nei te taupānga.
- **Kei runga i ēhea taputapu?** Waea pūkoro me papapātuhi Android, iPhone me iPad, Android TV me Google TV, ngā pouaka whakaata LG me webOS (he taupānga motuhake, te hanganga whakamātau tuatahi), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 me te 3S, Windows (he whakaaturanga), me Linux me macOS hei ngā whakaaturanga tōmua.
- **Me whai tūmau, pūkete rānei ahau?** Kāo. Ka mahi tonu a Immuch360 anake ki ngā pāpāho o tō taputapu. He kōwhiringa te tūmau Immich, te NAS, te tūmau pāpāho, te tūmau Plex rānei, e tāpirihia e koe ina hiahiatia e koe.

## Ka taea e koe te aha

Ka kōrero ia whakamahinga i raro nei mō te mea ka riro i a koe, kātahi ka whakaatu me pēhea te mahi i roto i te iti noa o nga taonga. Ko te aratohu katoa, me ōna kōwhiringa, karere, me ōna here katoa, kei te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Mātakitaki i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata

Ko Immuch360 i te tuatahi he whare whakaahua, he kaiwhakatangi mō ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata māori. Ko te rārangi wā, ngā kohinga, ngā tino pai, ngā maharatanga me te rapu e mahi ana me tētahi tūmau Immich; ko ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata ake o te taputapu, me ngā kōpaki o tētahi rorohiko, ka kitea me te kore tūmau. Ka whakatangihia ngā kiriata i roto i tētahi kaiwhakatangi māori me te oro, te rapu wāhi, tētahi tohu pūhiki, ā, i roto i ngā kaiwhakatangi 360°, Mokowā, rorohiko hoki, he kōwhiringa ara oro. I runga i te pouaka whakaata ko te pou whakahau e whakahaere ana i te katoa; i runga i te rorohiko ko te kiore me te papapātuhi.

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa Whakaahua: te rārangi wā o tō tūmau, o te taputapu rānei me te kore tūmau.
2. Pāwhiritia tētahi whakaahua, kiriata rānei kia tuwhera; paki whakatehau, paki whakamatau hoki kia haere ki tērā e whai ake, kōpiri, pāwhiri rua rānei kia whakanui.
3. Paki ki runga i runga i tētahi kiriata mō ōna taipitopito: codec, rahi, tere papamata, reiti pūhiki, me te mea ka wetewetehia tēnei e te taputapu, kāore rānei.

Ētahi atu: [ngā kaimātakitaki me te kaiwhakatangi ataata](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [taipitopito ataata me ngā kaiwetewete](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Hurihia tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi

Hurihia tētahi whakaahua 90° ki te pēhi kotahi, me te kore e whakatuwhera i tētahi ētita: he pātene, he pātene papapātuhi, he pātene tae rānei o te pou whakahau o te pouaka whakaata, i ia pūhara. Ka hurihia e te taupānga Immich tētahi whakaahua i roto i tōna ētita; ka pērā a Immuch360 ki te pātene kotahi, ā, mō āu ake whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka haere mā taua ētita tūmau anō, nā reira ka whakaatu te taupānga tukutuku me ōu taputapu kē atu i aua whakaahua kua hurihia. Ko ētahi atu whakaahua ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake. Kāore rawa ngā whakaahua 360°, 180° me te 3D e hurihia, kia noho tika tonu ai ngā tirohanga rukuhanga.

1. **Waea pūkoro, papapātuhi me te matapihi Meta Quest**: whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, ā, pāwhiritia Hurihia i te rārangi o raro, i mua i Whakatika, kia hurihia ki te ahunga o te karaka; mā te pēhi roa ka huri ki tērā atu taha. Mā te papapātuhi, ko R me Shift+R.
2. **Android TV me Google TV**: ka hurihia e te pātene kōwhai o te pou whakahau te whakaahua ki te ahunga o te karaka, ā, e te pātene kikorangi ki te ahunga kōaro, ahakoa kei hea te arotahi i te kaimātakitaki. I runga i tētahi pou whakahau kāore he pātene tae, pēhia Raro: ka puta te pae i raro i te whakaahua me Hurihia kua tīpakohia (kotahi Matau te tawhiti ina whai pātene kupu te whakaahua), ā, ka hurihia te whakaahua e ia OK.
3. **Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)**: kōwhai me kikorangi pērā i Android TV, R me Shift+R i runga i te papapātuhi, te pātene huri rānei o ngā mana whakahaere o te whakaahua.
4. **Windows, Linux me macOS**: ka huri a R ki te ahunga o te karaka, a Shift+R ki te ahunga kōaro, te pātene huri rānei i te pae o runga, i mua i te mata katoa.
5. **Ngā whakaahua o tētahi tiritiri whatunga, o Plex, o tētahi kāmera rānei**: te pātene huri tuatahi i te pae o runga, R me Shift+R, ngā pātene kōwhai me kikorangi rānei.

Kei hea te hurihanga e tiakina ai: ko tētahi whakaahua o tō ake pūkete i runga i Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka hurihia mā te ētita whakaahua o te tūmau, e pupuri ana i te kōnae taketake, e hanga ana i tētahi whakaahua kua hurihia (ka puta he pae angiangi i a ia e mahi ana; ka whā ngā hurihanga, kotahi rānei te hurihanga whakamuri, ka hoki ki te taketake). Ko ngā whakaahua o te taputapu, o tētahi tiritiri, o tētahi hoa, o tētahi kohinga tiritiri rānei, me ia whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau tawhito ake, ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake, mō taua whakaahua anake, ā, kāore rawa te kōnae e whakarerekētia; ka kī te taupānga i tēnei kotahi te wā mō tētahi whakaahua Immich. I roto i ngā taupānga Android, iOS me te rorohiko, ka kitea tētahi hurihanga kua tiakina i runga i te taputapu i te kaimātakitaki, kāore anō i ngā whakaahua iti, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me ngā maharatanga. Kāore he Hurihia mō ngā kiriata, te kōpaki kua rakaina, me ia whakaahua 360°, 180°, 3D rānei.

<a id="explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d"></a>
### Torotoro i ngā whakaahua whānui, 360°, VR180 me te 3D

Ka tuwhera tētahi whakaahua 360° hei porotaka ka hurihia e koe mā tētahi matihao, mā te nekehia rānei o te waea pūkoro; ka whakatangihia tētahi kiriata 360° i roto i tētahi kaiwhakatangi porotaka me te oro; ka tuhia ngā kōnae VR180 ki runga i tētahi hawhe porotaka; ka whakaatu ngā kōnae 3D taurua-tirohanga i tētahi karu i runga i te mata papatahi, i ngā karu e rua i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga. Ka pupuri ngā whakaahua whānui-whāiti i tō rātou tapahanga, ā, ka tīmata te pikitia i te tirohanga i hopukina e te kāmera. Ka taea tētahi kōnae kāore e tohuhia e te tūmau te tiro hei 360° mā te pāwhiri kotahi.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-sphere.jpg" width="260" alt="He whakaahua 360° o te taha roto i te mātaki pōro: te pātene kati kei runga mauī, ngā pātene 360°, tahora 3D me te pūhurihuri kei runga matau">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-video.png" width="420" alt="He ataata 360° o tētahi rori takutai e purei ana i te pūrei 360° o tētahi waea e puritia whakapae ana: kati me te ingoa kōnae kei runga mauī, 360° me 3D kei runga matau, o mua, peke whakamuri, pūrei, peke whakamua me o muri kei waenganui, te pae wā kei raro">
</p>

1. Whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, te kiriata rānei. Ko ngā kōnae 360° ka mau i tētahi paetohu 360° i runga i tō rātou whakaahua iti, ā, kei te ripa Kohinga tētahi rārangi 360° e kohi ana i ēnei, me ngā tātari mā te wā, te pūtake, te momo, me te kāmera.
2. Pāwhiritia te 360° i te papa o runga. Toia kia titiro haere, kōpiri kia whakanui, pāwhiritia te pātene pūhiki-haurangi kia titiro haere mā te huri i te waea pūkoro.
3. Ka pōkaikaha te pātene 3D i waenga i "Mono (ehara i te 3D)", "3D, runga me raro", me "3D, taha ki te taha"; ka whakawhiti te pātene 360°/180° i waenga i te porotaka katoa me te hawhe porotaka o VR180.
4. Mō tētahi kōnae e whakaatu papatahi ana ahakoa he 360° tāna: ⋮ i te pokapū matau o runga, kātahi "Tirohia hei 360°".

I runga i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā papapātuhi, ka taea anō e tētahi kiriata 3D taurua-tirohanga te whakawhiwhi i te hōhonutanga ki runga i te mata papatahi: ka whakamahia e te kaiwhakatangi Mokowā 2.5D te kāmera o mua kia aru i tō māhunga, ā, ka whakaatu i te tirohanga i waenganui i ngā karu e rua (arotakenga). - **Ngā āhuatanga o ia pūnaha**: te pūhiki-haurangi me te kaiwhakatangi Mokowā 2.5D i runga i ngā waea pūkoro; te tirohanga rukuhanga, ngā rengarenga, me ō ringaringa i runga i te Quest; te tahora o te pou whakahau, te kōrerorero papapātuhi, me te haki o te pouaka whakaata i runga i Android TV; i runga i te rorohiko, te kiore me te papapātuhi, te mata katoa, tētahi matapihi kotahi, he kōnae me he kōpaki i whakatuwherahia mā te hōtaka, i whakataka rānei ki runga i tōna matapihi, e pānuihia ana pēnei i ngā kōnae o ō kōpaki, ngā HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, me ngā whakaahua mata a te kāmera o ngā kōpaki, ina whai kaiwetewete a Windows mō aua mea, me te whakamarumaru raraunga a Windows mō ngā kupuhipa me ngā tohu.
- **Matatapu nā te hanga**: kāore he pūkete, he kapua, he tātari rānei; ko ngā kupuhuna me ngā tohu kei roto i te rokiroki haumaru o te taputapu; ko te kāmera o te kaiwhakatangi Mokowā ka whakamahia i runga i te taputapu anake; ko ngā tiritiri, Plex, me ngā kāmera ka whakapāhia i tō whatunga anake.

<a id="install"></a>
## Whakauru

### Waea pūkoro me papapātuhi Android

1. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aprogsys.immuch360), te APK rānei o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases): `Immuch360-v<version>-arm64-v8a-release.apk` mō tētahi waea pūkoro; ka mahi te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui ki ngā wāhi katoa.
2. Ka whakaurua te taupānga i te taha o te taupānga Immich whaimana. Kāore te APK mai i GitHub e whakahou ake ana i a ia anō: tirotirohia te wharangi Releases; ina kua whakaurua e koe te taupānga mai i tētahi toa, tangohia ngā whakahoutanga mai i tērā toa.

### iPhone me iPad

1. Kei te tatari te arotakenga App Store ki te arotake a Apple. I mua i tōna tukunga, hangaia te taupānga mā Xcode i runga i tētahi Mac, tirohia [Hangaia ake ko koe](../docs/immuch360/build.md).
2. Pāwhiritia "Whakamahia me te kore tūmau", "Tūhono ki tētahi tūmau Immich" rānei kia takiuru mā tō tūmau; kei te whārangi haere mai raua, mai i te hanganga 24.

<a id="android-tv-and-google-tv"></a>
### Android TV me Google TV

1. I mua i te tuku a Google Play i te taupānga i runga i ngā pouaka whakaata, tangohia te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. I te pouaka whakaata, whakakāngia ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake me te patuiro USB (i tētahi Google TV: Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Mō, tīpakohia te “Android TV OS build” e whitu ngā wā, kātahi ko Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake; he rerekē ngā ingoa i ia pouaka whakaata).
3. Mai i tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite, me te adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, whakaaetia te tono i te pouaka whakaata, kātahi ko `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Ka puta te taupānga i waenga i ngā taupānga o te pouaka whakaata, me tōna haki. Tāutahia te tuku e whai ake ana mā te huarahi ōrite: ka pupuri a `-r` i te takiuru me ngā tautuhinga.

<a id="lg-tvs-webos"></a>
### Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)

1. Me whai koe i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG me webOS 4.0, tērā hōu ake rānei, tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite me tētahi pūkete kaiwhakawhanake LG koreutu. I te pouaka whakaata, whakaurua te Developer Mode mai i te LG Content Store, takiuru, whakakāngia te Dev Mode Status, ā, tukuna te pouaka whakaata kia tīmata anō; tuhia tōna wāhitau IP me te kīanga huna e whakaaturia ana e te taupānga.
2. I te rorohiko, whakaurua te webOS CLI (Node.js 18, tērā hōu ake rānei): `npm install -g @webos-tools/cli`, kātahi rēhitatia te pouaka whakaata mā `ares-setup-device` (tōna wāhitau IP, tauranga 9922, kaiwhakamahi `prisoner`) me `ares-novacom --device <name> --getkey`, ka tono i te kīanga huna.
3. Tangohia `Immuch360-webOS-0.1.0.ipk` mai i te [putanga tōmua webOS](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/webos-v0.1.0), tirohia ki `SHA256SUMS.txt`, kātahi ko `ares-install --device <name> Immuch360-webOS-0.1.0.ipk`. Ka pērā anō te [webOS Dev Manager](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop) a webosbrew, me te kore rārangi tono.
4. Ka roa te wātū Developer Mode ki te 1000 hāora, ā, ka whakahoutia e te pātene EXTEND o te taupānga Developer Mode; ina pau, ka tangohia e te pouaka whakaata ngā taupānga i whakaurua pēnei. Kei [webos/README.md](../webos/README.md#install-on-a-tv-in-developer-mode) ngā taipitopito.

<a id="meta-quest"></a>
### Meta Quest

1. Mai i te [Horizon Store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/immuch360/1384208401440714/): te arotakenga toa, e whakahoutia ana i a Meta e arotake ana i ia tukunga.
2. Uta-taha rānei i te kōnae `-quest-release.apk` o tētahi [tukunga](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases) i roto i te aratau kaihanga mā te adb, mā te SideQuest rānei: `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-quest-release.apk`, kātahi whakatuwheratia te Kohinga o te taputapu mahunga, "Unknown sources".
3. Kua hainatia te arotakenga toa me te APK a GitHub mā ngā kī rerekē: kia huri mai i tētahi ki tērā, whakakorehia te taupānga i mua, ka ngaro i konā te takiuru, ngā tautuhinga, me te rārangi tiritiri whatunga.

<a id="windows"></a>
### Windows

1. Tangohia `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.7-windows-x64-setup.exe` mai i te [putanga tōmua o te desktop](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-7) (me `SHA256SUMS.txt` hei tirotiro), kātahi whakatuwheratia. Kāore anō ngā kōnae kia hainatia: ka whakaatu pea a Windows SmartScreen i te "Windows protected your PC", kōwhiria "More info" kātahi ko "Run anyway".
2. Kōwhiria "Install for me only" (kāore he mana whakahaere), "Install for all users" rānei, kātahi ko te reo o te kaiwhakauru, ko tērā kua tīpakohia kē e Windows.
3. Tīmata i a Immuch360 Desktop mai i te Start menu. Ka kitea ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata i roto i te "Open with" (pāwhiri matau ki tētahi kōnae, Open with); kāore rawa ia e noho hei hōtaka taunoa mō aua mea. Ka taea e te tīmatanga tuatahi o tētahi hanganga hōu te tae atu ki te meneti kotahi, i a Windows e hopu ana i ngā kōnae.
4. Windows 10, 11 rānei, 64 piti. Kia whakahoutia, whakatuwheratia te kaiwhakauru o te hanganga e whai ake nei: ka noho tonu tō takiuru, ō kōpaki, me ō tautuhinga. He hōtaka ōrite te ZIP o te tukunga ōrite ki tētahi kōpaki, e taea ai te wetewete ki hea noa, kātahi ka tīmata i `immuch360.exe` mai i reira.

### Linux

I runga i Ubuntu 22.04, tērā hōu ake rānei, me Debian 12, tērā hōu ake rānei: `sudo apt install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.7-linux-amd64.deb`; i runga i Fedora 40, tērā hōu ake rānei: `sudo dnf install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.7-linux-x86_64.rpm`, mai i te [putanga tōmua o te desktop](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-7) ēnei e rua. Kātahi tīmata i a Immuch360 Desktop mai i āu taupānga. He whakaaturanga: ngā kōpaki o te rorohiko, he whakaahua me he whakaahua 360° hei porotaka, me ngā kiriata papatahi mā te libmpv o te pūnaha, ka whakaurua e te mōkihi (ka papatahi te whakatangi o ngā kiriata 360°, 3D me ngā kiriata mata, me tētahi karere); ka mahi ngā tiritiri SMB mai i Ubuntu 22.04 me Debian 12 whakarunga. I runga i Fedora, me whai ngā kiriata kāmera i te FFmpeg o RPM Fusion. Ki te kore he GNOME Keyring, he KWallet rānei, ka puritia ngā kupuhipa mō te wātū anake, ā, ka kī pērā ngā tautuhinga o te taupānga.

### macOS

Whakatuwheratia `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.7-macos.dmg` mai i te [putanga tōmua o te desktop](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-7), kātahi tōia te taupānga ki Applications (Apple silicon me Intel, macOS 12, tērā hōu ake rānei). Kāore anō kia hainatia, kia whakamanatia rānei: Pāwhiri Control ki runga, kōwhiria Open i runga i macOS 14, me ngā mea tawhito ake, kōwhiria rānei "Open Anyway" i roto i System Settings, Privacy & Security i runga i macOS 15, tērā hōu ake rānei. Kāore he Mac kei tētahi o te hunga e mahi ana i tēnei kaupapa: i whakahaerehia noatia te taupānga i runga i te Mac CI o te kaupapa, i reira ka whakatuwhera ia i ngā kōnae me ngā kōpaki, ka whakatangi i ngā kiriata papatahi mā tōna ake libmpv; kāore anō tōna urunga whatunga kia whakamātauria. E tautokona ana ngā pūrongo.

## Mahere ara

Ngā mea kāore anō kia oti, ko te mea tūpono rawa i te tuatahi. Ehara tētahi mea i konei i te kupu taurangi, ā, ka āwhina ngā urupare i te [pūaroturuki take](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) ki te whakatau he aha ka tae tuatahi.

- **Ngā rorohiko**: te whakamātau ā-ringa i ia taumahi i runga i tētahi PC Windows; kātahi ko te kaiwhakaatu kiriata 360° i runga i Linux me macOS, he whakamātau i runga i tētahi Mac pono me tētahi PC Linux pono; kātahi he hanganga kua hainatia me ngā whakahōunga aunoa; kātahi ko Mokowā 2.5D me te kāmera tukutuku.
- **Ngā pouaka whakaata**: he whakamātau i te hanganga 27 i runga i tētahi Android TV pono; te taupānga LG webOS i runga i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG pono, kātahi ōna kiriata 360° ki runga i te porotaka me ngā tiritiri whatunga, Plex me ngā kāmera Tapo; he waehere QR hei hono i tētahi pouaka whakaata, rorohiko rānei ki tētahi waea pūkoro, mā te mata nui e whakaatu, mā te waea e matawai.

Ko te rārangi katoa, me ngā tirohanga taputapu e toe ana mō ia āhuatanga, kei [Te tūnga tuku](../docs/immuch360/release-status.md#roadmap).
