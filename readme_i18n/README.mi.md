# Immuch360

**Āu pāpāho. Ngā mata katoa. Ngā tirohanga katoa.**

Ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata papatahi, 360° me te 3D mai i tō taputapu, tō NAS, tō tūmau Immich, āu kāmera me tō wakarererangi. Kāore he pūkete e hiahiatia ana. He taupānga koreutu, puna tuwhera hoki a Immuch360, ka pānui i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata i te wāhi e noho ana rātou, ka whakatangi i ia mea i te kaimātakitaki e tika ana mōna: papatahi, hei porotaka ka taea e koe te titiro huri noa, i te 3D, huri noa rānei i a koe i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga. Ka rere i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, ngā papahiko, ngā pouaka whakaata, ngā taputapu mahunga Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko.

**He mea hōu i ngā hanganga 33 ki te 42.** [Ka haere tonu ngā kiriata i te wāhi i mutu ai koe](#pick-up-a-film-where-you-stopped). Ka tīmata wawe ake rātou, ā, mai i tētahi tūmau pōturi [ka haere tonu te whakatangi, kāore e mutu](#when-a-video-waits-or-will-not-play). Ka [topa atu te rārangi wā ki ngā marama me ngā tau](#find-a-month-or-a-year-in-a-long-timeline) i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā rorohiko. I tētahi waea Android, i tētahi Meta Quest rānei, ka whakatuwhera a ["Tirotiro i tētahi kōpaki"](#browse-a-folder-of-your-phone-or-headset) i tētahi kōpaki o te pūrere pērā i tētahi tiritiri, ā, ka kitea ngā whakaahua TIFF. Ka [rapu a Android TV mā te rā me te pou whakahau](#on-android-tv-and-google-tv), ka [whakatuwhera tētahi Meta Quest 3, 3S rānei i ngā Gaussian splats](#explore-gaussian-splats-in-a-meta-quest-3-or-3s) (whakamātautau), e hurihia ana mā te rākau koromatua matau, me te SPZ putanga 4 hoki, ā, ka [hanga tētahi rorohiko i ngā putanga 3D o ngā whakaahua o tō NAS](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama). I runga i tētahi rorohiko pōnaho Windows e rua ōna matū whakaahua, [ka rere te taupānga ināianei i runga i te matū tere](#when-a-video-waits-or-will-not-play), nō reira ka maeneene te whakatangi o ngā kiriata 8K. Ka tuwhera ināianei ngā Gaussian splats [i runga i ngā rorohiko, i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā papahiko Android](#explore-gaussian-splats-on-your-phone-tablet-or-computer), ka [hanga tētahi rorohiko Windows i ngā atamira splat mai i āu whakaahua, i tētahi ataata rānei](#make-a-splat-scene-from-your-photos-or-a-video-ai) mā te AI, ka [pupuri tō tūmau Immich i aua atamira ki roto i ngā whakaahua](#keep-a-splat-scene-on-your-immich-server) ka tuwhera mai i tō rārangi wā, ā, ka [whakatangi ngā rorohiko i ngā ataata AV2](#play-av2-videos-on-your-computer), he whakamātautau katoa. Mai i te hanganga 42, ka [whakatangihia ngā ataata Insta360 mata o tō kohinga Immich kua whakapūmautia ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera](#stabilise-your-360-videos-with-the-cameras-gyroscope-experimental) i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, ngā papahiko me ngā pouaka whakaata Android, ka [tuku te kaihanga splat o te hanganga desktop 22 i tētahi kounga taipitopito](#make-a-splat-scene-from-your-photos-or-a-video-ai) mō ngā atamira e titiro tata ana koe, ā, ka [tīmata tētahi whakaahua whānui 360° ina wātea te 3.5 GiB o te kāri whakairoiro](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama), he whakamātautau katoa. Kei [te App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/immuch360-360-3d-player/id6817655965) te taupānga iPhone me iPad, putanga 3.3.2.

## Mā wai

- **Ka hopu koe i te 360°, i te 3D rānei** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te titiro huri noa i roto i āu hopunga, kaua ki te kite papatahi, tāruarua, toro rānei: [360°, VR180 me te 3D](#explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d).
- **Ka hopu koe ki tētahi kāmera 360° i tō ringa, i runga i tētahi pahikara, i runga rānei i tētahi pōtae mārō** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia whakatangihia ngā ataata mata o tō kāmera Insta360, kua puritia ki Immich, kia papatahi te pae, kia mārie hoki i tō waea pūkoro me tō pouaka whakaata (whakamātautau): [kua whakapūmautia ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera](#stabilise-your-360-videos-with-the-cameras-gyroscope-experimental).
- **Kei runga āu whakaahua me āu kiriata i tētahi NAS, rorohiko, Plex, Immich rānei** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia kitea i te pouaka whakaata me te kore tārua: [i te pouaka whakaata](#watch-on-the-tv-with-the-remote), [NAS, tiritiri me Plex](#play-the-files-of-a-nas-a-computer-a-media-server-or-plex).
- **He Meta Quest tāu** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te tomo ki roto i āu ake whakaahua me āu kiriata: [i te taputapu mahunga](#step-inside-your-photos-and-videos-with-a-meta-quest).
- **He papatahi āu whakaahua**, ā, e hiahia ana koe kia 3D, hei atamira hei whakatata atu i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga, i roto rānei i tētahi whakaahua whānui 360°, me te kore kāmera 3D: [hurihia tētahi whakaahua hei 3D, hei atamira, hei whakaahua whānui](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama), he mea hōu, nā te AI i hanga i runga i tō ake rorohiko.
- **Ka whakahaere koe i a Immich** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tōna taupānga, me ngā kaimātakitaki rukuhanga me ngā mata nui ka takiuru koe mai i tō waea pūkoro: [tō kohinga Immich](#keep-your-immich-library-with-more), [ka whakatauritea ki a Immich](#compared-with-immich).
- **Ka tiaki koe i tō kāinga ki ngā kāmera Tapo** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia noho tahi rātou me āu whakaahua, i te pouaka whakaata anō hoki (i tirohia ki tētahi kāmera whakataruna): [kāmera kāinga](#watch-your-home-cameras-on-the-big-screen).
- **Ka rere koe i tētahi wakarererangi DJI** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tōna pikitia ora i tō waea pūkoro me te kore DJI Fly, i tētahi Meta Quest, i tētahi Android TV rānei mō te hunga e noho tahi ana me koe (whakamātautau): [tō wakarererangi](#see-what-your-drone-sees-on-your-phone).
- **Ka hopu koe i ngā wāhi me ngā mea i te 3D, hei Gaussian splats,** e hiahia ana rānei koe ki te hanga i aua mea mai i āu ake whakaahua, ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te hīkoi i roto i a rātou i tō waea pūkoro, i tō rorohiko, i tētahi taputapu mahunga rānei (whakamātautau): [i tētahi waea pūkoro, papahiko, rorohiko rānei](#explore-gaussian-splats-on-your-phone-tablet-or-computer), [i hangaia mai i āu whakaahua](#make-a-splat-scene-from-your-photos-or-a-video-ai), [i puritia ki Immich](#keep-a-splat-scene-on-your-immich-server).
- **He ataata AV2 āu**, te hōputu e whai ana i a AV1, ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te mātakitaki i a rātou i tō rorohiko (whakamātautau): [AV2 i tō rorohiko](#play-av2-videos-on-your-computer).
- **He maha ō mata i te kāinga** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia mahi tahi rātou: [ngā mahi tahi a ō mata](#use-cases).

Kāore he pūkete Immuch360 hei waihanga, ā, kāore he mea e tae mai ki a mātou: ka kōrero noa te taupānga ki āu taputapu me ngā tūmau ka kōwhiria e koe, ā, ka tango i tētahi wāhanga AI, i te kaiwetewete AV2 rānei, anake ina tono koe.

2. Ka whakaurua te taupānga i te taha o te taupānga Immich whaimana. Kāore te APK mai i GitHub e whakahou ake ana i a ia anō: tirotirohia te wharangi Releases; ina kua whakaurua e koe te taupānga mai i tētahi toa, tangohia ngā whakahoutanga mai i tērā toa.

1. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/immuch360-360-3d-player/id6817655965): putanga 3.3.2, i whakaputaina i te 6 Whiringa-ā-rangi 2026. Hangaia rānei te taupānga mā Xcode i runga i tētahi Mac, tirohia [Hangaia ake ko koe](../docs/immuch360/build.md).
2. Pāwhiritia "Whakamahia me te kore tūmau", "Tūhono ki tētahi tūmau Immich" rānei kia takiuru mā tō tūmau; kei te whārangi haere mai raua, mai i te hanganga 24.

<a id="android-tv-and-google-tv"></a>
### Android TV me Google TV

1. I mua i te tuku a Google Play i te taupānga i runga i ngā pouaka whakaata, tangohia te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. I te pouaka whakaata, whakakāngia ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake me te patuiro USB (i tētahi Google TV: Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Mō, tīpakohia te “Android TV OS build” e whitu ngā wā, kātahi ko Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake; he rerekē ngā ingoa i ia pouaka whakaata).
3. Mai i tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite, me te adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, whakaaetia te tono i te pouaka whakaata, kātahi ko `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Ka puta te taupānga i waenga i ngā taupānga o te pouaka whakaata, me tōna haki. Tāutahia te tuku e whai ake ana mā te huarahi ōrite: ka pupuri a `-r` i te takiuru me ngā tautuhinga.

### Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)

1. Kei te arotakehia te putanga 1.0.0 e LG. Kia tae rā anō ki te LG Content Store, ka whakaurua i te Developer Mode: me whai koe i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG me webOS 5.0, tērā hōu ake rānei, tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite me tētahi pūkete kaiwhakawhanake LG koreutu. I te pouaka whakaata, whakaurua te Developer Mode mai i te LG Content Store, takiuru, whakakāngia te Dev Mode Status, ā, tukuna te pouaka whakaata kia tīmata anō; tuhia tōna wāhitau IP me te kīanga huna e whakaaturia ana e te taupānga.

<a id="explore-gaussian-splats-in-a-meta-quest-3-or-3s"></a>
### Tūhurahia ngā Gaussian splats i tētahi Meta Quest 3, 3S rānei

He hopunga 3D tētahi Gaussian splat o tētahi wāhi, mea rānei, i hangaia ki Polycam, Scaniverse, Postshot, Luma me ētahi atu taputapu. I runga i tētahi Meta Quest 3, 3S rānei, ka whakatakotoria e Immuch360 ki mua i a koe, ki tō taha katoa rānei, i tōna ake rahi, ā, ka hīkoi koe huri noa, ka paheke rānei i roto. He whakamātautau, ā, kua whakakāngia hei taunoa.

1. Whakatakotoria ō splats .spz, .ply rānei ki tētahi tiritiri SMB, WebDAV rānei, ki tētahi kōpaki o te pōtae rānei ("Tirotiro i tētahi kōpaki", te whakatuwhera kōnae splat rānei i te tahua + o Ngā tiritiri whatunga). Ka puta mai i muri i ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata, me tētahi tohu splat 3D me te maha o ō rātou gaussian.
2. Whakatuwheratia tētahi, kātahi te tūhura i te atamira splat. Ka tū tētahi mea ki mua i a koe i te taumata o te kanohi, ka tuwhera tētahi wāhi huri noa i a koe. Ka paheke te rākau koromatua mauī i roto, ā, ka hurihia e te rākau koromatua matau te tirohanga 30° huri noa i a koe, me te pupuri i te wāhi i tae atu ai koe (ka hurihia e "Whakahuripokotia te huri a te rākau koromatua" ki tērā atu ahunga). Ka whakatakoto anō a "Tautuhi anō", i te paewhiri e whakaaturia ana e A, X, e te pātene kapo rānei, mai i te wāhi e tū ana koe, ā, ka hurihia e te pātene whakatika te mea ka tae mai e takoto ana, e kōaro ana rānei, ka maumaharatia mō taua kōnae.
3. Ka whakaatu tētahi splat i te 50,000 gaussian te nuinga, ā, ka whakaatu tētahi nui ake i ōna 50,000 kaha rawa; ka whakaae te kōwhiringa splat nui (whakamātautau), i Settings, Advanced, ki te 150,000.
4. Ka pānui te pōtae i ngā kōnae .ply ā-rua me te SPZ putanga 1 ki te 4, ko te putanga 4 mai i te hanganga 40.

Ka whakamahi ngā Gaussian splats i tētahi API whakamātautau o Meta Spatial SDK, kāore nei a Meta e tautoko, e kī taurangi rānei: ko Immuch360 anake te kawenga mō ngā hapa katoa. <a id="when-a-video-waits-or-will-not-play"></a>
### Ina tatari tētahi ataata, ina kore rānei e whakatangi

Ka whakatangi tētahi kiriata mai i tō tūmau, i tō NAS rānei i te kōnae e wetewetehia ana e tēnei pūrere, ā, ina me tatari, ka kī he aha, ā, mō te roa hea.

- **Ka whakatangihia te kōnae ka taea te wetewete.** Ka puritia e Immich te taketake o tētahi ataata, ā, ina whakawaehere anō, he kape hoki. Ka whakatangihia e te taupānga te mea e wetewetehia ana e tēnei pūrere: "E whakatangi ana i te taketake: ka nui ake te kape a te tūmau (H.264 5760 x 2880) i tā te kaiwetewete o tēnei pūrere". Ina kāore e taea e te tūmau te tuku rere i taua taketake, ka tikiake tuatahitia e te taupānga ("E tikiake ana i te taketake hei whakatangi: 45 %"); ina kāore tētahi o ngā kōnae e taea te wetewete, ka kī he aha hei huri i Immich. Kōrere whakarunga i runga i tētahi ataata: ka kī te kāri "Te mātāpuna ataata" ko tēhea kōnae e whakatangi ana, ā, he aha ai, ā, ka whakatangihia e tōna pātene tērā atu.
- **He hononga pōturi.** I raro i te tohu uta, "E uta ana: 2 Mbit/s o te 8.1 Mbit/s e hiahiatia ana e tēnei ataata" me "Ka tīmata hei roto i te 37 s pea": ka tatari te taupānga ki ngā mea e kawea mai ana e te hononga, kātahi ka whakatangi i ngā wāhanga roa ake. Ka tae mai "Ka tukuna e te tūmau tēnei ataata i te 2 Mbit/s, e hiahia ana ia ki te 8.1 Mbit/s: ka tū ia i ētahi wā" me te huarahi puta: i Immich, Administration, Video transcoding, he taumira ūnga iti ake, he pāpātanga paraiti mōrahi rānei.
- **He tūmau kua mutu.** "Kua mutu te tuku a te tūmau i tēnei ataata. Ka haere tonu ina whakautu te tūmau": ka tatari te kiriata ki te tūmau, ā, ka haere tonu ina whakautu ia. He pērā anō mō tētahi tiritiri.
- **He rorohiko pōnaho e rua ōna matū whakaahua.** Mai i te hanganga desktop 21, ka rere te taupānga Windows i runga i te matū tere: i runga i te rorohiko pōnaho o te kaituhi, ko tētahi kiriata 8K HEVC i whakaatu i te 15 ki te 19 pikitia ia hēkona i runga i te matū kua whakaurua, me ētahi pikitia pango, ināianei ka whakaatu i te 30 pea, kāore he pikitia pango kotahi. I ngā taipitopito o tētahi kiriata e whakatangi ana (pātuhi I), ka whakaingoa a "Te wetewete ataata" i te kāri whakairoiro me te kaiwetewete ("Pūmārō, kei NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU • d3d11va"), ā, mō tētahi kiriata 4K, 8K rānei, ina pupuri a Penapena pūngao i ngā tautuhinga whakairoiro o Windows i te taupānga ki te matū kua whakaurua, ka kī te rārangi ki hea kōwhiri ai i te Mahinga teitei: Tautuhinga Windows, Pūnaha, Whakaaturanga, Whakairoiro, kātahi ka tīmata anō i te taupānga. I runga pūhiko, ka pupuri a Penapena pūngao i reira i te taupānga ki te matū kua whakaurua, ā, ka penapena pūngao, ahakoa te utu ki te whakatangi 4K me 8K. I runga i tētahi matū kua whakaurua, he pai ake te whakatangi o te 8K i tō te hanganga desktop 20, engari ehara i te maeneene: 23 pea ngā pikitia ia hēkona, me ētahi pikitia pango.

Ka pupuri te iPhone me te iPad i ō rātou kaiwetewete pūnaha me ō rātou ake ture tatari. I tirohia i runga i tētahi whakatinana Android ki tētahi tūmau me tētahi tiritiri i whakapōturitia ki waenga i te 0.6 me te 3 Mbit/s: ka whakatangi ināianei ngā kiriata i whakarerea e te hanganga 38 i muri i te 20 s, ā, i haere tonu te whakatangi o tētahi kiriata i noho wahangū tōna tūmau mō te meneti kotahi, 1.3 s i muri i te whakautu a te tūmau.

Me whai aua tūtohu a Immich: ko te kōwhiringa tukuake "Tohua ngā porotaka wakarererangi hei 360° mō Immich", kua weto taunoa, ka tuku i te whakaahua kāore i whakarerekētia me tētahi kōnae .xmp iti i tōna taha, kia whakaatuhia anō e Immich hei porotaka. Kāore he aratau porotaka, whakaahua whānui rānei o te Neo 2: he papatahi ōna whakaahua me ōna kiriata. Ina hopukina e te waea pūkoro te oro o tētahi topenga Neo 2, kei tētahi kōnae .m4a motuhake taua oro: ka tohua e te whārangi te topenga, ā, ka whiwhi a Immich i taua topenga kāore he oro (tikiake mā DJI Fly kia mau tonu te oro). Ka pērā anō te mahi a tētahi kāri kāmera me tētahi kōpaki DCIM i roto i tētahi kaipānui kāri. Kāore he mea e tukuna ki DJI.

Ētahi atu: [ngā wakarererangi DJI mā te USB](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#dji-drones-photos-and-videos-over-usb-experimental), [ngā tātaritanga o te hononga DJI](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#dji-link-diagnostics-experimental).

### Whakatuwhera i ngā kōnae mata o ngā kāmera 360°

Ko ngā whakaahua mata .insp me ngā kiriata .insv o ngā kāmera Insta360 (kotahi, e rua rānei ngā arotahi, i tētahi ara, e rua ngā ara, e rua rānei ngā kōnae), ngā kōnae .360 o te GoPro MAX me ngā kōnae .osv o te DJI Osmo 360, ka tuituia e te taupānga i te wā e whakatangi ana, me te whakataurite kua tuhia ki te kōnae, me te kore he mea hei whakauru ki hea.

1. Whakatakotoria ngā kōnae mata ki te wāhi e pānui ai te taupānga i a rātou: tētahi tiritiri SMB, WebDAV rānei, tō tūmau Immich (ka whakaae noa ki ngā .insp me ngā .insv), ngā kōpaki rānei o tētahi rorohiko. Kāore te whare whakaahua o tētahi waea pūkoro Android e whakarārangi i ngā kōnae mata, nō reira kāore tētahi kōnae mata kua tāruatia ki te waea pūkoro e kitea i tōna whare whakaahua, i te taupānga rānei.
2. Puritia tahitia ngā kōnae e rua o tētahi hopunga e rua ōna kōnae: ka kimi te taupānga i tētahi atu i te taha o te tuatahi.
3. Whakatuwheratia te kōnae, ā, pāwhiritia 360°. Ka whakaatu tētahi whakaahua i "Raw 360° file, stitched by the app" me te puna o tōna whakataurite; ka tuituia tētahi kiriata i te wā e whakatangi ana. I runga i ngā waea pūkoro, ngā papahiko me ngā pouaka whakaata Android, ka whakatangihia hoki tētahi ataata Insta360 o tō kohinga Immich [kua whakapūmautia ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera](#stabilise-your-360-videos-with-the-cameras-gyroscope-experimental) (whakamātautau).

### Whakapūmautia āu ataata 360° ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera (whakamātautau)

Ka wiri tētahi ataata 360° i hopukina i te ringa, i runga i tētahi pahikara, i runga rānei i tētahi pōtae mārō, ā, ka tītaha tōna pae. Ka hopu ngā kāmera i tō rātou ake nekehanga ki te kōnae mata me te kore e whakapūmau i ōna pika. Mai i te hanganga 42, ka pānui te kaiwhakatangi 360° o ngā waea pūkoro, ngā papahiko me ngā pouaka whakaata Android i taua nekehanga, i hopukina e te pūhurihuri o te kāmera, ā, ka hurihia whakamuri ia papamata: ka noho papatahi te pae, ka ngaro te wiri, ā, ka whai te tirohanga i te huarahi ina huri koe. He whakamātautau, kua whakakāngia hei taunoa. Kāore he mea pērā i te taupānga Immich.

1. Tauruatia ngā ataata .insv mata o tō Insta360 X3, X4, X5 rānei ki tō tūmau Immich, e whakaae ana ki a rātou kia pērā tonu; puritia ngā kōnae e rua o tētahi hopunga e rua ōna kōnae, pērā i [Whakatuwhera i ngā kōnae mata o ngā kāmera 360°](#open-the-raw-files-of-360-cameras).
2. Whakatuwheratia te ataata i tō rārangi wā, ā, pāwhiritia 360°: ka tīmata tonu, ā, ka puta a "Kua whakapūmautia ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera" mō ētahi hēkona.
3. Ko te pātene whakapūmau, i te pae o runga o te kaiwhakatangi i te taha o ngā pātene oro, 360° me te 3D, ka whakaweto i a ia kia kite ai koe i te ataata pērā i tōna hopukanga, ā, ka whakakā anō. Ka whai a "Whakapūmau ki te pūhurihuri o te kāmera (whakamātautau)", i Settings, Asset Viewer, Videos, i te pātene, ā, ka pupuri i tāu kōwhiringa mō ngā ataata e whai ake ana.

Kāore anō ngā ataata kua whakatuwheratia mai i tētahi tiritiri whatunga, i tētahi kōpaki rānei o te pūrere kia whakapūmautia, kāore hoki ngā kōnae GoPro MAX (.360) me DJI Osmo 360 (.osv), kāore e whakaaetia e tētahi tūmau Immich, ahakoa ka pānui hoki te taupānga i tō rātou nekehanga. Ka whakatangihia tētahi ataata kāore he ara nekehanga pērā i mua, kāore he pātene, ā, tērā pea ka tīmata te tirohanga kua huri ki tētahi taha, he papatahi te pae. Kāore anō te iPhone, te iPad, te Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko kia whakapūmau.

I tirohia e ngā whakamātautau me te CI o te kaupapa i runga i ngā ara nekehanga o ngā kōnae tauira o ngā kāmera Insta360 X3, X4 me X5, GoPro MAX me DJI Osmo 360: ka ōrite te pūhurihuri ki te nekehanga e kitea ana i te pikitia ki roto i te 0.1 ki te 0.3° ia papamata, ki te wiri o te 0.65 ki te 1.0° ia papamata i ngā haerenga motopaika. Kāore anō kia whakamātauria i runga i tētahi waea pūkoro.

Ētahi atu: ngā tuhipoka o te [hanganga 42](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-42), me te [issue 14](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues/14), i tono ai a Aoaodacat.

### Hurihia tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi

Hurihia tētahi whakaahua 90° ki te pēhi kotahi, me te kore e whakatuwhera i tētahi ētita: he pātene, he pātene papapātuhi, he pātene tae rānei o te pou whakahau o te pouaka whakaata, i ia pūhara. Ka hurihia e te taupānga Immich tētahi whakaahua i roto i tōna ētita; ka pērā a Immuch360 ki te pātene kotahi, ā, mō āu ake whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka haere mā taua ētita tūmau anō, nā reira ka whakaatu te taupānga tukutuku me ōu taputapu kē atu i aua whakaahua kua hurihia. Ko ētahi atu whakaahua ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake. Kāore rawa ngā whakaahua 360°, 180° me te 3D e hurihia, kia noho tika tonu ai ngā tirohanga rukuhanga.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="He whakaahua whakaari, ehara i te hopunga mata: ka whakaatu tētahi waea pūkoro i tētahi whakaahua e huri hauwhā ana ki te ara karaka, i runga ake i tētahi rārangi pātene porotaka, kua mūrama te pātene huri">

1. **Waea pūkoro, papapātuhi me te matapihi Meta Quest**: whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, ā, pāwhiritia Hurihia i te rārangi o raro, i mua i Whakatika, kia hurihia ki te ahunga o te karaka; mā te pēhi roa ka huri ki tērā atu taha. Mā te papapātuhi, ko R me Shift+R.
2. **Android TV me Google TV**: ka hurihia e te pātene kōwhai o te pou whakahau te whakaahua ki te ahunga o te karaka, ā, e te pātene kikorangi ki te ahunga kōaro, ahakoa kei hea te arotahi i te kaimātakitaki. Kei hea te hurihanga e tiakina ai: ko tētahi whakaahua o tō ake pūkete i runga i Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka hurihia mā te ētita whakaahua o te tūmau, e pupuri ana i te kōnae taketake, e hanga ana i tētahi whakaahua kua hurihia (ka puta he pae angiangi i a ia e mahi ana; ka whā ngā hurihanga, kotahi rānei te hurihanga whakamuri, ka hoki ki te taketake). Ko ngā whakaahua o te taputapu, o tētahi tiritiri, o tētahi hoa, o tētahi kohinga tiritiri rānei, me ia whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau tawhito ake, ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake, mō taua whakaahua anake, ā, kāore rawa te kōnae e whakarerekētia; ka kī te taupānga i tēnei kotahi te wā mō tētahi whakaahua Immich. I roto i ngā taupānga Android, iOS me te rorohiko, ka kitea tētahi hurihanga kua tiakina i runga i te taputapu i te kaimātakitaki, kāore anō i ngā whakaahua iti, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me ngā maharatanga. Kāore he Hurihia mō ngā kiriata, te kōpaki kua rakaina, me ia whakaahua 360°, 180°, 3D rānei.

<a id="play-av2-videos-on-your-computer"></a>
### Whakatangihia ngā ataata AV2 i tō rorohiko

Ko AV2 te hōputu ataata e whai ana i a AV1, i whakaotia e te Alliance for Open Media i te 2026, ā, kāore anō tētahi waea, pouaka whakaata, kāri whakairoiro rānei e mōhio ana mātou e wetewete i a ia. Ka pānui a Immuch360 Desktop i ngā kōnae AV2 mā te wetewete pūmanawa: ka wetewetehia e te tukatuka o tō rorohiko ki dav2d nā VideoLAN, i te tere e whakaaetia ana e tō rorohiko. Kāore a Immich e whakatangi i a rātou. He whakamātautau, kua whakakāngia hei taunoa, i runga i Windows me Linux; i runga i macOS, i tirohia e te CI o te kaupapa anake, kāore anō kia whakamātauria i tētahi Mac.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/howto/av2-on-computer.jpg" width="720" alt="He whakaahua whakaari, ehara i te hopunga mata: i te pō, e mātakitaki ana tētahi tangata e kitea ana mai i muri i tētahi kiriata o tētahi whare rama i runga i tētahi rorohiko pōnaho e rere ana a Immuch360, ko ōna kōnae kei tētahi NAS i runga i tētahi whata; i raro, te whakaahua iti o te kiriata me tōna tohu AV2 me te rārangi taipitopito Video decoding: AV2, decoded by the processor">
</p>

1. Whakatakotoria ō kōnae AV2 `.ivf`, `.obu` rānei ki tētahi kōpaki o tō kohinga, whakatuwheratia rānei te tiritiri e pupuri ana i a rātou, whakatuwheratia rānei tētahi ki Immuch360 Desktop mai i tō kaiwhakahaere kōnae. He tohu AV2 kei ō rātou whakaahua iti.
2. Whakatuwheratia tētahi. Kotahi te wā, ka tikiake a "Tikiake, whakatangi" i te kaiwetewete AV2 (1 MB pea, 1.7 MB i runga i tētahi Mac; ko tō Windows kua hainatia e APROGSYS), ā, ka tirohia mā tōna SHA-256; kātahi ka mahi tuimotu, ā, ka whiwhi ō ataata AV2 i ō rātou whakaahua iti.
3. Ka whakaatu a "Te wetewete ataata", i ngā taipitopito o te ataata (pātuhi I), i te tere o te wetewete a tēnei rorohiko. I runga i te rorohiko pōnaho o te kaituhi, me tētahi Intel Core i9-13980HX, ka maeneene te whakatangi o ngā ataata 8 bit tae atu ki te 1080p, ka ea noa te 10 bit 1080p, ā, kāore te 4K e ea, i te 8 bit pērā i te 10 bit: ka tatari taua ataata kia mau anō. Ko tētahi ataata ka wetewetehia pōturitia e tēnei rorohiko ka kī pērā i mua i tōna whakatangi, me "Whakatangihia tonu".

Hei whakaweto: Settings, Asset Viewer, "Ngā ataata AV2 (whakamātau)"; kei reira hoki a "Tangohia te pūwetewete AV2". Ka whakamahi te wetewete i ia uho o te tukatuka, ā, ka whakamahana i te rorohiko. Kāore anō ngā waea pūkoro, te pouaka whakaata me te Quest e whakatangi i AV2, ā, kāore te tūmau Immich e tango i ngā kōnae `.ivf` me `.obu`.

Ētahi atu: ngā tuhipoka o te [hanganga desktop 21](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-21), me [te kaiwetewete AV2 me ōna raihana](../licenses/av2/README.md), mai i te putanga tōmua [av2-decoder-1](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/av2-decoder-1).

Ka kōrero noa te taupānga ki ngā tūmau ka kōwhiria e koe (tō tūmau Immich, āu tiritiri, tō tūmau Plex, āu kāmera), ā, me te kore tūmau, ki tētahi mea kore rawa. Ka whakamahia noa te kāmera i te taputapu, e te kaiwhakatangi Spatial 2.5D me te matawai i te waehere o tētahi mata ka takiuru e koe; kāore rawa ōna atahanga e tiakina, e tukuna rānei ki hea. Ka whiwhi tētahi mata ka takiuru mai i tō waea pūkoro i tōna ake wātū, ka tukuna kua whakamunaia i tō whatunga kāinga, kāore rawa tō kupuhipa; ka takiuru tētahi pouaka whakaata LG mā tētahi whārangi i te pūtirotiro o te waea pūkoro, e tuku ana i tō īmēra me tō kupuhipa ki te pouaka whakaata kāore i whakamunaia i tō whatunga kāinga, ā, ko te wātū anake ka puritia e te pouaka whakaata. Ka noho ngā hopunga me ngā pūkete whiwhinga o ngā whārangi wakarererangi ki te taputapu ki te kore koe e tiritiri: kei roto te ataata o te wakarererangi, tērā pea tōna tūranga, ā, kei tētahi hopunga ngā tau rangatū o te pou whakahau me te waka rererangi. Ka haere te pikitia me ngā uara o te wakarererangi mai i te waea pūkoro ki ngā taputapu mahunga me ngā pouaka whakaata anake ka honoa e koe, i tō whatunga ā-rohe, kāore i whakamunaia, pērā i tētahi waea pūkoro kua tiritiria i te Wi-Fi. Kāore he mea e tukuna ki a DJI, ki a TP-Link rānei. Kei [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) te kaupapa here katoa, ā, kei te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy) ngā taipitopito mō ia puna. Ka puritia ngā mea kua mātakitakina e koe o ō kiriata ki tētahi pātengi raraunga i runga i te pūrere, ā, kāore rawa e tukuna ki tō tūmau.

**Ngā āhuatanga AI** ka mahi i runga i āu taputapu anake. Ko te whakaahua, tōna āhua hōhonu, tōna atamira me tōna whakaahua whānui ka tātaitia i runga i tō rorohiko, i roto rānei i tō taputapu mahunga, ā, kāore e tukuna ki hea. Ka hono noa te taupānga hei tango i tētahi wāhanga i tonoa e koe, mai i ngā putanga o tēnei kaupapa i GitHub, ki te kore rānei, mai i te whārangi ōkawa o te tauira mēnā he whārangi tōna, ka tirotiro i ia kōnae mā tōna SHA-256, kātahi ka mahi tuimotu. Ka tuku a ONNX Runtime, e whakahaere ana i ngā tauira i runga i tētahi rorohiko, i te inemamao ki a Microsoft i ōna hanganga ōkawa; ka whakawetohia e Immuch360. Ka haere ngā kōnae hōu ki te taha o āu taketake, kaua rawa ki runga, ā, ka tae ki tō tūmau Immich anake mēnā ka whakakā koe i "Taurua hoki i ngā putanga AI" (ka whakakāngia hoki e "Select all" i ngā pukaemi taurua).

## Mahere ara

Ngā mea kāore anō kia oti, ko te mea tūpono rawa i te tuatahi. Ehara tētahi mea i konei i te kupu taurangi, ā, ka āwhina ngā urupare i te [pūaroturuki take](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) ki te whakatau he aha ka tae tuatahi.

- **Ngā toa**: Google Play me te hanganga 41 (kei te arotakehia e Google mai i te 5 Whiringa-ā-rangi; kei te arotakehia te rārangi TV), te Meta Horizon Store me te hanganga 41 (kei te arotakehia e Meta mai i te 5 Whiringa-ā-rangi), te App Store me te putanga iOS o ngā hanganga e whai ake, te LG Content Store (putanga 1.0.0 kei te arotakehia e LG mai i te 24 Whiringa-ā-nuku, kātahi ko te putanga 1.0.1), ā, ā muri ake ko te toa a Samsung.
- **Kaihanga AI**: ngā whakaahua whānui 360° i runga i te Mac; ngā atamira splat i hangaia i runga i Linux, me ngā whakairoiro whakauru, mai hoki i ngā whakaahua me ngā ataata 360°; te tārua mō te taputapu mahunga o ngā atamira splat Taipitopito me Mōrahi, kua inea i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga; ngā tapanga AI i ngā taupānga iOS, LG me Samsung; ngā whakaahua whānui i whakamātauria i runga i tētahi rorohiko Linux me tētahi kāri whakairoiro, i runga hoki i ngā kāri AMD me Intel.
- **Wakarererangi**: he rere i waho hei whakamana i te teitei me ngā tere i tua atu i te 1.8 m me te 1.5 m/s me ngā tawhiti ārai ki tētahi rīpene ine, kātahi hei whakaatu i ngā amiorangi me te tawhiti mai i te kāinga; te pikitia pūmau o te tukunga ka tirohia i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga; te tukunga ki ngā pouaka whakaata LG me Samsung; te wakarererangi i runga i iPhone me iPad, kāore anō kia tīmata; he whakamātautau o te whārangi kawe mai me tētahi DJI Neo 2 pono, kātahi ko te oro i hopukina e te waea pūkoro ka whakaurua ki tōna topenga. Kāore he mea e tuku whakahau ana ki tētahi wakarererangi kua whakaritea i mua i aua whakamātautau.
- **Pouaka whakaata**: te hanganga 29 i tētahi Android TV pono, tae atu ki ōna mana whakahaere kiriata me te takiuru mā tō waea pūkoro; ngā tiritiri, Plex me te tirohanga ora o te taupānga LG i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG pono, me te webOS 26; te hanganga whakamātau Samsung 0.2.0 i tētahi pouaka whakaata Samsung pono, kātahi ko te takiuru mā te waea pūkoro, ngā tiritiri, Plex me ngā kāmera i ngā pouaka whakaata Samsung; kātahi ko ngā kiriata 360° i te porotaka o ngā pouaka whakaata LG, te oro o ngā kāmera me ā rātou hopunga (kaua i mua i Hakihea 2026).

- **Ngā rorohiko**: te whakamātau ā-ringa i ia taumahi i runga i tētahi PC Windows, tae atu ki te takiuru mā tō waea pūkoro; Mokowā 2.5D me te kāmera tukutuku i runga i Windows, kei te whakawhanakehia mō Whiringa-ā-rangi 2026; kātahi ko te kaiwhakaatu kiriata 360° i runga i Linux me macOS, he whakamātau i runga i tētahi PC Linux pono; kātahi ko ngā whakahōunga aunoa me tētahi taupānga macOS kua hainatia; AV2 i ngā kōnae MKV, WebM me MP4, ka tangohia e tētahi tūmau Immich.

### Ngā āhuatanga matatau

He aha ka taea e te taupānga te pānui, te whakatangi, mō te kaipānui e hiahia ana ki te whānuitanga katoa. Ka whakaaturia ia rārangi i roto i te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md) me te [wharangi noho rite](../docs/immuch360/compatibility.md).

<details>
<summary><b>Ngā hōputu, ngā puna, te whakatangi me ngā taputapu, kia hōhonu</b></summary>

- **Hōputu**: whakaahua me kiriata papatahi (te mea ka wetewetehia e te taputapu: MP4, MOV, MKV me ētahi atu); whakaahua me kiriata 360° equirectangular; whakaahua whānui-whāiti (tapahanga GPano); VR180; 3D taurua-tirohanga, runga me raro, taha ki te taha rānei, i pānuihia mai i te kōnae (te pouaka st3d) i whakaarohia rānei mai i te papamata; whakaahua mokowā a Apple (takirua karu HEIC) me kiriata mokowā (MV-HEVC, kotahi karu); kōnae mata Insta360 .insp me .insv, GoPro .360, DJI .osv; puna 10 piti HLG me PQ kua whakamaherehia ki runga i te porotaka; TIFF ka wetewetehia e te taupānga i Android (8 me te 16 paraiti, kiwikiwi, RGB, papatae me CMYK, kāore i kōpekehia, me te kōpeke LZW, Deflate, PackBits, JPEG rānei); ngā Gaussian splats i .spz putanga 1 ki te 4 me .ply ā-rua, i runga i te Meta Quest 3 me te 3S, ā, mai i te hanganga 41 me te hanganga desktop 21, i runga i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā papahiko Android, i runga hoki i ngā rorohiko, i roto hoki i ngā whakaahua `_splat.jpg` ka tiakina e te taupānga ki Immich; AV2 i ngā kōnae .ivf me .obu i runga i ngā rorohiko (whakamātautau).
- **Whakaahuatanga me ngā kōrero ā-muri**: whakaahuatanga GPano me te mokowā e pānuihia mai i te kōnae, me ngā tono awhe i runga i tētahi tūmau, tiritiri rānei; te tirohanga tuatahi a te kāmera; ngā porotaka o ngā wakarererangi DJI ka mōhiotia mai i tō rātou kāmera, me tētahi kōnae āpiti .xmp kōwhiringa kia tohua anō e Immich; "Tirohia hei 360°" kua maharatia i runga i te taputapu; ka mōhiotia te VR180 mai i te kōnae, i tōna ingoa rānei; te wāhi o tētahi kiriata wakarererangi ka pānuihia mai i tōna rangitaki rere .SRT; kei te ora tonu ngā kōrero ā-muri nā te mea ko te kōnae taketake tonu e whakatangihia, ehara i tētahi kape kua hurihia; he hurihanga hauwhā ka tiakina mā te ētita Immich, ka puritia rānei i runga i te taputapu, kāore rawa i runga i tētahi kōnae rukuhanga.

- **Ngā kōnae mata kua tuituihia i runga i te taputapu**: he shader kongakonga Flutter mō ngā whakaahua; he pānga GPU i roto i te kaiwhakatangi mō ngā kiriata ara kotahi; e rua ngā kaiwetewete taputa i te wā kotahi me tētahi compositor GPU mō ngā kiriata kotahi te arotahi mō ia ara, mō ia kōnae rānei (OpenGL ES 3 i runga i Android me te Quest, Metal i runga i iOS, i runga i Windows hoki, mai i te hanganga desktop 5, e rua ngā kaiwhakatangi libmpv e tuituihia ai ō rātou papamata e te taupānga i runga i te matū whakaahua); te whakatikatika ake o te kāmera (ngā tauira Mei, V6, me te equidistant) me te hopunga a te mea ine nekehanga; ka hoki ki te arotahi kotahi, ki te awa whakaputa, ki te kape LRV a te kāmera, ki te kiriata kāore anō kia tuituihia rānei, ina kore e taea e te taputapu te whakahaere i ngā kaiwetewete e rua. - **Ngā puna**: te whare whakaahua o te taputapu; ngā kōpaki o tētahi rorohiko; tētahi wakarererangi, kāri kāmera rānei mā te USB, mā te kōwhiri kōpaki o te pūnaha (Android, whakamātautau); tētahi pou whakahau DJI RC-N3 mā tōna taura mō te ataata ora o te wakarererangi (Android, whakamātautau), ka tukuna i te whatunga ā-rohe ki tētahi Meta Quest, ki tētahi Android TV rānei; tētahi tūmau Immich; SMB 2 me te 3, WebDAV, DLNA/UPnP; Plex mā te HTTPS kua tirohia ki te tiwhikete ake o te tūmau; ngā kāmera Tapo mā te whatunga ā-rohe (HTTPS me RTSP); tētahi waea pūkoro, rorohiko rānei kua tiritiria i te Wi-Fi hei tūmau WebDAV; ngā tūmau i kitea mā Bonjour/mDNS, tētahi matawai whatunga ā-rohe, SSDP, GDM me te kimi TP-Link, ā, ka whāia ina huri tō rātou wāhitau; ngā kōpaki o tētahi waea, papa rorohiko, Meta Quest Android rānei mā te kaikōwhiri kōpaki o te pūnaha.
