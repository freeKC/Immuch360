# Immuch360

**Āu pāpāho. Ngā mata katoa. Ngā tirohanga katoa.**

Ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata papatahi, 360° me te 3D mai i tō taputapu, tō NAS, tō tūmau Immich, āu kāmera me tō wakarererangi. Kāore he pūkete e hiahiatia ana. He taupānga koreutu, puna tuwhera hoki a Immuch360, ka pānui i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata i te wāhi e noho ana rātou, ka whakatangi i ia mea i te kaimātakitaki e tika ana mōna: papatahi, hei porotaka ka taea e koe te titiro huri noa, i te 3D, huri noa rānei i a koe i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga. Ka rere i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, ngā papahiko, ngā pouaka whakaata, ngā taputapu mahunga Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko.

**New: your flat photos, in 3D.** Pick a photo on your computer and get a 3D photo, a 3D scene to step into, or a 360° panorama that imagines the rest of the place around you. In a Meta Quest 3 or 3S, one press on "AI relief" and any flat photo stands out. The AI runs on your own computer and headset, and nothing is sent anywhere. Experimental, and not yet tried in a headset by the author (first test on 30 October). [See how](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama).

## Mā wai

- **Your photos are flat** and you want them in 3D, as a scene to lean into in a headset, or inside a 360° panorama, without a 3D camera: [turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama), new, made by AI on your own computer.
- **Ka hopu koe i te 360°, i te 3D rānei** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te titiro huri noa i roto i āu hopunga, kaua ki te kite papatahi, tāruarua, toro rānei: [360°, VR180 me te 3D](#explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d).
- **Kei runga āu whakaahua me āu kiriata i tētahi NAS, rorohiko, Plex, Immich rānei** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia kitea i te pouaka whakaata me te kore tārua: [i te pouaka whakaata](#watch-on-the-tv-with-the-remote), [NAS, tiritiri me Plex](#play-the-files-of-a-nas-a-computer-a-media-server-or-plex).
- **He Meta Quest tāu** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki te tomo ki roto i āu ake whakaahua me āu kiriata: [i te taputapu mahunga](#step-inside-your-photos-and-videos-with-a-meta-quest).
- **Ka whakahaere koe i a Immich** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tōna taupānga, me ngā kaimātakitaki rukuhanga me ngā mata nui ka takiuru koe mai i tō waea pūkoro: [tō kohinga Immich](#keep-your-immich-library-with-more), [ka whakatauritea ki a Immich](#compared-with-immich).
- **Ka tiaki koe i tō kāinga ki ngā kāmera Tapo** ā, e hiahia ana koe kia noho tahi rātou me āu whakaahua, i te pouaka whakaata anō hoki (kāore anō kia tirohia ki tētahi kāmera pono): [kāmera kāinga](#watch-your-home-cameras-on-the-big-screen).
- **Ka rere koe i tētahi wakarererangi DJI** ā, e hiahia ana koe ki tōna pikitia ora i tō waea pūkoro me te kore DJI Fly, i tētahi Meta Quest, i tētahi Android TV rānei mō te hunga e noho tahi ana me koe (whakamātautau): [tō wakarererangi](#see-what-your-drone-sees-on-your-phone).

There is no Immuch360 account to create, and nothing goes to us: the app talks only to your devices and to the servers you choose, and downloads an AI component only when you ask for it.

## Tikina ki tō taputapu

Tikina te taupānga mai i te toa o tō taputapu i te wāhi kua whakaputaina, mai i te whārangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases) rānei i tēnei wā.

### Waea pūkoro me papapātuhi Android

1. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aprogsys.immuch360), te APK rānei o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases): `Immuch360-v<version>-arm64-v8a-release.apk` mō tētahi waea pūkoro; ka mahi te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui ki ngā wāhi katoa.
2. Ka whakaurua te taupānga i te taha o te taupānga Immich whaimana. Kāore te APK mai i GitHub e whakahou ake ana i a ia anō: tirotirohia te wharangi Releases; ina kua whakaurua e koe te taupānga mai i tētahi toa, tangohia ngā whakahoutanga mai i tērā toa.

### iPhone me iPad

2. Pāwhiritia "Whakamahia me te kore tūmau", "Tūhono ki tētahi tūmau Immich" rānei kia takiuru mā tō tūmau; kei te whārangi haere mai raua, mai i te hanganga 24.

<a id="android-tv-and-google-tv"></a>
### Android TV me Google TV

1. I mua i te tuku a Google Play i te taupānga i runga i ngā pouaka whakaata, tangohia te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. I te pouaka whakaata, whakakāngia ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake me te patuiro USB (i tētahi Google TV: Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Mō, tīpakohia te “Android TV OS build” e whitu ngā wā, kātahi ko Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake; he rerekē ngā ingoa i ia pouaka whakaata).
3. Mai i tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite, me te adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, whakaaetia te tono i te pouaka whakaata, kātahi ko `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Ka puta te taupānga i waenga i ngā taupānga o te pouaka whakaata, me tōna haki. Tāutahia te tuku e whai ake ana mā te huarahi ōrite: ka pupuri a `-r` i te takiuru me ngā tautuhinga.

### Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)

1. Kei te arotakehia te putanga 1.0.0 e LG. Kia tae rā anō ki te LG Content Store, ka whakaurua i te Developer Mode: me whai koe i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG me webOS 5.0, tērā hōu ake rānei, tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite me tētahi pūkete kaiwhakawhanake LG koreutu. I te pouaka whakaata, whakaurua te Developer Mode mai i te LG Content Store, takiuru, whakakāngia te Dev Mode Status, ā, tukuna te pouaka whakaata kia tīmata anō; tuhia tōna wāhitau IP me te kīanga huna e whakaaturia ana e te taupānga.
2. I te rorohiko, whakaurua te webOS CLI (Node.js 18, tērā hōu ake rānei): `npm install -g @webos-tools/cli`, kātahi rēhitatia te pouaka whakaata mā `ares-setup-device` (tōna wāhitau IP, tauranga 9922, kaiwhakamahi `prisoner`) me `ares-novacom --device <name> --getkey`, ka tono i te kīanga huna.
3. From the [webOS 1.0.1 pre-release](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/webos-v1.0.1), download `Immuch360-webOS-1.0.1.ipk` for a UHD TV, or `Immuch360-webOS-1.0.1-1280x720.ipk` for a Full HD TV, to which LG gives 1280x720 apps. Check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, then `ares-install --device <name> Immuch360-webOS-1.0.1.ipk`. The [webOS Dev Manager](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop) of webosbrew does the same without the command line.
4. Ka roa te wātū Developer Mode ki te 1000 hāora, ā, ka whakahoutia e te pātene EXTEND o te taupānga Developer Mode; ina pau, ka tangohia e te pouaka whakaata ngā taupānga i whakaurua pēnei. Kei [webos/README.md](../webos/README.md#install-on-a-tv-in-developer-mode) ngā taipitopito.
5. Ina tae te taupānga ki te LG Content Store, tangohia te tārua o te Developer Mode, ā, whakaurua mai i te toa.

### Turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama

Most of your photos are flat, and you cannot go back and take them again in 3D. Immuch360 gives them depth. On your computer, one photo becomes a **3D photo**, a **3D scene** you lean into in a headset, or a **360° panorama** that imagines the rest of the place around you. A Quest 3 or 3S does the relief itself, for any flat photo. Everything runs on your own computer and headset: no cloud, no account, your originals never changed. New in build 32 and desktop build 13, experimental and on by default. The Immich app has nothing like it.

**On your computer**, with desktop build 13 on Windows, Linux or macOS:

1. Select one or more photos in the library or in a folder, then "Make 3D (AI)".
2. Pick what you want: "3D photo", "3D scene", "360° panorama", or "3D version of a 360° photo" for your own 360° shots. The box says what AI will estimate or invent ("About 96% of this panorama will be invented by AI"), about how long it takes on this computer, and where the file goes: "Next to the original" or "A folder of my choice". A type this computer cannot do stays greyed, with its reason, such as "Not yet on Mac" or "The graphics card has 6.0 GiB of memory, 360° panoramas need 7.5 GiB".
3. The first time, the box offers the components of that type: Download, once. They run on this computer and work offline afterwards.
4. Convert. The jobs queue on two lanes, the processor and the graphics card, with their steps and the time left; Cancel stops one at any moment, and quitting the app asks first.
5. Open the result. A 3D photo shows as "Left eye", "Side by side", "Wiggle" (the animation at the top of this page) or "Red and cyan" for glasses; a scene has "Preview in 3D", turned with the mouse or the touchpad, and "Export for other 3D apps (.glb)"; a panorama opens in the 360° viewer.

Every new file goes next to its original, never over it, or into a folder of your choice when the original's folder takes no new file; on a network folder, only once you say yes for that folder ("Immuch360 writes new files there, never the originals"). The library links each version to its original with its badge, "3D AI", "3D scene" or "360° AI", in the album "Immuch360 AI versions", which stays out of your Immich backup unless you turn on "Also back up AI versions"; careful, "Select all" in the backup albums picks this album too and turns that switch on. To see a file in the Quest, save it on your NAS, back it up to your Immich server or, on Windows, turn on "Share this computer".

**In a Meta Quest**, with build 32:

- **AI relief**: open any flat photo, from your library or a share, and press "AI relief" in the top bar: the headset estimates the relief itself and shows the photo in 3D, "Relief estimated by AI". The model, 99.5 MB, downloads the first time you press it, and the reliefs are kept in a cache. "Open AI reliefs in 2.5D (experimental)", in Settings, Advanced, "AI and 3D", makes near objects move in front of the background as you move your head. On a Quest 2 or a Quest Pro the button stays greyed, "Not tested on this headset yet", until you turn on "AI relief on Quest 2 and Quest Pro (untested)" there.
- **3D photos and scenes from the computer**: "View in 3D" shows a 3D photo in true 3D. "View the 3D scene" on the original, or "Explore the 3D scene" on the scene, puts the scene in your room: lean in, or glide with the left thumbstick inside the rebuilt area ("Room to move: about 0.5 m each way" for the room at the top of this page); the layers flatten and darken at its edge, a click on a thumbstick resets the view, and B or Y goes back.
- **Panoramas**: a generated panorama opens all around you, facing your photo, with "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and a thin outline of the original photo, which fade after a few seconds; its 3D version is in true 3D.

Not yet tried in a headset by the author: the first headset test is on 30 October 2026. The headset's code for the relief, the scenes and the panoramas passed its tests on a computer, and what you see in your own Quest is welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues).

**On phones, tablets and TVs**, the files a computer made open as they are, and nothing is generated there. A panorama opens in the 360° viewer with the "360° AI" badge, the sentence and the outline on Android phones, tablets and Android TV; on iPhone, iPad, LG and Samsung TVs it opens as a plain 360° photo for now. A scene shows as its photo, with a small 3D SCENE mark in its corner; on Android, its details add "Open it on a Meta Quest to explore it". A 3D photo shows its two eyes side by side.

**What is real and what is not, always said.** It is the point, not the small print. The relief of a 3D photo is estimated from one photo, and what the photo hides is filled in and is not real. A scene is "Reconstructed from one photo, explore within its limits". A panorama keeps your real pixels where they were and invents the rest, about 90% of the sphere for a typical photo, and the box says how much before you start. The app says it where you look, with "Relief estimated by AI", "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and the badges, and the files say it too: in our XMP, and for the scenes and the panoramas in the IPTC digital source type of an image made with AI, so that other apps can tell a generated panorama from a real 360° photo. Your originals are never changed, and nothing leaves your computer or your headset. As the box puts it: "A finished conversion does not guarantee a good result."

**What was checked**, by 29 October 2026, with test builds of this code: 3D photos by the Linux app in a container on a Linux PC, offline too; scenes by the Windows app on a laptop with an RTX 4060 Laptop GPU (ten in 2 min 39 s) and by the engine on the Linux PC; on that laptop, four panoramas in 4 min 16 s to 4 min 44 s each, their 3D versions in about 30 s, with Cancel, a killed app, another program holding the graphics card, and no network connection of the app during the tests. The author has not judged the panoramas in a headset yet. Not tried: a Mac, a Linux PC with a dedicated graphics card, AMD and Intel graphics cards for the panoramas, a phone or a TV opening these files, and anything in a headset.

Limits: no 360° panorama on a Mac yet ("Not yet on Mac"), nor without a suitable graphics card, and the box says why ("No graphics card with Vulkan found", "Integrated graphics share the computer's memory: too slow for panoramas", with "Try on integrated graphics (untested)" to try anyway); inside a panorama, the edge of your photo can show as a sharp step in tone; most scenes say "Field of view guessed: shapes may be off"; a scene needs about 2 GB of free memory ("Not enough memory" otherwise); with AI versions backed up, Immich's duplicate finder pairs each photo with its scene and often suggests keeping the scene, the heavier file: keep the original. Phones and TVs make nothing, and the headset makes reliefs only; a scene comes from one photo, not yet from several photos of the same place.

**The components**: on the computer, Settings, "AI and 3D" lists the components of each feature with their size, Download and Remove, then "Remove all AI components" and "Move the AI components" to another drive; "AI features" off hides every AI button and downloads nothing. Each file is checked by its SHA-256. On Windows they sit in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.aprogsys\Immuch360 Desktop\ai-components`, which the uninstaller deletes (not a folder you moved them to); on Linux in `~/.local/share/immuch360/ai-components` on most systems, and on a Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/com.aprogsys.immuch360.desktop/ai-components`. In the Quest, "AI and 3D" is in Settings, Advanced, with the relief cache.

More: the notes of [build 32](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-32) and [desktop build 13](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-13), and [the models, their licences, their sources and their sums](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/blob/immuch360/licenses/ai-components/MODEL-LICENSES.md). The user guide gets its AI chapter with the next pass; until then, this section is the guide.

### Whakatangi i ngā kōnae o tētahi NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho, Plex rānei

Kei runga āu kiriata i tētahi NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho, Plex rānei. Ka kimi a Immuch360 i ngā tūmau o tō whatunga ko ia anake, ka tirotiro i a rātou, ka whakatangi i ā rātou whakaahua me ā rātou kiriata i tō rātou wāhi, i ia kaimātakitaki o te taupānga, me te kore tārua: SMB (Samba, ngā tiritiri Windows), WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology me ētahi atu) me ngā tūmau pāpāho DLNA (Jellyfin, Emby, minidlna, he NAS, he pouaka TV). He whakamātautau tēnei whārangi, i runga anake i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā papapātuhi Android, ā, kāore anō kia whakamātauria ki tētahi wakarererangi pono.

Ko ngā whakaahua mata .insp me ngā kiriata .insv o ngā kāmera Insta360 (kotahi, e rua rānei ngā arotahi, i tētahi ara, e rua ngā ara, e rua rānei ngā kōnae), ngā kōnae .360 o te GoPro MAX me ngā kōnae .osv o te DJI Osmo 360, ka tuituia e te taupānga i te wā e whakatangi ana, me te whakataurite kua tuhia ki te kōnae, me te kore he mea hei whakauru ki hea.

Kāore te whare whakaahua o tētahi waea pūkoro Android e whakarārangi i ngā kōnae mata, nō reira kāore tētahi kōnae mata kua tāruatia ki te waea pūkoro e kitea i tōna whare whakaahua, i te taupānga rānei.

### Hurihia tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi

Hurihia tētahi whakaahua 90° ki te pēhi kotahi, me te kore e whakatuwhera i tētahi ētita: he pātene, he pātene papapātuhi, he pātene tae rānei o te pou whakahau o te pouaka whakaata, i ia pūhara. Ka hurihia e te taupānga Immich tētahi whakaahua i roto i tōna ētita; ka pērā a Immuch360 ki te pātene kotahi, ā, mō āu ake whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka haere mā taua ētita tūmau anō, nā reira ka whakaatu te taupānga tukutuku me ōu taputapu kē atu i aua whakaahua kua hurihia. Ko ētahi atu whakaahua ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake. Kāore rawa ngā whakaahua 360°, 180° me te 3D e hurihia, kia noho tika tonu ai ngā tirohanga rukuhanga.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="He whakaahua whakaari, ehara i te hopunga mata: ka whakaatu tētahi waea pūkoro i tētahi whakaahua e huri hauwhā ana ki te ara karaka, i runga ake i tētahi rārangi pātene porotaka, kua mūrama te pātene huri">
</p>

1. **Waea pūkoro, papapātuhi me te matapihi Meta Quest**: whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, ā, pāwhiritia Hurihia i te rārangi o raro, i mua i Whakatika, kia hurihia ki te ahunga o te karaka; mā te pēhi roa ka huri ki tērā atu taha. Mā te papapātuhi, ko R me Shift+R.
2. **Android TV me Google TV**: ka hurihia e te pātene kōwhai o te pou whakahau te whakaahua ki te ahunga o te karaka, ā, e te pātene kikorangi ki te ahunga kōaro, ahakoa kei hea te arotahi i te kaimātakitaki. I runga i tētahi pou whakahau kāore he pātene tae, pēhia Raro: ka puta te pae i raro i te whakaahua me Hurihia kua tīpakohia (kotahi Matau te tawhiti ina whai pātene kupu te whakaahua), ā, ka hurihia te whakaahua e ia OK.
3. **Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)**: kōwhai me kikorangi pērā i Android TV, R me Shift+R i tētahi papapātuhi, te pātene huri rānei o ngā mana whakahaere o te whakaahua. Ka huri hoki ngā pouaka whakaata Samsung i tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi.
4. **Windows, Linux me macOS**: ka huri a R ki te ahunga o te karaka, a Shift+R ki te ahunga kōaro, te pātene huri rānei i te pae o runga, i mua i te mata katoa.
5. **Ngā whakaahua o tētahi tiritiri whatunga, o Plex, o tētahi kāmera rānei**: te pātene huri tuatahi i te pae o runga, R me Shift+R, ngā pātene kōwhai me kikorangi rānei.

Kei hea te hurihanga e tiakina ai: ko tētahi whakaahua o tō ake pūkete i runga i Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka hurihia mā te ētita whakaahua o te tūmau, e pupuri ana i te kōnae taketake, e hanga ana i tētahi whakaahua kua hurihia (ka puta he pae angiangi i a ia e mahi ana; ka whā ngā hurihanga, kotahi rānei te hurihanga whakamuri, ka hoki ki te taketake). Ko ngā whakaahua o te taputapu, o tētahi tiritiri, o tētahi hoa, o tētahi kohinga tiritiri rānei, me ia whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau tawhito ake, ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake, mō taua whakaahua anake, ā, kāore rawa te kōnae e whakarerekētia; ka kī te taupānga i tēnei kotahi te wā mō tētahi whakaahua Immich. I roto i ngā taupānga Android, iOS me te rorohiko, ka kitea tētahi hurihanga kua tiakina i runga i te taputapu i te kaimātakitaki, kāore anō i ngā whakaahua iti, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me ngā maharatanga. Kāore he Hurihia mō ngā kiriata, te kōpaki kua rakaina, me ia whakaahua 360°, 180°, 3D rānei.

## Tūmataiti

Kāore he mea e tae ki te kaiwhakawhanake: kāore he pūkete, kāore he tātaritanga, kāore he ratonga pūrongo hinga, kāore he pānui hokohoko. Ka kōrero noa te taupānga ki ngā tūmau ka kōwhiria e koe (tō tūmau Immich, āu tiritiri, tō tūmau Plex, āu kāmera), ā, me te kore tūmau, ki tētahi mea kore rawa. Ka noho ngā kupuhipa me ngā tohu ki te rokiroki haumaru o te taputapu; kāore he mea pērā i ngā pouaka whakaata LG me Samsung mō te taupānga, nō reira ka noho rātou ki te rokiroki ake o te taupānga i reira, kāore i whakamunaia. Ka whakamahia noa te kāmera i te taputapu, e te kaiwhakatangi Spatial 2.5D me te matawai i te waehere o tētahi mata ka takiuru e koe; kāore rawa ōna atahanga e tiakina, e tukuna rānei ki hea. Ka whiwhi tētahi mata ka takiuru mai i tō waea pūkoro i tōna ake wātū, ka tukuna kua whakamunaia i tō whatunga kāinga, kāore rawa tō kupuhipa; ka takiuru tētahi pouaka whakaata LG mā tētahi whārangi i te pūtirotiro o te waea pūkoro, e tuku ana i tō īmēra me tō kupuhipa ki te pouaka whakaata kāore i whakamunaia i tō whatunga kāinga, ā, ko te wātū anake ka puritia e te pouaka whakaata. Ka noho ngā hopunga me ngā pūkete whiwhinga o ngā whārangi wakarererangi ki te taputapu ki te kore koe e tiritiri: kei roto te ataata o te wakarererangi, tērā pea tōna tūranga, ā, kei tētahi hopunga ngā tau rangatū o te pou whakahau me te waka rererangi. Ka haere te pikitia me ngā uara o te wakarererangi mai i te waea pūkoro ki ngā taputapu mahunga me ngā pouaka whakaata anake ka honoa e koe, i tō whatunga ā-rohe, kāore i whakamunaia, pērā i tētahi waea pūkoro kua tiritiria i te Wi-Fi. Kāore he mea e tukuna ki a DJI, ki a TP-Link rānei. Kei [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) te kaupapa here katoa, ā, kei te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy) ngā taipitopito mō ia puna.

**The AI features** work on your devices only. The photo, its relief, its scene and its panorama are computed on your computer or in your headset and sent nowhere. The app connects only to download a component you asked for, from the releases of this project on GitHub or, failing that, from the model's official page when it has one, checks each file by its SHA-256, and then works offline. ONNX Runtime, which runs the models on a computer, sends telemetry to Microsoft in its official builds; Immuch360 turns it off. The new files go next to your originals, never over them, and reach your Immich server only if you turn on "Also back up AI versions" ("Select all" in the backup albums turns it on too).
