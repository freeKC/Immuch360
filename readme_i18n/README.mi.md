# Immuch360

**Āu pāpāho. Ngā mata katoa. Ngā tirohanga katoa.**

He taupānga koreutu, puka tuwhera a Immuch360 hei kimi, hei tirotiro, hei whakatangi, hei torotoro i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata: ngā mea māori, me ngā mea horopaki, 360°, 3D, kaipuku hoki e whakaatuhia papatahi, ruarua, kumea rānei e ētahi atu taupānga. Ka pānuihia e ia i te wāhi e noho nei, i runga i te taputapu tonu, i te NAS, i te rorohiko, i te tūmau Plex, i te tūmau Immich rānei, ā, ka whakatangihia i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, papapātuhi, pouaka whakaata, ngā taputapu mahunga Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko. Kāore he pūkete, he kapua, he tūmau e hiahiatia.

- **He aha tāna mahi?** Ka whakaatuhia āu whakaahua me āu kiriata hei whare whakaahua, ā, ka whakatangihia i te kaimātakitaki e tau ai ki tēnā, ki tēnā kōnae: papatahi, hei porotaka ka titiro haere koe, i roto i te 3D, i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga.
- **He aha ngā pāpāho?** He whakaahua me he kiriata papatahi, he whakaahua whānui, he whakaahua me he kiriata 360°, VR180, 3D taurua-tirohanga, he whakaahua mokowā a Apple, ngā porotaka 360° o ngā wakarererangi DJI, me ngā kōnae mata o ngā kāmera 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), e tuitui nei te taupānga.
- **Kei runga i ēhea taputapu?** Waea pūkoro me papapātuhi Android, iPhone me iPad, Android TV me Google TV, ngā pouaka whakaata LG me webOS me ngā pouaka whakaata Samsung me Tizen (he taupānga pouaka whakaata motuhake, i ngā hanganga whakamātau), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 me te 3S, Windows (he whakaaturanga), me Linux me macOS hei ngā whakaaturanga tōmua.
- **Me whai tūmau, pūkete rānei ahau?** Kāo. Ka mahi tonu a Immuch360 anake ki ngā pāpāho o tō taputapu. He kōwhiringa te tūmau Immich, te NAS, te tūmau pāpāho, te tūmau Plex rānei, e tāpirihia e koe ina hiahiatia e koe.
- **Me pēhea au e takiuru ai i tētahi pouaka whakaata, taputapu mahunga, rorohiko rānei?** Mā tō waea pūkoro: matawaitia te waehere QR e whakaaturia ana e tōna whārangi takiuru, patohia rānei tōna waehere, whakaūngia, ā, ka tuwhera ki tō pātaka Immich i roto i ētahi hēkona, kāore he mea hei patopato mā te pou whakahau.

## Ka taea e koe te aha

Ka kōrero ia whakamahinga i raro nei mō te mea ka riro i a koe, kātahi ka whakaatu me pēhea te mahi i roto i te iti noa o nga taonga. Ko te aratohu katoa, me ōna kōwhiringa, karere, me ōna here katoa, kei te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Mātakitaki i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata

Ko Immuch360 i te tuatahi he whare whakaahua, he kaiwhakatangi mō ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata māori. Ko te rārangi wā, ngā kohinga, ngā tino pai, ngā maharatanga me te rapu e mahi ana me tētahi tūmau Immich; ko ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata ake o te taputapu, me ngā kōpaki o tētahi rorohiko, ka kitea me te kore tūmau. Ka whakatangihia ngā kiriata i roto i tētahi kaiwhakatangi māori me te oro, te rapu wāhi, tētahi tohu pūhiki me tētahi kōwhiringa ara oro, i roto i ngā kaiwhakatangi 360°, Mokowā, rorohiko hoki, ā, mai i te hanganga 29, i roto i te kaiwhakatangi papatahi o te taupānga Android, e whakaatu ana hoki i ngā kupu hauraro. I runga i te pouaka whakaata ko te pou whakahau e whakahaere ana i te katoa; i runga i te rorohiko ko te kiore me te papapātuhi.

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa Whakaahua: te rārangi wā o tō tūmau, o te taputapu rānei me te kore tūmau.
2. Pāwhiritia tētahi whakaahua, kiriata rānei kia tuwhera; paki whakatehau, paki whakamatau hoki kia haere ki tērā e whai ake, kōpiri, pāwhiri rua rānei kia whakanui.
3. Paki ki runga i runga i tētahi kiriata mō ōna taipitopito: codec, rahi, tere papamata, reiti pūhiki, me te mea ka wetewetehia tēnei e te taputapu, kāore rānei.
4. I runga i ngā waea pūkoro me ngā pouaka whakaata Android, mai i te hanganga 29, he pātene oro me tētahi pātene kupu hauraro kei ngā mana whakahaere o tētahi kiriata he maha ōna reo oro, he kupu hauraro rānei: ka puritia te reo oro ka kōwhiria e koe mō ngā kiriata e whai ake ana, ā, ka noho weto ngā kupu hauraro kia kōwhiria rā anō e koe, hāunga ngā kupu hauraro e kitea tonu ana. Kāore tētahi o ēnei i te kaiwhakatangi Android o te taupānga Immich.

Ētahi atu: [ngā kaimātakitaki me te kaiwhakatangi ataata](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [taipitopito ataata me ngā kaiwetewete](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Hurihia tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi

Hurihia tētahi whakaahua 90° ki te pēhi kotahi, me te kore e whakatuwhera i tētahi ētita: he pātene, he pātene papapātuhi, he pātene tae rānei o te pou whakahau o te pouaka whakaata, i ia pūhara. Ka hurihia e te taupānga Immich tētahi whakaahua i roto i tōna ētita; ka pērā a Immuch360 ki te pātene kotahi, ā, mō āu ake whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka haere mā taua ētita tūmau anō, nā reira ka whakaatu te taupānga tukutuku me ōu taputapu kē atu i aua whakaahua kua hurihia. Ko ētahi atu whakaahua ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake. Kāore rawa ngā whakaahua 360°, 180° me te 3D e hurihia, kia noho tika tonu ai ngā tirohanga rukuhanga.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360 Rotate 90°">
</p>

1. **Waea pūkoro, papapātuhi me te matapihi Meta Quest**: whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, ā, pāwhiritia Hurihia i te rārangi o raro, i mua i Whakatika, kia hurihia ki te ahunga o te karaka; mā te pēhi roa ka huri ki tērā atu taha. Mā te papapātuhi, ko R me Shift+R.
2. **Android TV me Google TV**: ka hurihia e te pātene kōwhai o te pou whakahau te whakaahua ki te ahunga o te karaka, ā, e te pātene kikorangi ki te ahunga kōaro, ahakoa kei hea te arotahi i te kaimātakitaki. I runga i tētahi pou whakahau kāore he pātene tae, pēhia Raro: ka puta te pae i raro i te whakaahua me Hurihia kua tīpakohia (kotahi Matau te tawhiti ina whai pātene kupu te whakaahua), ā, ka hurihia te whakaahua e ia OK.
3. **Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)**: kōwhai me kikorangi pērā i Android TV, R me Shift+R i runga i te papapātuhi, te pātene huri rānei o ngā mana whakahaere o te whakaahua.
4. **Windows, Linux me macOS**: ka huri a R ki te ahunga o te karaka, a Shift+R ki te ahunga kōaro, te pātene huri rānei i te pae o runga, i mua i te mata katoa.
5. **Ngā whakaahua o tētahi tiritiri whatunga, o Plex, o tētahi kāmera rānei**: te pātene huri tuatahi i te pae o runga, R me Shift+R, ngā pātene kōwhai me kikorangi rānei.

Kei hea te hurihanga e tiakina ai: ko tētahi whakaahua o tō ake pūkete i runga i Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka hurihia mā te ētita whakaahua o te tūmau, e pupuri ana i te kōnae taketake, e hanga ana i tētahi whakaahua kua hurihia (ka puta he pae angiangi i a ia e mahi ana; ka whā ngā hurihanga, kotahi rānei te hurihanga whakamuri, ka hoki ki te taketake). Ko ngā whakaahua o te taputapu, o tētahi tiritiri, o tētahi hoa, o tētahi kohinga tiritiri rānei, me ia whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau tawhito ake, ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake, mō taua whakaahua anake, ā, kāore rawa te kōnae e whakarerekētia; ka kī te taupānga i tēnei kotahi te wā mō tētahi whakaahua Immich. I roto i ngā taupānga Android, iOS me te rorohiko, ka kitea tētahi hurihanga kua tiakina i runga i te taputapu i te kaimātakitaki, kāore anō i ngā whakaahua iti, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me ngā maharatanga. Kāore he Hurihia mō ngā kiriata, te kōpaki kua rakaina, me ia whakaahua 360°, 180°, 3D rānei.

### Torotoro i ngā whakaahua whānui, 360°, VR180 me te 3D

Ka tuwhera tētahi whakaahua 360° hei porotaka ka hurihia e koe mā tētahi matihao, mā te nekehia rānei o te waea pūkoro; ka whakatangihia tētahi kiriata 360° i roto i tētahi kaiwhakatangi porotaka me te oro; ka tuhia ngā kōnae VR180 ki runga i tētahi hawhe porotaka; ka whakaatu ngā kōnae 3D taurua-tirohanga i tētahi karu i runga i te mata papatahi, i ngā karu e rua i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga. Ka pupuri ngā whakaahua whānui-whāiti i tō rātou tapahanga, ā, ka tīmata te pikitia i te tirohanga i hopukina e te kāmera. Ka taea tētahi kōnae kāore e tohuhia e te tūmau te tiro hei 360° mā te pāwhiri kotahi.

1. Whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, te kiriata rānei. Ko ngā kōnae 360° ka mau i tētahi paetohu 360° i runga i tō rātou whakaahua iti, ā, kei te ripa Kohinga tētahi rārangi 360° e kohi ana i ēnei, me ngā tātari mā te wā, te pūtake, te momo, me te kāmera.

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa Pātaka, kātahi ko Ngā tiritiri whatunga, kātahi ko te +, me "He tiritiri whatunga (NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho)". ## Ngā āhuatanga matatau

He aha ka taea e te taupānga te pānui, te whakatangi, mō te kaipānui e hiahia ana ki te whānuitanga katoa. Ka whakaaturia ia rārangi i roto i te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md) me te [wharangi noho rite](../docs/immuch360/compatibility.md).

- **Hōputu**: whakaahua me kiriata papatahi (te mea ka wetewetehia e te taputapu: MP4, MOV, MKV me ētahi atu); whakaahua me kiriata 360° equirectangular; whakaahua whānui-whāiti (tapahanga GPano); VR180; 3D taurua-tirohanga, runga me raro, taha ki te taha rānei, i pānuihia mai i te kōnae (te pouaka st3d) i whakaarohia rānei mai i te papamata; whakaahua mokowā a Apple (takirua karu HEIC) me kiriata mokowā (MV-HEVC, kotahi karu); kōnae mata Insta360 .insp me .insv, GoPro .360, DJI .osv; puna 10 piti HLG me PQ kua whakamaherehia ki runga i te porotaka.
- **Whakaahuatanga me ngā kōrero ā-muri**: whakaahuatanga GPano me te mokowā e pānuihia mai i te kōnae, me ngā tono awhe i runga i tētahi tūmau, tiritiri rānei; te tirohanga tuatahi a te kāmera; ngā porotaka o ngā wakarererangi DJI ka mōhiotia mai i tō rātou kāmera, me tētahi kōnae āpiti .xmp kōwhiringa kia tohua anō e Immich; "Tirohia hei 360°" kua maharatia i runga i te taputapu; ka mōhiotia te VR180 mai i te kōnae, i tōna ingoa rānei; te wāhi o tētahi kiriata wakarererangi ka pānuihia mai i tōna rangitaki rere .SRT; kei te ora tonu ngā kōrero ā-muri nā te mea ko te kōnae taketake tonu e whakatangihia, ehara i tētahi kape kua hurihia; he hurihanga hauwhā ka tiakina mā te ētita Immich, ka puritia rānei i runga i te taputapu, kāore rawa i runga i tētahi kōnae rukuhanga.
- **Whakatangihanga māori**: Media3 i runga i Android me te pouaka whakaata, SceneKit me AVFoundation i runga i iOS, te Meta Spatial SDK i roto i te taputapu mahunga, libmpv i runga i Windows; ka tīpakohia te kōnae taketake, te awa whakaputa rānei e ahu mai i te mea ka wetewetehia e te taputapu; he rārangi o ngā kaiwetewete ataata o te taputapu kei roto i ngā Tautuhinga, Matatau; te kōwhiringa ara oro, ngā kupu hauraro i te kaiwhakatangi Android me tētahi tohu pūhiki.
- **Ngā kōnae mata kua tuituihia i runga i te taputapu**: he shader kongakonga Flutter mō ngā whakaahua; he pānga GPU i roto i te kaiwhakatangi mō ngā kiriata ara kotahi; e rua ngā kaiwetewete taputa i te wā kotahi me tētahi compositor GPU mō ngā kiriata kotahi te arotahi mō ia ara, mō ia kōnae rānei (OpenGL ES 3 i runga i Android me te Quest, Metal i runga i iOS, i runga i Windows hoki, mai i te hanganga desktop 5, e rua ngā kaiwhakatangi libmpv e tuituihia ai ō rātou papamata e te taupānga i runga i te matū whakaahua); te whakatikatika ake o te kāmera (ngā tauira Mei, V6, me te equidistant) me te hopunga a te mea ine nekehanga; ka hoki ki te arotahi kotahi, ki te awa whakaputa, ki te kape LRV a te kāmera, ki te kiriata kāore anō kia tuituihia rānei, ina kore e taea e te taputapu te whakahaere i ngā kaiwetewete e rua.
- **Pūtake**: te whare whakaahua o te taputapu; ngā kōpaki o tētahi rorohiko; tētahi wakarererangi, tētahi kāri kāmera rānei mā te USB, mā te kaikōwhiri kōpaki o te pūnaha (Android, whakamātautau); tētahi pūrere whakahaere DJI RC-N3 mā tōna taura mō te ataata ora a te wakarererangi (Android, whakamātautau); tētahi tūmau Immich; SMB 2 me te 3, WebDAV, DLNA/UPnP; Plex mā te HTTPS e tirohia ana ki te tiwhikete ake o te tūmau; kāmera Tapo mā te whatunga ā-rohe (HTTPS me RTSP); tētahi waea pūkoro, rorohiko rānei kua tiritirihia i runga i te Wi-Fi hei tūmau WebDAV; ngā tūmau i kitea mā te Bonjour/mDNS, he hopu whatunga ā-rohe, SSDP, GDM, me te kimi a TP-Link, ā, ka arumia ina rerekē ai tō rātou wāhitau.
- **Pānui i te wā e hiahiatia ana**: ka whakahaeretia e tētahi arawhata ā-rohe ngā kaiwhakatangi mā ngā awhe paiti me tētahi tohu mō ia wā e whakamahia ana; ngā poraka nui, te pānui mua, me te tae atu ki te ono awhe SMB i te wā kotahi; whakaahua iti mai i tētahi papamata o ia kiriata; kāore he mea e kapetia ki te taputapu.
- **Taputa**: ka whakamahia ngā kaiwetewete taputa i ngā wāhi katoa e noho ai, me ō rātou here kua ine (te pae H.264 o te Quest 3, e rua noa ngā arotahi 2048x2048 mō ngā kaiwetewete pūmanawa); i runga i Windows ka tuhia te tirohanga 360° e te matū whakaahua mai i ia papamata kua wetewetehia, ka inea te rahi papamata i runga i ia rorohiko, ka kitea te matū kua whakaurua, te matū motuhake rānei.
- **Ngā āhuatanga o ia pūnaha**: te pūhiki-haurangi me te kaiwhakatangi Mokowā 2.5D i runga i ngā waea pūkoro; te tirohanga rukuhanga, ngā rengarenga, me ō ringaringa i runga i te Quest; te tahora o te pou whakahau, te kōrerorero papapātuhi, me te haki o te pouaka whakaata i runga i Android TV; i runga i te rorohiko, te kiore me te papapātuhi, te mata katoa, tētahi matapihi kotahi, he kōnae me he kōpaki i whakatuwherahia mā te hōtaka, i whakataka rānei ki runga i tōna matapihi, e pānuihia ana pēnei i ngā kōnae o ō kōpaki, ngā HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, me ngā whakaahua mata a te kāmera o ngā kōpaki, ina whai kaiwetewete a Windows mō aua mea, me te whakamarumaru raraunga a Windows mō ngā kupuhipa me ngā tohu.
- **Matatapu nā te hanga**: kāore he pūkete, he kapua, he tātari rānei; ko ngā kupuhuna me ngā tohu kei roto i te rokiroki haumaru o te taputapu (i runga i ngā pouaka whakaata LG me Samsung, kāore nei he rokiroki pērā mō te taupānga, kei te rokiroki ake o te taupānga, kāore i whakamunatia); ka whiwhi tētahi mata i takiuru mai i tētahi waea pūkoro i tōna ake wātū, kua whakamunatia i te whatunga kāinga, kāore rawa te kupuhipa (ka whiwhi tētahi pouaka whakaata LG i te kupuhipa mai i tētahi whārangi i te pūtirotiro o te waea pūkoro, kāore i whakamunatia i te whatunga kāinga, ā, ka pupuri i te wātū anake); ko te kāmera o te kaiwhakatangi Mokowā me te kaimatawai waehere ka whakamahia i runga i te taputapu anake; ko ngā tiritiri, Plex, me ngā kāmera ka whakapāhia i tō whatunga anake.

## Whakauru

### Waea pūkoro me papapātuhi Android

1. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aprogsys.immuch360), te APK rānei o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases): `Immuch360-v<version>-arm64-v8a-release.apk` mō tētahi waea pūkoro; ka mahi te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui ki ngā wāhi katoa.
2. Ka whakaurua te taupānga i te taha o te taupānga Immich whaimana. Kāore te APK mai i GitHub e whakahou ake ana i a ia anō: tirotirohia te wharangi Releases; ina kua whakaurua e koe te taupānga mai i tētahi toa, tangohia ngā whakahoutanga mai i tērā toa.

### iPhone me iPad

1. Kei te tatari te arotakenga App Store ki te arotake a Apple. I mua i tōna tukunga, hangaia te taupānga mā Xcode i runga i tētahi Mac, tirohia [Hangaia ake ko koe](../docs/immuch360/build.md).
2. Pāwhiritia "Whakamahia me te kore tūmau", "Tūhono ki tētahi tūmau Immich" rānei kia takiuru mā tō tūmau; kei te whārangi haere mai raua, mai i te hanganga 24.

<a id="android-tv-and-google-tv"></a>
### Android TV me Google TV

1. I mua i te tuku a Google Play i te taupānga i runga i ngā pouaka whakaata, tangohia te `Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk` whānui o te wharangi [Releases](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases).
2. I te pouaka whakaata, whakakāngia ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake me te patuiro USB (i tētahi Google TV: Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Mō, tīpakohia te “Android TV OS build” e whitu ngā wā, kātahi ko Ngā tautuhinga, Pūnaha, Ngā kōwhiringa kaiwhakawhanake; he rerekē ngā ingoa i ia pouaka whakaata).
3. Mai i tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite, me te adb (Android SDK Platform Tools): `adb connect <address of the TV>`, whakaaetia te tono i te pouaka whakaata, kātahi ko `adb install -r Immuch360-v<version>-release.apk`.
4. Ka puta te taupānga i waenga i ngā taupānga o te pouaka whakaata, me tōna haki. Tāutahia te tuku e whai ake ana mā te huarahi ōrite: ka pupuri a `-r` i te takiuru me ngā tautuhinga.

<a id="lg-tvs-webos"></a>
### Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)

1. Me whai koe i tētahi pouaka whakaata LG me webOS 4.0, tērā hōu ake rānei, tētahi rorohiko i te whatunga ōrite me tētahi pūkete kaiwhakawhanake LG koreutu. I te pouaka whakaata, whakaurua te Developer Mode mai i te LG Content Store, takiuru, whakakāngia te Dev Mode Status, ā, tukuna te pouaka whakaata kia tīmata anō; tuhia tōna wāhitau IP me te kīanga huna e whakaaturia ana e te taupānga.
2. I te rorohiko, whakaurua te webOS CLI (Node.js 18, tērā hōu ake rānei): `npm install -g @webos-tools/cli`, kātahi rēhitatia te pouaka whakaata mā `ares-setup-device` (tōna wāhitau IP, tauranga 9922, kaiwhakamahi `prisoner`) me `ares-novacom --device <name> --getkey`, ka tono i te kīanga huna.
3. Tangohia `Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk` mai i te [putanga tōmua webOS](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/webos-v0.2.0), tirohia ki `SHA256SUMS.txt`, kātahi ko `ares-install --device <name> Immuch360-webOS-0.2.0.ipk`. Ka pērā anō te [webOS Dev Manager](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop) a webosbrew, me te kore rārangi tono.
4. Ka roa te wātū Developer Mode ki te 1000 hāora, ā, ka whakahoutia e te pātene EXTEND o te taupānga Developer Mode; ina pau, ka tangohia e te pouaka whakaata ngā taupānga i whakaurua pēnei. Kei [webos/README.md](../webos/README.md#install-on-a-tv-in-developer-mode) ngā taipitopito.

<a id="samsung-tvs-tizen"></a>

I runga i te pouaka whakaata: Apps, App Settings, patohia 12345, whakakāngia te Developer mode, tāurua te wāhitau IP o te rorohiko, kātahi tīmatahia anōtia te pouaka whakaata.

Ka whakaatu a "Mō tēnei pouaka whakaata" i ngā mea e tautokona ana e te pouaka whakaata, hei kupu, hei waehere QR hoki: ko taua kupu, ka tukuna ki [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) me ngā mea kāore e mahi ana, te āwhina nui rawa.

### Windows

1. Tangohia `Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-windows-x64-setup.exe` mai i te [putanga tōmua o te desktop](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) (me `SHA256SUMS.txt` hei tirotiro), kātahi whakatuwheratia. He hōtaka ōrite te ZIP o te tukunga ōrite ki tētahi kōpaki, e taea ai te wetewete ki hea noa, kātahi ka tīmata i `immuch360.exe` mai i reira. I runga i tētahi rorohiko i tū ai ngā hanganga desktop 4 ki te 8 i te tīmatanga me "duplicate column name: group_date", ka whakatuwhera te hanganga 9 i taua pātengi raraunga, ā, ka pupuri i ō raraunga.

I runga i Ubuntu 22.04, tērā hōu ake rānei, me Debian 12, tērā hōu ake rānei: `sudo apt install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-amd64.deb`; i runga i Fedora 40, tērā hōu ake rānei: `sudo dnf install ./Immuch360-Desktop-3.3.0-rc.0-desktop.9-linux-x86_64.rpm`, mai i te [putanga tōmua o te desktop](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-9) ēnei e rua.
