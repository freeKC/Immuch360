# Immuch360

**Āu pāpāho. Ngā mata katoa. Ngā tirohanga katoa.**

He taupānga koreutu, puka tuwhera a Immuch360 hei kimi, hei tirotiro, hei whakatangi, hei torotoro i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata: ngā mea māori, me ngā mea horopaki, 360°, 3D, kaipuku hoki e whakaatuhia papatahi, ruarua, kumea rānei e ētahi atu taupānga. Ka pānuihia e ia i te wāhi e noho nei, i runga i te taputapu tonu, i te NAS, i te rorohiko, i te tūmau Plex, i te tūmau Immich rānei, ā, ka whakatangihia i runga i ngā waea pūkoro, papapātuhi, pouaka whakaata, ngā taputapu mahunga Meta Quest me ngā rorohiko. Kāore he pūkete, he kapua, he tūmau e hiahiatia.

- **He aha tāna mahi?** Ka whakaatuhia āu whakaahua me āu kiriata hei whare whakaahua, ā, ka whakatangihia i te kaimātakitaki e tau ai ki tēnā, ki tēnā kōnae: papatahi, hei porotaka ka titiro haere koe, i roto i te 3D, i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga.
- **He aha ngā pāpāho?** He whakaahua me he kiriata papatahi, he whakaahua whānui, he whakaahua me he kiriata 360°, VR180, 3D taurua-tirohanga, he whakaahua mokowā a Apple, ngā porotaka 360° o ngā wakarererangi DJI, me ngā kōnae mata o ngā kāmera 360° (Insta360, GoPro MAX, DJI Osmo 360), e tuitui nei te taupānga.
- **On which devices?** Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Android TV and Google TV, LG TVs with webOS and Samsung TVs with Tizen (a separate TV app, in test builds), Meta Quest 2, Pro, 3 and 3S, Windows (preview), and Linux and macOS as early previews.
- **Me whai tūmau, pūkete rānei ahau?** Kāo. Ka mahi tonu a Immuch360 anake ki ngā pāpāho o tō taputapu. He kōwhiringa te tūmau Immich, te NAS, te tūmau pāpāho, te tūmau Plex rānei, e tāpirihia e koe ina hiahiatia e koe.
- **Me pēhea au e takiuru ai i tētahi pouaka whakaata, taputapu mahunga, rorohiko rānei?** Mā tō waea pūkoro: matawaitia te waehere QR e whakaaturia ana e tōna whārangi takiuru, patohia rānei tōna waehere, whakaūngia, ā, ka tuwhera ki tō pātaka Immich i roto i ētahi hēkona, kāore he mea hei patopato mā te pou whakahau.

<a id="what-you-can-do"></a>
## Ka taea e koe te aha

Ka kōrero ia whakamahinga i raro nei mō te mea ka riro i a koe, kātahi ka whakaatu me pēhea te mahi i roto i te iti noa o nga taonga. Ko te aratohu katoa, me ōna kōwhiringa, karere, me ōna here katoa, kei te [aratohu kaiwhakamahi](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

<a id="watch-your-photos-and-videos"></a>
### Mātakitaki i āu whakaahua me āu kiriata

Immuch360 is first a gallery and a player for ordinary photos and videos. The timeline, the albums, the favorites, the memories and the search work with an Immich server; the device's own photos and videos, and the folders of a computer, show without any server. Videos play in a native player with sound, seeking, a buffering indicator and a choice of audio tracks, in the 360°, Spatial and computer players and, from build 29, in the flat player of the Android app, which shows subtitles too. On a TV the remote drives everything; on a computer the mouse and the keyboard do.

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa Whakaahua: te rārangi wā o tō tūmau, o te taputapu rānei me te kore tūmau.
2. Pāwhiritia tētahi whakaahua, kiriata rānei kia tuwhera; paki whakatehau, paki whakamatau hoki kia haere ki tērā e whai ake, kōpiri, pāwhiri rua rānei kia whakanui.
3. Paki ki runga i runga i tētahi kiriata mō ōna taipitopito: codec, rahi, tere papamata, reiti pūhiki, me te mea ka wetewetehia tēnei e te taputapu, kāore rānei.
4. On Android phones and TVs, from build 29, a film with several audio languages or with subtitles has an audio button and a subtitles button in its controls: the audio language you pick is kept for the next films, and subtitles stay off until you pick them, except forced ones. The Immich app's Android player has neither.

Ētahi atu: [ngā kaimātakitaki me te kaiwhakatangi ataata](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#watch-your-photos-and-videos), [taipitopito ataata me ngā kaiwetewete](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#video-details-decoders-and-why-a-video-stutters).

<a id="turn-a-photo-with-one-key"></a>
### Hurihia tētahi whakaahua ki te pātene kotahi

Hurihia tētahi whakaahua 90° ki te pēhi kotahi, me te kore e whakatuwhera i tētahi ētita: he pātene, he pātene papapātuhi, he pātene tae rānei o te pou whakahau o te pouaka whakaata, i ia pūhara. Ka hurihia e te taupānga Immich tētahi whakaahua i roto i tōna ētita; ka pērā a Immuch360 ki te pātene kotahi, ā, mō āu ake whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka haere mā taua ētita tūmau anō, nā reira ka whakaatu te taupānga tukutuku me ōu taputapu kē atu i aua whakaahua kua hurihia. Ko ētahi atu whakaahua ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake. Kāore rawa ngā whakaahua 360°, 180° me te 3D e hurihia, kia noho tika tonu ai ngā tirohanga rukuhanga.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/rotate-one-key.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360 Rotate 90°">
</p>

1. **Waea pūkoro, papapātuhi me te matapihi Meta Quest**: whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, ā, pāwhiritia Hurihia i te rārangi o raro, i mua i Whakatika, kia hurihia ki te ahunga o te karaka; mā te pēhi roa ka huri ki tērā atu taha. Mā te papapātuhi, ko R me Shift+R.
2. **Android TV me Google TV**: ka hurihia e te pātene kōwhai o te pou whakahau te whakaahua ki te ahunga o te karaka, ā, e te pātene kikorangi ki te ahunga kōaro, ahakoa kei hea te arotahi i te kaimātakitaki. I runga i tētahi pou whakahau kāore he pātene tae, pēhia Raro: ka puta te pae i raro i te whakaahua me Hurihia kua tīpakohia (kotahi Matau te tawhiti ina whai pātene kupu te whakaahua), ā, ka hurihia te whakaahua e ia OK.
3. **Pouaka whakaata LG (webOS)**: kōwhai me kikorangi pērā i Android TV, R me Shift+R i runga i te papapātuhi, te pātene huri rānei o ngā mana whakahaere o te whakaahua.
4. **Windows, Linux me macOS**: ka huri a R ki te ahunga o te karaka, a Shift+R ki te ahunga kōaro, te pātene huri rānei i te pae o runga, i mua i te mata katoa.
5. **Ngā whakaahua o tētahi tiritiri whatunga, o Plex, o tētahi kāmera rānei**: te pātene huri tuatahi i te pae o runga, R me Shift+R, ngā pātene kōwhai me kikorangi rānei.

Kei hea te hurihanga e tiakina ai: ko tētahi whakaahua o tō ake pūkete i runga i Immich 2.6, tērā hōu ake rānei, ka hurihia mā te ētita whakaahua o te tūmau, e pupuri ana i te kōnae taketake, e hanga ana i tētahi whakaahua kua hurihia (ka puta he pae angiangi i a ia e mahi ana; ka whā ngā hurihanga, kotahi rānei te hurihanga whakamuri, ka hoki ki te taketake). Ko ngā whakaahua o te taputapu, o tētahi tiritiri, o tētahi hoa, o tētahi kohinga tiritiri rānei, me ia whakaahua i runga i tētahi tūmau tawhito ake, ka hurihia i runga i tēnei taputapu anake, mō taua whakaahua anake, ā, kāore rawa te kōnae e whakarerekētia; ka kī te taupānga i tēnei kotahi te wā mō tētahi whakaahua Immich. I roto i ngā taupānga Android, iOS me te rorohiko, ka kitea tētahi hurihanga kua tiakina i runga i te taputapu i te kaimātakitaki, kāore anō i ngā whakaahua iti, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me ngā maharatanga. Kāore he Hurihia mō ngā kiriata, te kōpaki kua rakaina, me ia whakaahua 360°, 180°, 3D rānei.

<a id="explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d"></a>
### Torotoro i ngā whakaahua whānui, 360°, VR180 me te 3D

Ka tuwhera tētahi whakaahua 360° hei porotaka ka hurihia e koe mā tētahi matihao, mā te nekehia rānei o te waea pūkoro; ka whakatangihia tētahi kiriata 360° i roto i tētahi kaiwhakatangi porotaka me te oro; ka tuhia ngā kōnae VR180 ki runga i tētahi hawhe porotaka; ka whakaatu ngā kōnae 3D taurua-tirohanga i tētahi karu i runga i te mata papatahi, i ngā karu e rua i roto i tētahi taputapu mahunga. Ka pupuri ngā whakaahua whānui-whāiti i tō rātou tapahanga, ā, ka tīmata te pikitia i te tirohanga i hopukina e te kāmera. Ka taea tētahi kōnae kāore e tohuhia e te tūmau te tiro hei 360° mā te pāwhiri kotahi.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-sphere.jpg" width="260" alt="He whakaahua 360° o te taha roto i te mātaki pōro: te pātene kati kei runga mauī, ngā pātene 360°, tahora 3D me te pūhurihuri kei runga matau">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-video.png" width="420" alt="He ataata 360° o tētahi rori takutai e purei ana i te pūrei 360° o tētahi waea e puritia whakapae ana: kati me te ingoa kōnae kei runga mauī, 360° me 3D kei runga matau, o mua, peke whakamuri, pūrei, peke whakamua me o muri kei waenganui, te pae wā kei raro">
</p>

1. Whakatuwheratia te whakaahua, te kiriata rānei. Ko ngā kōnae 360° ka mau i tētahi paetohu 360° i runga i tō rātou whakaahua iti, ā, kei te ripa Kohinga tētahi rārangi 360° e kohi ana i ēnei, me ngā tātari mā te wā, te pūtake, te momo, me te kāmera.
2. Pāwhiritia te 360° i te papa o runga. Toia kia titiro haere, kōpiri kia whakanui, pāwhiritia te pātene pūhiki-haurangi kia titiro haere mā te huri i te waea pūkoro.
3. Ka pōkaikaha te pātene 3D i waenga i "Mono (ehara i te 3D)", "3D, runga me raro", me "3D, taha ki te taha"; ka whakawhiti te pātene 360°/180° i waenga i te porotaka katoa me te hawhe porotaka o VR180.
4. Mō tētahi kōnae e whakaatu papatahi ana ahakoa he 360° tāna: ⋮ i te pokapū matau o runga, kātahi "Tirohia hei 360°".

### Whakapā atu ki ngā pāpāho o tētahi NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho rānei

Kei tētahi NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho rānei āu kiriata e noho ai. Ka kitea e Immuch360 ake anō ngā tūmau o tō whatunga, ka tirotirohia, ā, ka whakatangihia ō rātou whakaahua me ngā kiriata i te wāhi tonu e noho ai, i roto i ia kaimātakitaki o te taupānga, me te kore tāruatanga: SMB (Samba, tiritiri Windows), WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology me ētahi atu), me ngā tūmau pāpāho DLNA (Jellyfin, Emby, minidlna, he NAS, he pouaka TV). Ka taea anō e tētahi waea pūkoro tōna ake whare whakaahua te tiritiri i runga i te Wi-Fi mō tētahi taputapu mahunga, mō tētahi kiritaki WebDAV rānei, ā, ka taea anō e tētahi rorohiko Windows.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-add-share.png" width="260" alt="Te whārangi Tāpiri tiritiri: Ingoa, Ingoa, wāhitau rānei o te tūmau, Tauranga (kōwhiringa), Tiritiri, Kōwhiria he tiritiri, Kōpaki tīmatanga (kōwhiringa), Ingoa kaiwhakamahi, Kupuhipa, Whakamātauria te tūhononga, me te hua Kua tūhono, 2 tāurunga kei te kōpaki tīmatanga">

</p>

1. Whakatuwheratia te ripa Pātaka, kātahi ko Ngā tiritiri whatunga, kātahi ko te +, me "He tiritiri whatunga (NAS, rorohiko, tūmau pāpāho)". Mai i te hanganga 24, ka whakatuwherahia tika tonu a Ngā tiritiri whatunga e "Tāpirihia he tiritiri whatunga, he tūmau pāpāho rānei" i te whārangi haere mai, me te kore tūmau.
2. Pāwhiritia tētahi tūmau i raro i "I kitea i te whatunga", whakakīa rānei te puka mā te ringa: momo, ingoa tūmau, wāhitau rānei, tiritiri, ara rānei, kōpaki tīmatanga, ingoa kaiwhakamahi, me te kupuhuna.

The players read the bytes they need through a bridge inside the app, with byte ranges, so seeking works and nothing is stored on the device; an SMB video is read over up to six connections in parallel for smooth playback. From build 29, films recorded as M2TS (Blu-ray, AVCHD camcorders, TV recorders) play in the Android app from SMB and WebDAV shares and from DLNA servers that list them as transport streams, and a DLNA server that lists a size of 0 no longer gives an empty video.

I hangaia ki runga i [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) nā Seeky91, nāna i kawe mai te rārangi wā, ngā kohinga, ngā tāngata, ngā wāhi, te rapu, te whakaatu whakaahua aunoa me te takiuru mā te waea pūkoro; ngā mihi ki a Seeky91. Ka whakaurua i te Developer Mode anake, ā, ka āwhina ia pūrongo i [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues).

<a id="your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server"></a>
### Ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata o tō wakarererangi DJI i tō tūmau Immich

Import the photos and videos of a DJI drone over USB, straight to your Immich server: plug the drone or a card reader into an Android phone, pick the new files and send them, with the videos placed on the map from the drone's flight data and the drone's 360° spheres shown as 360°. The Immich app backs up only what is already in the phone's gallery, and the Immich server cannot read the place of a DJI Neo 2 video yet. This page is experimental, on Android phones and tablets only, and has not been tried with a real drone yet.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/dji-drone-import.png" width="720" alt="Immuch360, a DJI drone and Immich">
</p>

1. Kei Kohinga, Ngā tiritiri whatunga he taera "Wakarererangi DJI": kua whakakāngia a "Wakarererangi DJI (whakamātautau)" hei tautuhinga taunoa i Ngā tautuhinga, Matatau. Whakawetongia i reira hei huna i te taera.
2. Plug the drone (a Neo 2 can stay off) or a card reader into the phone with a USB-C data cable; a DJI remote without a screen (RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3) holds no photos. On the DJI drone page, tap "Choose the drone or the card", choose it in the side menu of the picker, open its DCIM folder or keep the whole drive, then "Use this folder" and Allow.
3. Ka whakarārangitia e te whārangi ngā whakaahua me ngā kiriata i ia rā, kāore he kōnae āpiti .LRF, .SRT, .THM me .m4a. Pāwhiritia "Tīpakohia ngā mea hōu katoa", kātahi ko "Tukuake ki Immich"; ko ngā kōnae i tukuna i mua he tohu kapua tō rātou, ā, ka peke atu. Ka kape kē a "Puritia ki te taputapu" i ngā kōnae ki te whare whakaahua o te waea, ki te kohinga "Immuch360 import".
4. Kia tau ai tētahi kiriata ki te mahere, whakakāngia te Video Subtitles i DJI Fly (aratau kiriata, Settings, Camera) i mua i tō rere: ka tuhi te wakarererangi i tētahi kōnae .SRT i te taha o ia kiriata, ka pānui te taupānga i te wāhi mai i taua kōnae, ā, ka tautuhi i tō tūmau i muri i te tukuake, ina kāore i kitea e Immich.

Ko ngā porotaka 360° o ngā wakarererangi DJI (Mini, Air, Mavic me ētahi atu, ka mōhiotia mai i tō rātou kāmera) ka whakaatuhia hei 360° i Immuch360 ahakoa kāore he tūtohu 360°. Me whai aua tūtohu a Immich: ko te kōwhiringa tukuake "Tohua ngā porotaka wakarererangi hei 360° mō Immich", kua weto taunoa, ka tuku i te whakaahua kāore i whakarerekētia me tētahi kōnae .xmp iti i tōna taha, kia whakaatuhia anō e Immich hei porotaka. Kāore he aratau porotaka, whakaahua whānui rānei o te Neo 2: he papatahi ōna whakaahua me ōna kiriata. Ina hopukina e te waea pūkoro te oro o tētahi topenga Neo 2, kei tētahi kōnae .m4a motuhake taua oro: ka tohua e te whārangi te topenga, ā, ka whiwhi a Immich i taua topenga kāore he oro (tikiake mā DJI Fly kia mau tonu te oro). Ka pērā anō te mahi a tētahi kāri kāmera me tētahi kōpaki DCIM i roto i tētahi kaipānui kāri. Kāore he mea e tukuna ki DJI.

<a id="your-dji-drones-live-video-on-your-phone-experimental"></a>
### Your DJI drone's live video on your phone (experimental)

See what your DJI drone sees on your Android phone, without DJI Fly and without any account: plug a DJI RC-N3 remote controller into the phone with its cable, and Immuch360 shows the drone's live video, H.265 in 1080p, with the battery of the remote and of the drone, the signal, the gimbal and the flight data, read from the cable. The remote keeps full control of the drone. It was tried on the ground with an RC-N3, a DJI Neo 2 and a Samsung Galaxy S24+; other remotes and drones have not been tried. The flight and battery numbers are not compared with DJI Fly yet: do not fly by them.

Ka whakarongo noa iho. Kāore rawa he tono whakahau e tukuna ki te pūrere whakahaere, ki te wakarererangi rānei: ko te paita kotahi e tuhia ana e te whārangi ia hēkona, mai i te 20 hēkona i muri i te whakatuwheratanga o te pūrere whakahaere, ka whakaatu noa mēnā ka tango te pūrere whakahaere i ngā raraunga, ā, kāore he tono whakahau i roto.

1. Ngā tautuhinga, Matatau, kua whakakāngia a "Tātaritanga hononga DJI (whakamātautau)" hei tautuhinga taunoa (kāore rawa i runga i tētahi pouaka whakaata); kei Pātaka, Ngā tiritiri whatunga te taera "Hononga DJI".
2. Turn on the drone and the remote, close DJI Fly, then open the DJI link tile and read the safety notice.
3. Tap "Start the capture", then plug in the remote with its cable. If Android asks which app should open it, choose Immuch360 without "Always", which would take the remote away from DJI Fly; if it asks for a USB mode, choose charging only.
4. The state reads "Accessory open", then "Remote frames arrive", and the video follows within a few seconds. The button on the picture shows it full screen, and the "Flight and battery" section gives the numbers of the remote and the drone.
5. "Export the capture" writes three files to `Download/Immuch360/dji-link`; "Play a capture" plays an export again on the page, without the remote, to see how well a phone or a headset decodes the drone's video.

The drone's photos and videos cannot be reached through the remote, which does not open the drone's storage: copy them from the drone itself over USB or from its card, as in [the section above](#your-dji-drones-photos-and-videos-on-your-immich-server). On a Meta Quest the page is there and plays a capture, but the drone's live video does not reach the headset yet. Keep the exported files to yourself, as the page says, since they hold the serial number of the remote; a report in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) with the states and counters the page shows helps, above all with other DJI remotes.

<a id="privacy"></a>
## Tūmataiti

Nothing goes to the developer: no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising. The app talks only to the servers you choose (your Immich server, your shares, your Plex server, your cameras) and, without a server, to nothing at all. Passwords and tokens stay in the secure storage of the device; LG and Samsung TVs have none for the app, so there they stay in the app's own storage, not encrypted. The camera is used on the device only, by the Spatial 2.5D player and to scan the code of a screen you sign in; its images are never stored and never sent anywhere. A screen signed in from your phone gets a session of its own, sent encrypted on your home network, never your password; an LG TV is signed in by a page in the phone's browser, which sends your email and password to the TV unencrypted on your home network, and the TV keeps only the session. The full policy is in [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), and the details per source in the [user guide](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

The LG webOS app, also packaged for Samsung TVs, is built on [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) by Seeky91, under the MIT licence, kept in its folder; thanks to Seeky91. The Tapo cameras were written from what [pytapo](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/pytapo), [Home Assistant Tapo Control](https://github.com/JurajNyiri/HomeAssistant-Tapo-Control) and [python-kasa](https://github.com/python-kasa/python-kasa) document. The Meta Quest view relies on the Meta Spatial SDK, which is not open source, so it stays in this fork. The SMB shares use libsmb2, under the LGPL 2.1 or later: from build 27 and desktop build 7, every build carries its notice, its licence texts and its source archive, `Immuch360-libsmb2-sources.tar.gz`, which is also attached to each release. The Windows and macOS builds carry libmpv (the library of [mpv](https://mpv.io), with [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)) built by the project, under the LGPL, with its licences and sources, listed on the licence page of the app (Licenses, at the bottom of the profile menu); the details and the trademarks of the platforms named on this page are in [License and trademarks](../docs/immuch360/platform-support.md#license-and-trademarks).

## Mahere ara

Ngā mea kāore anō kia oti, ko te mea tūpono rawa i te tuatahi. Ehara tētahi mea i konei i te kupu taurangi, ā, ka āwhina ngā urupare i te [pūaroturuki take](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) ki te whakatau he aha ka tae tuatahi.

- **The stores**: Google Play (build 28 in review; the TV listing in review), the App Store (version 3.3.1 in review, then version 3.3.2 with build 28), the Meta Horizon Store with build 29, the LG Content Store (the submission is in preparation).
- **Ngā rorohiko**: te whakamātau ā-ringa i ia taumahi i runga i tētahi PC Windows, tae atu ki te takiuru mā tō waea pūkoro; Mokowā 2.5D me te kāmera tukutuku i runga i Windows, kei te whakawhanakehia mō Whiringa-ā-rangi 2026; kātahi ko te kaiwhakaatu kiriata 360° i runga i Linux me macOS, he whakamātau i runga i tētahi Mac pono me tētahi PC Linux pono; kātahi ko ngā whakahōunga aunoa me tētahi taupānga macOS kua hainatia.
- **TVs**: build 29 on a real Android TV, its video controls and the sign in with your phone included; webOS 0.2.0 on a real LG TV, with its shares, Plex and live view, then the LG Content Store version; the Samsung test build on a real Samsung TV; then the 360° videos on the sphere, the sound of the cameras and their recordings (not before December 2026).
- **Phones and tablets**: the Tapo live view and the sign in of big screens on iPhone and iPad with the next iOS release; a time bar in the iOS 360° video player, previous and next in the 360° players of phones, audio track and subtitle menus in the iOS flat player.
- **Pāpāho rukuhanga**: takirua karu ika-whārua .dng, te whakaōrite i ngā kiriata GoPro me DJI mai i ō rātou ake raraunga nekehanga, kiriata mokowā hei 3D i runga i te Quest mehemea ka taea e ōna kaiwetewete, te mea o mua me te mea e whai ake i waenga i ngā whakaahua mokowā i roto i te taputapu mahunga.
- **Pūtake**: te paki i waenga i ngā kōnae o tētahi kōpaki tiritiri, te whakamanatanga Digest mō WebDAV, he waehere QR hei kawe i te tohu Plex mai i te rorohiko, IPv6 mō Plex, hopunga Tapo H.265, ngā ara i te mata kāinga o te pouaka whakaata.
- **Drones**: a test of the import page with a real DJI Neo 2, then the sound recorded by the phone merged into its clip; the drone's live video relayed from the phone to the Meta Quest. Nothing that sends a command to a drone is planned before those tests.
- **Ki runga**: ngā pull request iti ki a Immich mō ngā wāhanga e hiahiatia ana e ngā kaitiaki, ka tīmata ki te mātaki whakaahua 360°.

Ko te rārangi katoa, me ngā tirohanga taputapu e toe ana mō ia āhuatanga, kei [Te tūnga tuku](../docs/immuch360/release-status.md#roadmap).
