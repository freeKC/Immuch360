# Immuch360

**Ayla-k. Yal agdil. Yal tamuɣli.**

Tiwlafin d tividyutin tizeddigin, n 360° d 3D seg yibenk-ik, NAS-ik, aqeddac-ik Immich, tikamirawin-ik d udrun-ik. Ur tesriḍ amiḍan. Immuch360 d asnas ilelli, s uɣbalu yeldin, i yeqqaren tiwlafin-ik d tividyutin-ik anda llant, yerna yesskan yal yiwet deg umeskan i as-iwulmen: d tazeddigt, d takuret i deg tettmuqqleḍ yal tama, s 3D, neɣ yal tama n yiman-ik deg yibenk n uqerru. Yetteddu ɣef tilifunen, tablitin, tiliẓriyin, ibenkan n uqerru Meta Quest d yiselkimen.

**New: your flat photos, in 3D.** Pick a photo on your computer and get a 3D photo, a 3D scene to step into, or a 360° panorama that imagines the rest of the place around you. In a Meta Quest 3 or 3S, one press on "AI relief" and any flat photo stands out. The AI runs on your own computer and headset, and nothing is sent anywhere. Experimental, and not yet tried in a headset by the author (first test on 30 October). [See how](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama).

## I wumi

- **Your photos are flat** and you want them in 3D, as a scene to lean into in a headset, or inside a 360° panorama, without a 3D camera: [turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama](#turn-one-photo-into-3d-a-scene-a-panorama), new, made by AI on your own computer.
- **Tessuffuɣeḍ s 360° neɣ s 3D** yerna tebɣiḍ ad tmuqqleḍ yal tama deg tuṭṭfiwin-ik, mačči ad tent-twaliḍ d tizeddigin, snat tikkal neɣ ttwazzelent: [360°, VR180 d 3D](#explore-panoramas-360-vr180-and-3d).
- **Tiwlafin-ik d yisura-k llan ɣef NAS, aselkim, Plex neɣ Immich** yerna tebɣiḍ-ten ɣef tiliẓri war ma tneɣleḍ kra: [ɣef tiliẓri](#watch-on-the-tv-with-the-remote), [NAS, ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa d Plex](#play-the-files-of-a-nas-a-computer-a-media-server-or-plex).
- **Tesɛiḍ Meta Quest** yerna tebɣiḍ ad tkecmeḍ ɣer daxel n tewlafin-ik d tividyutin-ik: [deg yibenk n uqerru](#step-inside-your-photos-and-videos-with-a-meta-quest).
- **Tesseqdaceḍ Immich** yerna tebɣiḍ asnas-is, s yimeskanen imirsifen d yigdilen imeqqranen i tqqneḍ seg tilifun-ik: [tamkarḍit-ik Immich](#keep-your-immich-library-with-more), [s umyeẓri d Immich](#compared-with-immich).
- **Tettɛassaḍ axxam-ik s tkamirawin Tapo** yerna tebɣiḍ-tent ɣer tama n tewlafin-ik, ula ɣef tiliẓri (urɛad ttwasenqdent s tkamirat n tidet): [tikamirawin n wexxam](#watch-your-home-cameras-on-the-big-screen).
- **Tessafgeḍ adrun DJI** yerna tebɣiḍ tugna-ines srid ɣef tilifun-ik war DJI Fly, akked deg Meta Quest neɣ ɣef Android TV i yemdanen yellan yid-k (armitan): [adrun-ik](#see-what-your-drone-sees-on-your-phone).

There is no Immuch360 account to create, and nothing goes to us: the app talks only to your devices and to the servers you choose, and downloads an AI component only when you ask for it.

## Ayen i tzemreḍ a t-txedmeḍ

Yal izirig yeldi-d amek ara t-txedmeḍ ddaw-a. Adlis ačaran, s yal taɣtest, izen d talast, yella deg [adlis n useqdac](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md).

5. [Sign in a TV, a headset or a computer with your phone](#sign-in-a-tv-a-headset-or-a-computer-with-your-phone): scan its QR code, confirm, watch.
6. [Step inside your photos and videos with a Meta Quest](#step-inside-your-photos-and-videos-with-a-meta-quest): 360° all around you, 3D in true 3D.

11. [Bring a drone's or a camera card's photos and videos to Immich](#bring-a-drones-or-a-camera-cards-photos-and-videos-to-immich): over USB, from an Android phone. *Experimental.*
12. [Open the raw files of 360° cameras](#open-the-raw-files-of-360-cameras): Insta360, GoPro MAX and DJI Osmo 360, stitched by the app.
13. [Turn a photo with one key](#turn-a-photo-with-one-key): a button, a key of the keyboard or a colour key of the remote.
14. [Use your computer as the media player](#use-your-computer-as-the-media-player): your folders, Open with, a file dropped on the window. *Preview.*

### Android TV d Google TV

### Windows

ZIP n yiwen lexraj d asnas-nni s yiman-is s wudem n ukaram, akken a t-id-teldiḍ anida tebɣiḍ, sinna ssenker `immuch360.exe` seg-s. ### Turn one photo into 3D, a scene, a panorama

Most of your photos are flat, and you cannot go back and take them again in 3D. Immuch360 gives them depth. On your computer, one photo becomes a **3D photo**, a **3D scene** you lean into in a headset, or a **360° panorama** that imagines the rest of the place around you. A Quest 3 or 3S does the relief itself, for any flat photo. Everything runs on your own computer and headset: no cloud, no account, your originals never changed. New in build 32 and desktop build 13, experimental and on by default. The Immich app has nothing like it.

**On your computer**, with desktop build 13 on Windows, Linux or macOS:

1. Select one or more photos in the library or in a folder, then "Make 3D (AI)".
2. Pick what you want: "3D photo", "3D scene", "360° panorama", or "3D version of a 360° photo" for your own 360° shots. The box says what AI will estimate or invent ("About 96% of this panorama will be invented by AI"), about how long it takes on this computer, and where the file goes: "Next to the original" or "A folder of my choice". A type this computer cannot do stays greyed, with its reason, such as "Not yet on Mac" or "The graphics card has 6.0 GiB of memory, 360° panoramas need 7.5 GiB".
3. The first time, the box offers the components of that type: Download, once. They run on this computer and work offline afterwards.
4. Convert. The jobs queue on two lanes, the processor and the graphics card, with their steps and the time left; Cancel stops one at any moment, and quitting the app asks first.
5. Open the result. A 3D photo shows as "Left eye", "Side by side", "Wiggle" (the animation at the top of this page) or "Red and cyan" for glasses; a scene has "Preview in 3D", turned with the mouse or the touchpad, and "Export for other 3D apps (.glb)"; a panorama opens in the 360° viewer.

Every new file goes next to its original, never over it, or into a folder of your choice when the original's folder takes no new file; on a network folder, only once you say yes for that folder ("Immuch360 writes new files there, never the originals"). The library links each version to its original with its badge, "3D AI", "3D scene" or "360° AI", in the album "Immuch360 AI versions", which stays out of your Immich backup unless you turn on "Also back up AI versions"; careful, "Select all" in the backup albums picks this album too and turns that switch on. To see a file in the Quest, save it on your NAS, back it up to your Immich server or, on Windows, turn on "Share this computer".

**In a Meta Quest**, with build 32:

- **AI relief**: open any flat photo, from your library or a share, and press "AI relief" in the top bar: the headset estimates the relief itself and shows the photo in 3D, "Relief estimated by AI". The model, 99.5 MB, downloads the first time you press it, and the reliefs are kept in a cache. "Open AI reliefs in 2.5D (experimental)", in Settings, Advanced, "AI and 3D", makes near objects move in front of the background as you move your head. On a Quest 2 or a Quest Pro the button stays greyed, "Not tested on this headset yet", until you turn on "AI relief on Quest 2 and Quest Pro (untested)" there.
- **3D photos and scenes from the computer**: "View in 3D" shows a 3D photo in true 3D. "View the 3D scene" on the original, or "Explore the 3D scene" on the scene, puts the scene in your room: lean in, or glide with the left thumbstick inside the rebuilt area ("Room to move: about 0.5 m each way" for the room at the top of this page); the layers flatten and darken at its edge, a click on a thumbstick resets the view, and B or Y goes back.
- **Panoramas**: a generated panorama opens all around you, facing your photo, with "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and a thin outline of the original photo, which fade after a few seconds; its 3D version is in true 3D.

Not yet tried in a headset by the author: the first headset test is on 30 October 2026. The headset's code for the relief, the scenes and the panoramas passed its tests on a computer, and what you see in your own Quest is welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues).

**On phones, tablets and TVs**, the files a computer made open as they are, and nothing is generated there. A panorama opens in the 360° viewer with the "360° AI" badge, the sentence and the outline on Android phones, tablets and Android TV; on iPhone, iPad, LG and Samsung TVs it opens as a plain 360° photo for now. A scene shows as its photo, with a small 3D SCENE mark in its corner; on Android, its details add "Open it on a Meta Quest to explore it". A 3D photo shows its two eyes side by side.

**What is real and what is not, always said.** It is the point, not the small print. The relief of a 3D photo is estimated from one photo, and what the photo hides is filled in and is not real. A scene is "Reconstructed from one photo, explore within its limits". A panorama keeps your real pixels where they were and invents the rest, about 90% of the sphere for a typical photo, and the box says how much before you start. The app says it where you look, with "Relief estimated by AI", "Generated by AI beyond the photo's edges" and the badges, and the files say it too: in our XMP, and for the scenes and the panoramas in the IPTC digital source type of an image made with AI, so that other apps can tell a generated panorama from a real 360° photo. Your originals are never changed, and nothing leaves your computer or your headset. As the box puts it: "A finished conversion does not guarantee a good result."

**What was checked**, by 29 October 2026, with test builds of this code: 3D photos by the Linux app in a container on a Linux PC, offline too; scenes by the Windows app on a laptop with an RTX 4060 Laptop GPU (ten in 2 min 39 s) and by the engine on the Linux PC; on that laptop, four panoramas in 4 min 16 s to 4 min 44 s each, their 3D versions in about 30 s, with Cancel, a killed app, another program holding the graphics card, and no network connection of the app during the tests. The author has not judged the panoramas in a headset yet. Not tried: a Mac, a Linux PC with a dedicated graphics card, AMD and Intel graphics cards for the panoramas, a phone or a TV opening these files, and anything in a headset.

Limits: no 360° panorama on a Mac yet ("Not yet on Mac"), nor without a suitable graphics card, and the box says why ("No graphics card with Vulkan found", "Integrated graphics share the computer's memory: too slow for panoramas", with "Try on integrated graphics (untested)" to try anyway); inside a panorama, the edge of your photo can show as a sharp step in tone; most scenes say "Field of view guessed: shapes may be off"; a scene needs about 2 GB of free memory ("Not enough memory" otherwise); with AI versions backed up, Immich's duplicate finder pairs each photo with its scene and often suggests keeping the scene, the heavier file: keep the original. Phones and TVs make nothing, and the headset makes reliefs only; a scene comes from one photo, not yet from several photos of the same place.

**The components**: on the computer, Settings, "AI and 3D" lists the components of each feature with their size, Download and Remove, then "Remove all AI components" and "Move the AI components" to another drive; "AI features" off hides every AI button and downloads nothing. Each file is checked by its SHA-256. On Windows they sit in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.aprogsys\Immuch360 Desktop\ai-components`, which the uninstaller deletes (not a folder you moved them to); on Linux in `~/.local/share/immuch360/ai-components` on most systems, and on a Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/com.aprogsys.immuch360.desktop/ai-components`. In the Quest, "AI and 3D" is in Settings, Advanced, with the relief cache.

More: the notes of [build 32](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/v3.3.0-rc.0-32) and [desktop build 13](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/releases/tag/desktop-v3.3.0-rc.0-13), and [the models, their licences, their sources and their sums](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/blob/immuch360/licenses/ai-components/MODEL-LICENSES.md). Yebna ɣef [immich-tv-webos](https://github.com/Seeky91/immich-tv-webos) n Seeky91, i d-yewwin tasnakudt, albumat, imdanen, imukan, anadi, askan n tugniwin d tuqqna s tilifun; tanemmirt i Seeky91. Version 1.0.0 tettwazen ɣer LG Content Store ass n 24 Tubeṛ yerna teddi ɣef tiliẓriyin webOS 25 n Cloud Test Lab n LG; alamma LG yeqbel-itt, tettwasebded deg Developer Mode, yerna yal aneqqis deg [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues) yettɛawan.

1. Sebded-it ɣef TV deg Developer Mode, wali [Sebded](#lg-tvs-webos). Ɣur-s asulay-is n usnas, daymi yettwasebded ɣer tama n Immich TV n Seeky91.
2. Qqen s tilifun-ik (ḍegger tangalt QR), s imayl-ik d wawal-ik uffir, neɣ s tsarut API.
3. Browse with the arrows and OK. In the viewer, Left and Right go to the previous or next photo or video, also while a video plays; the controls hide 5 s after the last key; Info opens the details; the channel keys zoom; the Magic Remote pointer and wheel work. From version 1.0.1, on GitHub, Up zooms a photo in at once, the arrows then move it and Back zooms out, and a Loading pill shows while a photo is still coming.
4. Tawlaft n 360° teldi ɣef tkuret: yal asiti ɣef taqeffalt n tanila yezzi-tt s yiwen usurif, yerna taqeffalt yettwaṭṭfen tettkemmil ttezzi-tt. "Wali am 360°" yettaṭṭaf tawlaft 2:1 ur d-yemmal ara uqeddac; tawlaft VR180 tettban ɣef uzgen n tkuret, tawlaft 3D s yiwet n tiṭ. Tiqeffalen tawraɣt d tanilit ttezzint tawlaft tazeddigt.
5. Mi ara tqqneḍ, "Ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa" deg ufeggag n tama n usnas: rnu afaylu n uẓeṭṭa SMB, WebDAV neɣ DLNA, aqeddac Plex (yeqqnen s tengalt ɣef plex.tv/link) neɣ takamirat Tapo. Tiwlafin-nsen ldint deg yiwen umeskan, tiwlafin n 360° ɣef tkuret.

Version 1.0.0 tettwaheyya i LG Content Store: Tuɣalin teffeɣ seg usnas seg Tiwlafin neɣ seg usebter n ansuf akken i tt-yettraju yal version n webOS, yerna tmedd uqbel asfaylu udhim yeldin; aqeddac ur d-nerri ara deg 15 s yettfukk s yizen i d-yeqqaren acimi akked tqeffalt Ɛreḍ tikkelt nniḍen; tasertit n tbaḍnit teldi deg usnas, s tengalt QR akken ad teɣreḍ [tigezmi-ines ɣef tiliẓriyin LG](../PRIVACY.md#lg-tvs-webos) ɣef tilifun; TV tesseqdac askar-ines n tugna Galerie, akken i t-tessutur LG i yisnasen n twlafin; yerna akemmus wis sin yemmezg i tiliẓriyin Full HD i wumi tettak LG isnasen 1280x720.

Tilisa: SMB yesra webOS 22 (tiliẓriyin n 2022) neɣ win i d-iteddun. Ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa, Plex d tikamirawin ttɛeddin s umeẕlu n usnas ɣef TV, i ten-yeqqaren i usnas, yerna ttwasenqden s yiqeddacen n tidet d tkamirat tasemsalt ɣef uqeddac n build n usenfaṛ, urɛad ɣef TV. Tamuɣli srid n tkamirat d tavidyut kan. Urɛad: tuṭṭfiwin n tkamirawin (mačči uqbel Dujembeṛ 2026) d imesli-nsent, tividyutin n 360° ɣef tkuret (ttwaurarent d tizeddigin, s yizen), asali. Ɣef webOS uqbel 25, reverse proxy zdat Immich ilaq ad yazen iqerra CORS; asnas yesfehm-iten-id mi ara yemlil d wugur. webOS 26 urɛad yettwarmes.

### Qqen TV, ibenk n uqerru neɣ aselkim s tilifun-ik

**Sleḍ. Qqen. Wali.** Tira n tansa n uqeddac, n yimayl d wawal uffir s tlikumand n TV, neɣ deg yibenk n uqerru, tettawi tisdatin. S Immuch360 ɣef tilifun-ik, yeqqnen ɣer uqeddac-ik Immich, TV, Meta Quest neɣ aselkim yeqqen deg kra n tsinin: agdil ameqqran yesskan-d tangalt QR, tilifun yesleḍ-itt, tsentmeḍ, yerna agdil yeldi ɣef tmkarḍit-ik. S usnas n tilifun, awal-ik uffir yeqqim ɣef tilifun. Asnas Immich ur yesɛi ayen yecban aya.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/sign-in-with-your-phone.png" width="720" alt="Tugna, mačči d tuṭṭfa n ugdil: TV tesskan-d tangalt QR ɣef usebter-is n tuqqna, yerna tilifun yettwaṭṭfen zdat-s yesleḍ yiwet n tengalt-nni">
</p>

1. Ɣef ugdil ameqqran (Android TV d Google TV, ṭṭaq n Meta Quest, Windows, Linux neɣ macOS), fren "Qqen ɣer uqeddac Immich": asebter n tuqqna yesskan-d "Qqen s tilifun-ik", tangalt QR d tengalt n 10 n yisekkilen, ɣer tama n tferkit n uqeddac.
2. Ɣef tilifun yeqqnen ɣer uqeddac-ik: Iɣewwaren, "Qqen TV neɣ aselkim", sinna "Sleḍ tangalt". Deg Meta Quest, anda tilifun ur yezmir ara ad yesleḍ agdil-is, fren "Sekcem tangalt deg wadeg-is" yerna aru tangalt i d-yesskan yibenk n uqerru.
3. Tilifun ad k-yesteqsi "Ad yeqqen (agdil)?" yerna ad d-yesken amḍan n sḍis n wuṭṭunen: senqed ma yella agdil yesskan-d yiwen-nni, sinna sit ɣef Qqen. Agdil ad yeldi ɣef wumuɣ-ik azemani.

Amek yeqqim d aɣellsan: tilifun d ugdil ilaq ad ilin ɣef yiwen n uẓeṭṭa n uxxam, yerna tangalt tettdum snat n tesdidin ma yella aṭas, tteddu yiwet n tikkelt kan. Tilifun yessuter seg uqeddac-ik tiɣimit tamaynut i ugdil-nni, s yisem-is ("Immuch360 ɣef TV n usalun"), yerna yazen-itt i ugdil d tawgelhant, ihi awal-ik uffir werǧin ad yeffeɣ seg tilifun. "Qqen TV neɣ aselkim" yesskan-d umuɣ n igdilen i yeqqen tilifun-agi, yal yiwen s Ffeɣ, yerna Immich yesskan-iten-id deg Iɣewwaren n umiḍan, Ibenkan yettusirgen, anda i tzemreḍ daɣen ad ten-tekkseḍ. Tuffɣa n tilifun ad ten-tessuffeɣ ula d nutni.

Tama n tilifun tetteddu tura ɣef Android, yerna ɣef iPhone d iPad seg lexraj i d-iteddun n iOS. War Immuch360 ɣef tilifun, sleḍ tangalt s tkamirat n tilifun: asebter i d-yettak ugdil yeldi deg yiminig, i yimayl-ik d wawal-ik uffir, yettwawgelhen yerna yettwaseqdac yiwet n tikkelt. Tiliẓriyin LG qqnent s tengalt-nsent QR, deg yiminig n tilifun; seg build 28, amsiḍeḍ n usnas n tilifun yessen-itt yerna yeldi asebter-nni. Tiliẓriyin Samsung qqnent s tira i tura.

Ugar: [tuqqna s tilifun-ik](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#sign-in-a-tv-a-headset-or-a-computer-with-your-phone), [ayen i ttazznen igdilen d wayen i ḥerrzen](../docs/immuch360/technical-details.md#sign-in-with-your-phone).

### Ssurar ifuyla n NAS, n uselkim, n uqeddac n yimidyaten neɣ n Plex

Tividyutin-ik llant ɣef NAS, aselkim, aqeddac n yimidyaten neɣ Plex. Immuch360 yettaf iqeddacen n uẓeṭṭa-k s yiman-is, yettinig deg-sen yerna yessurar tiwlafin-nsen d tividyutin-nsen deg wadeg-nsen, deg yal ameskan n usnas, war ma yenɣel kra: SMB (Samba, ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa Windows), WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology d wiyaḍ) d yiqeddacen n yimidyaten DLNA (Jellyfin, Emby, minidlna, NAS, tabewwaṭ n TV). Tilifun yezmer daɣen ad yebḍu agraw-is ɣef Wi-Fi i yibenk n uqerru neɣ i yal amsaɣ WebDAV, akken daɣen aselkim Windows.

<p align="center">
  <img src="../.github/readme/b19-add-share.png" width="260" alt="Asebter Rnu afaylu yettwabḍan: Isem, Isem neɣ tansa n uqeddac, Tawwurt (d afrayan), Afaylu yettwabḍan, Fren afaylu yettwabḍan, Akaram n tazwara (d afrayan), Isem n useqdac, Awal uffir, Sekyed tuqqna, d ugmuḍ Yeqqen, 2 n yiferdisen deg ukaram n tazwara">

1. Ldi iccer Tamkarḍit, sinna Ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa, sinna + akked "Afaylu n uẓeṭṭa (NAS, aselkim, aqeddac n yimidyaten)". Seg build 24, "Rnu afaylu n uẓeṭṭa neɣ aqeddac n yimidyaten" ɣef usebter n ansuf yeldi-d srid Ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa, war aqeddac.
2. Sit ɣef uqeddac ddaw "Yettwaf deg uẓeṭṭa", neɣ ačaṛ isefka s ufus: anaw, isem neɣ tansa n uqeddac, afaylu n uẓeṭṭa neɣ abrid, akaram n ubeddu, isem n useqdac akked wawal uffir.
3. Sit ɣef "Sekyed tuqqna", sinna Sekles. Sit ɣef ufaylu n uẓeṭṭa i wakken a t-tiniged: ikaramen uqbel, sinna tiwlafin d tividyutin, s tacreṭ n 360° ɣef ifuyla ițwassnen d 360°.
4. I uqeddac Plex: deg Ifuyla n uẓeṭṭa, + d "A Plex Media Server", sinna sit ɣef uqeddac-ik ddaw "Found on the network", senṭeḍ ajiṭun yiwet n tikkelt ("How to get it" ɣef usebter yeqqar-d anda ara t-tafeḍ), sinna "Test the connection" d Save.

Imeskanen qqaren bytes i ten-ilaqen s allal n tuqqna i gensan deg usnas, s izilal n bytes, akken asikel deg wakud ad ixdem u ulac acemma ara yețwaḥrez ɣef ibenk; tividyut SMB teqqar s ɣer sețța n tuqqniwin ɣer yiwet n tikkelt i wakken ad tedduḍ war ɛeṭṭel. Seg build 29, isura yettwaseklsen s M2TS (Blu-ray, tikamirawin AVCHD, imseklas n TV) ttwaurarent deg usnas Android seg yifuyla n uẓeṭṭa SMB d WebDAV akked yiqeddacen DLNA i ten-yesskanen am transport stream, yerna aqeddac DLNA i d-yemmalen tiddi n 0 dayen ur d-yettak ara tavidyut tilemt.

**Plex Media Server, war plex.tv**: asnas yeqqen srid ɣer uqeddac-ik, s ujiṭun i tsenṭḍeḍ yiwet n tikkelt, yerna yeqqar srid ifuyla iẓuranen n tmkarḍiwin-ik, daymi 360° yeqqim d 360°, deg wexxam yerna, s usiweḍ n tewwurt-ik, beɛid ɣef wexxam. Ɣef tiliẓri LG, Plex yeqqen s tengalt ɣef plex.tv/link.

### Ḥrez tamkarḍit-ik Immich, s wugar

S uqeddac [Immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich), Immuch360 d asnas n tilifun n Immich, s yiwen uqeddac akked yiwen umiḍan am win n usnas n web: aḥraz, umuɣ azemani, albumat, anadi, imdanen, ismektayen, abeṭṭu, imdukal, kullec yemtawi akked uqeddac-ik. Yețwasebded ɣer tama n usnas arasmi. Nnig-s: tacreṭ d umuɣ n 360°, yal ameskan imirsif, tafrant ger ufaylu agejdan d wafaylu yettwabeddlen n uqeddac i yal tividyut, talqayt tusnakt n tvidyut, akked ifuyla n ufaylu n uẓeṭṭa neɣ n ibenk i d-ttwaznen ɣer uqeddac-ik s ufus.

1. Ɣef usebter n ansuf, sit ɣef "Qqen ɣer uqeddac Immich", sinna qqen s tansa n uqeddac-ik, imayl-ik akked wawal uffir-ik, akken deg usnas Immich; Tuɣalin ad ak-d-terr ɣer usebter n ansuf. Ar build 23 asebter n tuqqna yeldi-d srid. Tamkarḍit, albumat akked yiɣewwaṛen n uḥraz d wid n umiḍan-ik.
2. Deg Iɣewwaṛen, Ameskan n Ayla, Tividyutin, "Aɣbalu n tvidyut" tettxeṣṣes afaylu agejdan ticki ibenk-agi yezmer a t-idikuder, yal tikkelt afaylu agejdan, neɣ yal tikkelt afaylu yettwabeddlen.
3. I wakken a d-tesskeneḍ tawlaft n 360° i yiwen ur-nesɛi ara asnas, beṭṭu-tt s yiwen ussaɣ yebḍan n Immich: asnas n web n Immich yesskan-itt-id d takuret deg iminig-nsen.

Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/freeKC/Immuch360/issues), not to the Immich project.

Ibenk n uqerru yesskan-iten-id deg usfaylu neɣ, s tmeqqrit, deg tmuɣli n wafug zdat-k, yerna TV ɣef ugdil ačaran, s yiwen wazalen d yiwet n tewinest n yiɛewwiqen am tilifun. Ttwalin kan: ulac ayen yetteg yibenk n uqerru neɣ TV ara yawḍen ɣer tlikumand neɣ ɣer udrun. Ilɣa-nsen, am "Tamuɣli kan", ttruḥun seld kra n tsinin yerna ttuɣalen-d s tqeffalt, s usiti neɣ s tqeffalt n ugdil. Tugna n udrun tewweḍ ɣer Quest 3 yiwet n tikkelt, ass n 24 Tubeṛ, s Wi-Fi n wexxam; asfaylu n yibenk n uqerru d tmuɣli n wafug s wazalen d tewinest n yiɛewwiqen ad ttwarmsen i tikkelt tamezwarut s build 30, yerna Quest 2, Pro d 3S urɛad ttwarmsen. Ɣef Android TV TCL, tuqqna d tugna ttwarmsent ass n 25 Tubeṛ, s uḥbas d yini azegzaw deg usiweḍ yezrin; ass n 26 Tubeṛ, s build 30, seld abeddel ɣer usuddem urkid, TV tkemmel tesskan-d tugna deg yiwen n wafug anda adrun yettazen 18 Mbit/s, s 7 n yiḥbasen, win yeɣzifen ugar 1.7 s; amek i d-tban tugna ur yettwaru ara. Seg build 31, alɣu n tɣellist ɣef TV yettḥaz asaḍas n tlikumand (yettwasenqed s tirmiyin n widget, urɛad ɣef TV-nni); deg build 30 tamuɣli i yellan seddaw-s tezmer ad tawi asaḍas, ihi alɣu ur yezmir ad yettwaɣer wala ad yettwaqbel, yerna abrid kan n tuffɣa d Tuɣalin, i yeǧǧan asebter. Afug mi ara tettwaliḍ ibenk n uqerru neɣ TV yesra amɛassay ɣer tama-k i yettwalin adrun (taggayt yeldin n EU); war-it, wali adrun.

1. Ɣef tilifun, ɣef ugdil srid, sit ɣef "Send to a headset or a TV" sinna rmed-it.
2. **Meta Quest**: ldi Immuch360, Tamkarḍit, Drone, sinna fren tilifun; aru tangalt i d-yesskan tilifun, yiwet n tikkelt i tilifun-nni. Tugna teldi deg usfaylu; "Open the flight view" yessimɣur-itt, yerna B neɣ Y yerra-d asfaylu.

Ddaw "Let this TV watch", TV tesskan-d tangalt d tengalt-is QR: ɣef tilifun, deg yiwen ugalis-nni, sit ɣef "Add a TV" sinna sleḍ-itt, neɣ aru-tt. Sinna fren tilifun ɣef TV sinna sit ɣef OK: tugna tettas-d ɣef ugdil ačaran; OK yesskan neɣ yeffer imḍanen, yerna Menu yeldi tifranin.

**Taɣara n usiweḍ**, tamaynut deg build 30, deg Leqqayen ɣef tilifun: deg wafug adrun yettazen azal n 18 Mbit/s, yerna ass n 25 Tubeṛ abrid n Wi-Fi n wexxam seg tilifun ɣer TV s urawtar yewwi asuddem-nni ačaran kan tis kraḍ n wakud n wafug; deg wakud yeqqimen, tugna n TV tḥebbes, tettruẓ yerna tettawi yini azegzaw. "Asuddem ummid" yettazen yal tikkelt tugna n udrun akken i d-tusa, "Asuddem yerkden" yal tikkelt tin n tilifun. Ulac-it deg build-agi: adrun ɣef iPhone d iPad (urɛad yebda), asiweḍ ɣer tiliẓriyin LG d Samsung, d usbeggen n wazalen sennig 1.8 m d 1.5 m/s, sinna isatliten d wemcwaṛ seg wexxam, i yettrajun afug beṛṛa.

### Ldi ifuyla raw n tkamirawin n 360°

Tiwlafin raw .insp d tividyutin .insv n tkamirawin Insta360 (yiwet neɣ snat tewlafin n tmuɣli, deg yiwen ubrid, sin n yiberdan neɣ sin n yifuyla), ifuyla .360 n GoPro MAX d yifuyla .osv n DJI Osmo 360 ttwasdukklen sɣur usnas mi ara ttwaurarent, s userked yettwarun deg ufaylu, war ma tesbeddeḍ kra anda yebɣu yili.

1. Sers ifuyla raw anda ten-yeqqar usnas: afaylu n uẓeṭṭa SMB neɣ WebDAV, aqeddac-ik Immich (yeqbel kan .insp d .insv), neɣ ikaramen n uselkim. Agraw n tilifun Android ur d-yesskan ara ifuyla raw, daymi afaylu raw yettwanɣel ɣer tilifun ur d-yettban ara deg ugraw-is ula deg usnas.
2. Ḥrez sin n yifuyla n tuṭṭfa i yesɛan sin n yifuyla akken: asnas yettnadi wayeḍ ɣer tama n umezwaru.
3. Ldi afaylu sinna sit ɣef 360°. Tawlaft tesskan-d "Raw 360° file, stitched by the app" s uɣbalu n userked-is; tavidyut tettwasdukkel mi ara tettwaurar.

Linux d macOS ssurarent tividyutin raw d tizeddigin, yerna ifuyla .dng s snat n tewlafin n tmuɣli n uḥuddu ttbanen d izeddigen i tura.

Asnas Immich yettezzi tawlaft deg umaẓrag-is; Immuch360 yexdem-it s yiwet n tqeffalt, yerna i tiwlafin-ik ɣef uqeddac Immich 2.6 neɣ win i d-iteddun, ad iɛeddi s umaẓrag-nni n uqeddac, akken asnas web d yibenkan-ik nniḍen ad ten-sken ttwazzint. Ayen nniḍen n twlafin ttezzint ɣef ibenk-agi kan. Tiwlafin n 360°, 180° d 3D ur ttezzint ara, akken timuɣliwin timirsifin ad qqiment d tusridin.

1. **Tilifunen, tablitin d ṭṭaq n Meta Quest**: ldi tawlaft sinna sit ɣef Zzi deg yizirig n wadda, uqbel Ẓreg, akken ad tezzi ɣer tama n tsegnatin n temrilt; asiti aɣezfan yettezzi-tt ɣer tama nniḍen. S klavye, R d Shift+R.
2. **Android TV d Google TV**: taqeffalt tawraɣt n tlikumand tettezzi tawlaft ɣer tama n tsegnatin n temrilt, taqeffalt tanilit mgal-asent, anda yella uskan deg umeskan. Ɣef tlikumand war tiqeffalen s yini, sit ɣef Adda: afeggag ddaw twlaft ad d-iban s Zzi yettwafernen (yiwen Ayeffus ma yella tawlaft tesɛa taqeffalt n uḍris), yerna yal OK yettezzi tawlaft.

4. **Windows, Linux d macOS**: R yettezzi ɣer tama n tsegnatin n temrilt, Shift+R mgal-asent, neɣ taqeffalt n tuzzya deg ufeggag n usawen, uqbel agdil ameqqran.
5. **Tiwlafin n ufaylu n uẓeṭṭa, Plex neɣ takamirat**: taqeffalt n tuzzya d tamezwarut deg ufeggag n usawen, R d Shift+R, neɣ tiqeffalen tawraɣt d tanilit.

Anda tettwaḥraz tuzzya: tawlaft n umiḍan-ik ɣef Immich 2.6 neɣ win i d-iteddun tettezzi s umaẓrag n twlafin n uqeddac, i yeḥerzen afaylu agejdan yerna ixeddem tugna yettwazzin (afeggag arqaq yettban mi ara yexdem; ukkuẓ n tuzzyiwin, neɣ yiwet n tuzzya ɣer deffir, ad d-rrent agejdan). Tiwlafin n ibenk, n ufaylu n uẓeṭṭa, n umendad neɣ n walbum yettwabḍan, akked yal tawlaft ɣef uqeddac aqbur ttezzint ɣef ibenk-agi kan, i twlaft-nni, yerna afaylu ur yettbeddil ara; asnas ad t-yini tikkelt kan i twlaft n Immich. Deg yisnasen Android, iOS d uselkim, tuzzya yettwaḥerzen ɣef ibenk tettban deg umeskan, maca mačči yakan deg tugniwin timectuḥin, askan n tugniwin d yimektayen. Tividyutin, akaram yettwasekkṛen d yal tawlaft n 360°, 180° neɣ 3D ur sɛint ara Zzi.

## Tabaḍnit

Ulac ayen yettawḍen ɣer uneflay: ulac amiḍan, ulac tasleḍt n useqdec, ulac ameẕlu n uneqqis n uɣelluy, ulac adellel. Asnas yettmeslay kan d yiqeddacen i tferneḍ (aqeddac-ik Immich, ifuyla-ik n uẓeṭṭa, aqeddac-ik Plex, tikamirawin-ik) yerna, war aqeddac, ulac d wi. Awalen uffiren d yijiṭunen qqimen deg usekles aɣellsan n yibenk; tiliẓriyin LG d Samsung ur sɛint ara aya i usnas, daymi dinna qqimen deg usekles n usnas s yiman-is, ur ttwawgelhen ara. Takamirat tettwaseqdac kan ɣef yibenk, sɣur umeskan Spatial 2.5D d usleḍ n tengalt n ugdil i tqqneḍ; tugniwin-is werǧin ttwaskelsent neɣ ttwaznent anda yebɣu yili. Agdil yeqqnen seg tilifun-ik yettawi tiɣimit-is, yettwaznen yettwawgelhen deg uẓeṭṭa n wexxam-ik, werǧin awal-ik uffir; tiliẓri LG teqqen s usebter deg yiminig n tilifun, i yettazen imayl-ik d wawal-ik uffir ɣer TV war awgelhen deg uẓeṭṭa n wexxam-ik, yerna TV tettḥaz kan tiɣimit. Tuṭṭfiwin d yiɣmisen n tremsit n yisebtar n udrun qqimen ɣef yibenk ala ma tebḍiḍ-ten: sɛan tavidyut n udrun yerna zemren ad sɛun ideg-is, yerna tuṭṭfa imḍanen n umazrar n tlikumand d usafag. Tugna d wazalen n udrun ttruḥun seg tilifun kan ɣer yibenkan n uqerru d tiliẓriyin i tqqneḍ, deg uẓeṭṭa-k adigan, ur ttwawgelhen ara, am tilifun yettwabḍan ɣef Wi-Fi. Ulac ayen yettwaznen i DJI, ula i TP-Link. Tasertit tačarant tella deg [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md), yerna talqayt i yal aɣbalu deg [udlis n useqdac](../docs/immuch360/user-guide.md#privacy).

**The AI features** work on your devices only. The photo, its relief, its scene and its panorama are computed on your computer or in your headset and sent nowhere. The app connects only to download a component you asked for, from the releases of this project on GitHub or, failing that, from the model's official page when it has one, checks each file by its SHA-256, and then works offline. ONNX Runtime, which runs the models on a computer, sends telemetry to Microsoft in its official builds; Immuch360 turns it off. The new files go next to your originals, never over them, and reach your Immich server only if you turn on "Also back up AI versions" ("Select all" in the backup albums turns it on too).
